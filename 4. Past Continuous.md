Here are 10 Past Continuous examples, each with a different subject and interrupting action.


### Formula Breakdown

|**Sentence Type**|**Formula**|**Example**|
|---|---|---|
|**Positive (+)**|$\text{Subject} + \text{was / were} + \text{Verb(-ing)}$|_She **was studying** at 8 PM._<br><br>  <br><br>_They **were playing** football._|
|**Negative (-)**|$\text{Subject} + \text{was / were} + \text{not} + \text{Verb(-ing)}$|_She **wasn't studying** at 8 PM._<br><br>  <br><br>_They **weren't playing** football._|
**1.** Q: I _____ dinner when she arrived. a) cook b) cooked c) was cooking d) had cooked **Identify:** Two past actions — cooking (ongoing) + arrived (interruption, using "when"). The ongoing one takes Past Continuous → **was cooking**

**2.** Q: They _____ football when it started to rain. a) play b) played c) were playing d) had played **Identify:** "when" signals an interruption. Subject "They" = plural → **were** + verb-ing → **were playing**

**3.** Q: She _____ a book while her mother was cooking. a) read b) reads c) was reading d) had read **Identify:** "while" = two actions happening at the same time in the past → both take Past Continuous → **was reading**

**4.** Q: We _____ when the teacher walked in. a) talk b) talked c) were talking d) had talked **Identify:** "when" = a sudden past action interrupts an ongoing one. Subject "We" = plural → **were talking**

**5.** Q: He _____ his bike when he fell. a) ride b) rode c) was riding d) had ridden **Identify:** Ongoing action ("riding") interrupted by a sudden one ("fell"). Subject "He" = singular → **was riding**

**6.** Q: At that time, I _____ in Delhi. a) live b) lived c) was living d) had lived **Identify:** "At that time" = a specific past period, ongoing state → Past Continuous. Subject "I" → **was living**

**7.** Q: The children _____ in the garden when it started to thunder. a) play b) played c) were playing d) had played **Identify:** "when" = interruption of an ongoing action. Subject "The children" = plural → **were playing**

**8.** Q: You _____ too loudly while I was studying. a) talk b) talked c) were talking d) had talked **Identify:** "while" = simultaneous ongoing actions. Subject "You" → **were talking**

**9.** Q: She _____ when the lights suddenly went out. a) sleep b) slept c) was sleeping d) had slept **Identify:** Ongoing action ("sleeping") interrupted by a sudden one ("went out"). Subject "She" = singular → **was sleeping**

**10.** Q: We _____ up the hill when the storm hit. a) climb b) climbed c) were climbing d) had climbed **Identify:** Ongoing action interrupted by a sudden event. Subject "We" = plural → **were climbing**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- **was** → I, he, she, it (singular)
- **were** → you, we, they (plural)
- Signal words: **while** (two actions happening together) and **when** (a sudden action interrupts an ongoing one)

**Key contrast so far:**

- "He **watched** TV last night." (finished action, Simple Past)
- "He **was watching** TV when the phone rang." (ongoing, then interrupted, Past Continuous)
