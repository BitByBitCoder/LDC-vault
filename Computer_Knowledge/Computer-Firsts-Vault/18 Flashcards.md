## High priority

First computer to run a program stored in its own memory?::Manchester Baby (SSEM), 1948 (Ran its first program 21 June 1948 at Manchester. Many exam keys give EDSAC or EDVAC - see Exam Traps note)
First practical stored-program computer (regular useful work)?::EDSAC, 1949 (Built by Maurice Wilkes at Cambridge; first run May 1949)
Who proposed the stored-program concept (1945)?::John von Neumann (Paper: First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC; Eckert and Mauchly shared the ideas)
Full form of EDSAC?::Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator
Full form of EDVAC?::Electronic Discrete Variable Automatic Computer (Designed on the stored-program concept)
Who built EDSAC?::Maurice Wilkes (University of Cambridge)
Did ENIAC store its program in memory?::No - it was programmed by rewiring cables and switches (Stored programs were the big improvement of EDVAC and EDSAC)
Oldest known calculating device?::Abacus (Used in Mesopotamia/Babylonia from about 2400 BC; the Chinese suanpan is a later form)
First mechanical calculator / adding machine?::Pascaline, Blaise Pascal, 1642 (Built when Pascal was about 19, to help his tax-collector father)
Which early machine could add, subtract, multiply and divide?::Stepped Reckoner, Gottfried Leibniz (1673 design) (Leibniz also worked out the modern binary number system)
Father of the Computer?::Charles Babbage (Designed the Difference Engine (1822) and the Analytical Engine (1837))
Which Babbage machine was the first design of a general-purpose computer?::Analytical Engine (1837) (Parts: Store (memory), Mill (CPU), punched-card input, printer output)
Purpose of Babbage's Difference Engine?::Compute and print mathematical (polynomial) tables (Designed 1822)
World's first programmer?::Ada Lovelace (Wrote an algorithm for Bernoulli numbers for the Analytical Engine (1843); daughter of Lord Byron)
Who invented the punched-card tabulating machine used in the 1890 US census?::Herman Hollerith (His company later became part of IBM)
First use of punched cards to control a machine?::Jacquard loom (Joseph Marie Jacquard, 1801) (Inspired Babbage and Hollerith)
First general-purpose electronic digital computer?::ENIAC (completed 1945, unveiled 1946) (Built by J. Presper Eckert and John Mauchly at the University of Pennsylvania)
Full form of ENIAC?::Electronic Numerical Integrator And Computer
ENIAC used which technology?::Vacuum tubes (about 18,000) (Weighed about 30 tons; a first-generation machine)
First electronic digital computer?::Atanasoff-Berry Computer (ABC), 1937-42 (John V. Atanasoff and Clifford Berry, Iowa State; special-purpose, not programmable)
First programmable, fully automatic digital computer?::Zuse Z3 (1941) (Konrad Zuse, Germany; used electromechanical relays)
First programmable electronic digital computer?::Colossus (1943-44) (Tommy Flowers, Bletchley Park; used to break the German Lorenz cipher)
First electromechanical computer?::Harvard Mark I (1944) (Howard Aiken with IBM; official name Automatic Sequence Controlled Calculator (ASCC))
First commercial computer in the USA?::UNIVAC I (1951) (Eckert and Mauchly; delivered to the US Census Bureau; first to use magnetic tape)
Full form of UNIVAC?::Universal Automatic Computer
Technology of first-generation computers?::Vacuum tubes (1940-1956) (Machine language; huge, hot, unreliable. Examples: ENIAC, EDVAC, UNIVAC I)
Technology of second-generation computers?::Transistors (1956-1963) (Assembly and early high-level languages. Examples: IBM 1401, IBM 7090)
Technology of third-generation computers?::Integrated circuits (1964-1971) (Operating systems and time-sharing. Examples: IBM System/360, PDP-8)
Technology of fourth-generation computers?::Microprocessors / VLSI (1971-present) (Personal computers and GUI. Examples: Apple II, IBM PC)
Technology of fifth-generation computers?::Artificial intelligence, ULSI, parallel processing (present and beyond) (Natural language, robotics, expert systems; Japan started its Fifth Generation project in 1982)
Transistor invented by?::John Bardeen, Walter Brattain and William Shockley (Bell Labs, 1947) (Nobel Prize in Physics 1956)
Integrated circuit (IC) invented by?::Jack Kilby (Texas Instruments, 1958) and Robert Noyce (Fairchild, 1959) (Kilby won the 2000 Nobel Prize in Physics)
First microprocessor?::Intel 4004 (1971) (4-bit; designed by Ted Hoff, Federico Faggin, Stanley Mazor and Masatoshi Shima)
First hard disk drive?::IBM 350 disk storage in the RAMAC 305 (1956) (About 5 MB on fifty 24-inch platters)
Inventor of the computer mouse?::Douglas Engelbart (1964) (Wooden prototype; patent 1970; public demo 1968)
Inventor of the floppy disk?::IBM team (1971), 8-inch (Led by David Noble; Shugart's team made 5.25-inch (1976); Sony 3.5-inch (1981))
Inventor of USB?::Ajay Bhatt (Intel); USB 1.0 in 1996 (Indian-American engineer)
Who developed the Compact Disc?::Sony and Philips (standard 1980, first product 1982) (James Russell is credited with the underlying optical-storage idea)
First personal computer (widely cited)?::Kenbak-1 (1971) (John Blankenbaker; about 130 built. See Exam Traps note)
First commercially successful personal computer?::Altair 8800 (1975) (MITS, Ed Roberts; Intel 8080; Microsoft was founded to write its BASIC)
IBM PC (model 5150) launched?::12 August 1981 (Intel 8088, MS-DOS; made 'PC' a standard)
Apple I (1976) was built by?::Steve Wozniak (with Steve Jobs) (Apple II followed in 1977)
First computer with a GUI and mouse?::Xerox Alto (1973) (Xerox PARC; Xerox Star (1981) was the first commercial GUI system)
First mass-market computer with a GUI and mouse?::Apple Macintosh (24 January 1984) (Apple Lisa (1983) came first but sold poorly)
First mobile phone call?::3 April 1973, Martin Cooper (Motorola) using the DynaTAC (Called rival Joel Engel at Bell Labs)
First commercially available handheld mobile phone?::Motorola DynaTAC 8000X (1983)
First smartphone?::IBM Simon (1994) (Apple iPhone launched 2007; first Android phone HTC Dream 2008)
First laptop (most cited)?::Osborne 1 (1981) as first portable; GRiD Compass (1982) as first true clamshell laptop (Epson HX-20 (1981) is also called the first laptop - see Exam Traps note)
First widely used high-level language?::FORTRAN (1957) (John Backus, IBM; FORmula TRANslation, scientific work. Plankalkul (Zuse) was designed earlier but not used)
Machine language belongs to which language generation?::1GL (binary 0/1) (Assembly = 2GL; high-level = 3GL; SQL = 4GL; Prolog/AI = 5GL)
Business-oriented language of 1959?::COBOL (Common Business Oriented Language) (Grace Hopper's FLOW-MATIC was a major influence)
Who developed C?::Dennis Ritchie (Bell Labs, 1972) (Unix was rewritten in C in 1973; B (Ken Thompson) came before)
Who developed C++?::Bjarne Stroustrup (Began 1979 as 'C with Classes'; named C++ in 1983; first release 1985)
Who developed Java?::James Gosling (Sun Microsystems, 1995) (Began as Oak; 'write once, run anywhere')
BASIC: full form and creators?::Beginner's All-purpose Symbolic Instruction Code; John Kemeny and Thomas Kurtz (Dartmouth, 1964)
Who created the first compiler?::Grace Hopper (A-0 System, 1952) (Called 'Queen of Code' and 'Amazing Grace')
Creator of Python?::Guido van Rossum (1991) (Named after Monty Python, not the snake)
First operating system (commonly cited)?::GM-NAA I/O (1956) (General Motors Research and North American Aviation, for the IBM 704)
Who created Unix?::Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie (Bell Labs, 1969)
Who created Linux?::Linus Torvalds (1991) (First release 17 September 1991; kernel only - GNU tools by Richard Stallman)
First spreadsheet program?::VisiCalc (1979) (Dan Bricklin and Bob Frankston, for the Apple II)
Microsoft Windows 1.0 released?::20 November 1985
MS-DOS came with?::IBM PC (1981) (Microsoft bought 86-DOS (QDOS) from Seattle Computer Products - Tim Paterson)
Windows 95 launched?::24 August 1995 (Introduced the Start menu and taskbar)
Father of the Internet?::Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn (They designed TCP/IP (1974))
ARPANET first message sent?::29 October 1969, from UCLA to Stanford Research Institute (Message 'LO' (system crashed before LOGIN); sent by Charley Kline)
Full form of ARPANET?::Advanced Research Projects Agency Network (US Department of Defense)
Date the Internet switched to TCP/IP?::1 January 1983 (Often called the birthday of the modern Internet)
Father of the World Wide Web?::Tim Berners-Lee (CERN) (Proposal 1989; first website live 1991)
First web browser?::WorldWideWeb (1990, later renamed Nexus) (Tim Berners-Lee)
First popular graphical browser?::Mosaic (1993) (Marc Andreessen and Eric Bina, NCSA; Andreessen then co-founded Netscape)
Who sent the first email (1971) and chose the @ sign?::Ray Tomlinson (BBN, on ARPANET)
First web-based email service?::Hotmail (1996) (Sabeer Bhatia and Jack Smith; sold to Microsoft in 1997)
Google founded by?::Larry Page and Sergey Brin (Stanford), 4 September 1998 (First called BackRub)
First computer virus (experimental)?::Creeper (1971) (Bob Thomas, BBN, on ARPANET; Reaper was the first antivirus-like program)
First virus for IBM PCs?::Brain (1986) (Written by Basit and Amjad Farooq Alvi, Lahore, Pakistan)
First virus found in the wild on personal computers (Apple II)?::Elk Cloner (1982) (Rich Skrenta, age 15)
Who coined the term 'computer virus'?::Fred Cohen (1983-84) (His adviser Leonard Adleman suggested the term)
First Internet worm?::Morris Worm (1988) (Robert Tappan Morris; first person convicted under the US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act)
India's first cyber law?::Information Technology Act, 2000 (Passed June 2000; in force 17 October 2000)
Turing Test proposed in?::1950 paper 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence' by Alan Turing
Who coined 'Artificial Intelligence'?::John McCarthy (Dartmouth Conference, 1956) (Called the Father of AI)
First chatbot?::ELIZA (1966) (Joseph Weizenbaum, MIT)
Which computer beat world chess champion Garry Kasparov (1997)?::IBM Deep Blue
First computer in India?::HEC-2M, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata (1956) (Imported from Britain; some sources date its arrival to 1953; P. C. Mahalanobis drove the effort)
First indigenously built computer in India?::TIFRAC (TIFR, Mumbai), commissioned 1960 (ISIJU-1 (ISI and Jadavpur University, 1966) is also cited - see Exam Traps note)
India's first supercomputer?::PARAM 8000 (1991) (C-DAC, Pune; Vijay P. Bhatkar is called the Father of Indian supercomputing)
Public Internet service in India launched?::15 August 1995 by VSNL (Gateway Internet Access Service)
First mobile phone call in India?::31 July 1995, Kolkata (Jyoti Basu (West Bengal CM) called Sukh Ram (Union Telecom Minister))
First cyber law of India?::IT Act, 2000 (See [[10 Viruses and Security]])
Father of Computer?::Charles Babbage
Father of Modern Computer?::Depends on the options: John von Neumann (architecture) or Alan Turing (theory) (See Exam Traps note)
Father of Artificial Intelligence?::John McCarthy
Father of the Internet?::Vint Cerf (with Bob Kahn)
Father of the WWW?::Tim Berners-Lee
First programmer?::Ada Lovelace
Who found the first computer 'bug' and popularised debugging?::Grace Hopper (Harvard Mark II, 9 September 1947) (A moth was taped into the logbook)
Microsoft founded?::4 April 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen (Albuquerque) (First product: Altair BASIC)
Apple founded?::1 April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne
IBM name adopted?::1924 (formed 1911 as CTR) (Thomas J. Watson)
Intel founded?::1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore
Google incorporated?::4 September 1998 (Larry Page and Sergey Brin)

## Medium priority

Full form of SSEM?::Small-Scale Experimental Machine (Nicknamed the Baby)
Who built the Manchester Baby?::Frederic C. Williams, Tom Kilburn and Geoff Tootill (University of Manchester)
Memory used by the Manchester Baby?::Williams-Kilburn tube (First electronic random-access memory (CRT based))
Memory used by EDSAC?::Mercury delay lines (Hence 'Delay Storage' in the name)
Main idea of von Neumann architecture?::Instructions and data share one memory; CPU fetches, decodes, executes (Parts: CPU (ALU + control unit), memory, input/output)
Architecture with separate memories for instructions and data?::Harvard architecture (Named after the Harvard Mark I)
First commercial computer in the USA built on the stored-program idea?::UNIVAC I (1951)
Assembly language was first used on which machine?::EDSAC (1949) (Its 'Initial Orders' were an early assembler)

Who invented the slide rule?::William Oughtred (1622) (Built on Edmund Gunter's logarithmic scale)
First commercially successful mechanical calculator (Arithmometer, 1820)?::Thomas de Colmar
Boolean algebra was introduced by?::George Boole (1854) (Book: The Laws of Thought; basis of digital logic)
Who described the binary number system (published 1703)?::Gottfried Leibniz
Turing machine (1936) was proposed by?::Alan Turing (Theoretical model of computation; the ACM Turing Award is called the Nobel Prize of computing)
Hollerith's company became which firm?::IBM (Tabulating Machine Company (1896), CTR (1911), IBM (1924))
First commercially available general-purpose electronic computer (UK)?::Ferranti Mark 1 (1951) (Delivered to Manchester University; based on the Manchester Mark 1)
First computer used for routine business office work?::LEO I (1951) (Lyons Electronic Office, built for J. Lyons and Co. (tea shops))

First mass-produced computer?::IBM 650 (1954) (About 2,000 units built)
IBM's first commercial scientific computer?::IBM 701 (1952) (Also called the Defense Calculator)
First transistorized computer?::Manchester Transistor Computer (1953) (Some keys give TRADIC (Bell Labs, 1954) or TX-0 (MIT, 1956))

Which computer predicted the 1952 US presidential election result?::UNIVAC I (Correctly called Eisenhower's win on live TV)

First commercially successful minicomputer?::DEC PDP-8 (1965) (Digital Equipment Corporation, Ken Olsen)
Who were the original ENIAC programmers?::Six women: Kay McNulty, Betty Jennings, Betty Snyder, Marlyn Wescoff, Fran Bilas, Ruth Lichterman
Which generation introduced the operating system and multiprogramming?::Third
Which generation introduced the stored-program concept?::First (EDSAC/EDVAC era)
Main memory in each generation?::1st: magnetic drum / delay line; 2nd: magnetic core; 3rd: semiconductor memory; 4th: semiconductor RAM (VLSI)
Second-generation computers used which languages?::Assembly language plus early high-level languages (FORTRAN, COBOL)
Example machines of the 2nd generation?::IBM 1401, IBM 7090/7094, CDC 1604
Example machines of the 3rd generation?::IBM 360/370, PDP-8, PDP-11
Example machines of the 4th generation?::Altair 8800, Apple II, IBM PC, Cray-1
Which generation brought the GUI and mouse to the mass market?::Fourth
Who invented the vacuum-tube diode and triode?::John Ambrose Fleming (diode, 1904); Lee de Forest (triode, 1906)
Moore's Law?::Transistors on a chip double about every two years (Gordon Moore, 1965) (Moore co-founded Intel with Robert Noyce in 1968)
Who invented DRAM?::Robert Dennard (IBM, 1966)
Magnetic-core memory was developed by?::Jay Forrester (MIT, about 1951-53) (Used in second-generation machines)
Inventor of the laser printer?::Gary Starkweather (Xerox, 1969-71) (HP LaserJet (1984) made it mainstream)
Who made the first digital image scanner (1957)?::Russell Kirsch (US National Bureau of Standards) (First scanned image: his infant son; also credited with the pixel)

Ethernet inventor?::Robert Metcalfe (Xerox PARC, 1973) (Founded 3Com)
