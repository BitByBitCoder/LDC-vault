# Internet, Web, Email and Social Media

Back to [[00 Index]] · [[Priority - High]] · [[Priority - Medium]] · [[Priority - Low]]

ARPANET (1969), TCP/IP (1983), WWW (1989-91), browsers, search engines, email and social media.

## Questions (important first)

| Priority | Question | Answer | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 High | Father of the Internet? | **Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn** | They designed TCP/IP (1974) |
| 🔴 High | ARPANET first message sent? | **29 October 1969, from UCLA to Stanford Research Institute** | Message 'LO' (system crashed before LOGIN); sent by Charley Kline |
| 🔴 High | Full form of ARPANET? | **Advanced Research Projects Agency Network** | US Department of Defense |
| 🔴 High | Date the Internet switched to TCP/IP? | **1 January 1983** | Often called the birthday of the modern Internet |
| 🔴 High | Father of the World Wide Web? | **Tim Berners-Lee (CERN)** | Proposal 1989; first website live 1991 |
| 🔴 High | First web browser? | **WorldWideWeb (1990, later renamed Nexus)** | Tim Berners-Lee |
| 🔴 High | First popular graphical browser? | **Mosaic (1993)** | Marc Andreessen and Eric Bina, NCSA; Andreessen then co-founded Netscape |
| 🔴 High | Who sent the first email (1971) and chose the @ sign? | **Ray Tomlinson** | BBN, on ARPANET |
