# Indian Computing Firsts

Back to [[00 Index]] · [[Priority - High]] · [[Priority - Medium]] · [[Priority - Low]]

High-yield for state-level exams: first computer, PARAM, VSNL internet, IT Act, first mobile call.

## Questions (important first)

| Priority | Question | Answer | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 High | First computer in India? | **HEC-2M, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata (1956)** | Imported from Britain; some sources date its arrival to 1953; P. C. Mahalanobis drove the effort |
| 🔴 High | First indigenously built computer in India? | **TIFRAC (TIFR, Mumbai), commissioned 1960** | ISIJU-1 (ISI and Jadavpur University, 1966) is also cited - see [[15 Exam Traps]] |
| 🔴 High | India's first supercomputer? | **PARAM 8000 (1991)** | C-DAC, Pune; Vijay P. Bhatkar is called the Father of Indian supercomputing |
| 🔴 High | Public Internet service in India launched? | **15 August 1995 by VSNL** | Gateway Internet Access Service |
| 🔴 High | First mobile phone call in India? | **31 July 1995, Kolkata** | Jyoti Basu (West Bengal CM) called Sukh Ram (Union Telecom Minister) |
| 🔴 High | First cyber law of India? | **IT Act, 2000** | See [[10 Viruses and Security]] |
| 🟡 Medium | C-DAC founded? | **1988** | Centre for Development of Advanced Computing, Pune |
| 🟡 Medium | ERNET? | **Education and Research Network (1986)** | First academic/research network in India |
