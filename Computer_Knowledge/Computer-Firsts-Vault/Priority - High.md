# Priority - High 🔴 

Asked very often in competitive exams. Learn these first.

Back to [[00 Index]]

**102 facts**


## [[01 Stored-Program Computers]]

- First computer to run a program stored in its own memory? → **Manchester Baby (SSEM), 1948**
- First practical stored-program computer (regular useful work)? → **EDSAC, 1949**
- Who proposed the stored-program concept (1945)? → **John von Neumann**
- Full form of EDSAC? → **Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator**
- Full form of EDVAC? → **Electronic Discrete Variable Automatic Computer**
- Who built EDSAC? → **Maurice Wilkes (University of Cambridge)**
- Did ENIAC store its program in memory? → **No - it was programmed by rewiring cables and switches**

## [[02 Early Calculating Devices]]

- Oldest known calculating device? → **Abacus**
- First mechanical calculator / adding machine? → **Pascaline, Blaise Pascal, 1642**
- Which early machine could add, subtract, multiply and divide? → **Stepped Reckoner, Gottfried Leibniz (1673 design)**
- Father of the Computer? → **Charles Babbage**
- Which Babbage machine was the first design of a general-purpose computer? → **Analytical Engine (1837)**
- Purpose of Babbage's Difference Engine? → **Compute and print mathematical (polynomial) tables**
- World's first programmer? → **Ada Lovelace**
- Who invented the punched-card tabulating machine used in the 1890 US census? → **Herman Hollerith**
- First use of punched cards to control a machine? → **Jacquard loom (Joseph Marie Jacquard, 1801)**

## [[03 Early Electronic Computers]]

- First general-purpose electronic digital computer? → **ENIAC (completed 1945, unveiled 1946)**
- Full form of ENIAC? → **Electronic Numerical Integrator And Computer**
- ENIAC used which technology? → **Vacuum tubes (about 18,000)**
- First electronic digital computer? → **Atanasoff-Berry Computer (ABC), 1937-42**
- First programmable, fully automatic digital computer? → **Zuse Z3 (1941)**
- First programmable electronic digital computer? → **Colossus (1943-44)**
- First electromechanical computer? → **Harvard Mark I (1944)**
- First commercial computer in the USA? → **UNIVAC I (1951)**
- Full form of UNIVAC? → **Universal Automatic Computer**

## [[04 Computer Generations]]

- Technology of first-generation computers? → **Vacuum tubes (1940-1956)**
- Technology of second-generation computers? → **Transistors (1956-1963)**
- Technology of third-generation computers? → **Integrated circuits (1964-1971)**
- Technology of fourth-generation computers? → **Microprocessors / VLSI (1971-present)**
- Technology of fifth-generation computers? → **Artificial intelligence, ULSI, parallel processing (present and beyond)**

## [[05 Hardware Firsts]]

- Transistor invented by? → **John Bardeen, Walter Brattain and William Shockley (Bell Labs, 1947)**
- Integrated circuit (IC) invented by? → **Jack Kilby (Texas Instruments, 1958) and Robert Noyce (Fairchild, 1959)**
- First microprocessor? → **Intel 4004 (1971)**
- First hard disk drive? → **IBM 350 disk storage in the RAMAC 305 (1956)**
- Inventor of the computer mouse? → **Douglas Engelbart (1964)**
- Inventor of the floppy disk? → **IBM team (1971), 8-inch**
- Inventor of USB? → **Ajay Bhatt (Intel); USB 1.0 in 1996**
- Who developed the Compact Disc? → **Sony and Philips (standard 1980, first product 1982)**

## [[06 PCs Laptops and Mobiles]]

- First personal computer (widely cited)? → **Kenbak-1 (1971)**
- First commercially successful personal computer? → **Altair 8800 (1975)**
- IBM PC (model 5150) launched? → **12 August 1981**
- Apple I (1976) was built by? → **Steve Wozniak (with Steve Jobs)**
- First computer with a GUI and mouse? → **Xerox Alto (1973)**
- First mass-market computer with a GUI and mouse? → **Apple Macintosh (24 January 1984)**
- First mobile phone call? → **3 April 1973, Martin Cooper (Motorola) using the DynaTAC**
- First commercially available handheld mobile phone? → **Motorola DynaTAC 8000X (1983)**
- First smartphone? → **IBM Simon (1994)**
- First laptop (most cited)? → **Osborne 1 (1981) as first portable; GRiD Compass (1982) as first true clamshell laptop**

## [[07 Programming Languages]]

- First widely used high-level language? → **FORTRAN (1957)**
- Machine language belongs to which language generation? → **1GL (binary 0/1)**
- Business-oriented language of 1959? → **COBOL (Common Business Oriented Language)**
- Who developed C? → **Dennis Ritchie (Bell Labs, 1972)**
- Who developed C++? → **Bjarne Stroustrup**
- Who developed Java? → **James Gosling (Sun Microsystems, 1995)**
- BASIC: full form and creators? → **Beginner's All-purpose Symbolic Instruction Code; John Kemeny and Thomas Kurtz (Dartmouth, 1964)**
- Who created the first compiler? → **Grace Hopper (A-0 System, 1952)**
- Creator of Python? → **Guido van Rossum (1991)**

## [[08 Operating Systems and Software]]

- First operating system (commonly cited)? → **GM-NAA I/O (1956)**
- Who created Unix? → **Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie (Bell Labs, 1969)**
- Who created Linux? → **Linus Torvalds (1991)**
- First spreadsheet program? → **VisiCalc (1979)**
- Microsoft Windows 1.0 released? → **20 November 1985**
- MS-DOS came with? → **IBM PC (1981)**
- Windows 95 launched? → **24 August 1995**

## [[09 Internet and Web]]

- Father of the Internet? → **Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn**
- ARPANET first message sent? → **29 October 1969, from UCLA to Stanford Research Institute**
- Full form of ARPANET? → **Advanced Research Projects Agency Network**
- Date the Internet switched to TCP/IP? → **1 January 1983**
- Father of the World Wide Web? → **Tim Berners-Lee (CERN)**
- First web browser? → **WorldWideWeb (1990, later renamed Nexus)**
- First popular graphical browser? → **Mosaic (1993)**
- Who sent the first email (1971) and chose the @ sign? → **Ray Tomlinson**
- First web-based email service? → **Hotmail (1996)**
- Google founded by? → **Larry Page and Sergey Brin (Stanford), 4 September 1998**

## [[10 Viruses and Security]]

- First computer virus (experimental)? → **Creeper (1971)**
- First virus for IBM PCs? → **Brain (1986)**
- First virus found in the wild on personal computers (Apple II)? → **Elk Cloner (1982)**
- Who coined the term 'computer virus'? → **Fred Cohen (1983-84)**
- First Internet worm? → **Morris Worm (1988)**
- India's first cyber law? → **Information Technology Act, 2000**

## [[11 AI Robots and Games]]

- Turing Test proposed in? → **1950 paper 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence' by Alan Turing**
- Who coined 'Artificial Intelligence'? → **John McCarthy (Dartmouth Conference, 1956)**
- First chatbot? → **ELIZA (1966)**
- Which computer beat world chess champion Garry Kasparov (1997)? → **IBM Deep Blue**

## [[12 Indian Computing Firsts]]

- First computer in India? → **HEC-2M, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata (1956)**
- First indigenously built computer in India? → **TIFRAC (TIFR, Mumbai), commissioned 1960**
- India's first supercomputer? → **PARAM 8000 (1991)**
- Public Internet service in India launched? → **15 August 1995 by VSNL**
- First mobile phone call in India? → **31 July 1995, Kolkata**
- First cyber law of India? → **IT Act, 2000**

- Father of Computer? → **Charles Babbage**
- Father of Modern Computer? → **Depends on the options: John von Neumann (architecture) or Alan Turing (theory)**
- Father of Artificial Intelligence? → **John McCarthy**
- Father of the Internet? → **Vint Cerf (with Bob Kahn)**
- Father of the WWW? → **Tim Berners-Lee**
- First programmer? → **Ada Lovelace**
- Who found the first computer 'bug' and popularised debugging? → **Grace Hopper (Harvard Mark II, 9 September 1947)**
