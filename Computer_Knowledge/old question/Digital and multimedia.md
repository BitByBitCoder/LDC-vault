# Digital Audio & Multimedia

> **Q83 topic:** "Samples per second in DVD-Audio" falls under **Digital Audio → Sampling rate & Bit depth** (Multimedia / Optical media). The question is loosely worded: the options are in _bits_, so it is really asking **bit depth**. DVD-Audio = **24-bit** → answer **(d)**.

## 1. Key ideas

|Term|Meaning|Unit|
|---|---|---|
|Sampling|Measuring the analog sound wave at regular intervals|–|
|Sampling rate|Number of samples taken per second|Hz / kHz|
|Bit depth|Bits used to store each sample (more bits = better quality)|bits|
|Channels|Mono = 1, Stereo = 2, Surround = 5.1|–|
|Bit rate|Bits of audio data per second|kbps|
|ADC|Analog to Digital Converter (mic → computer)|–|
|DAC|Digital to Analog Converter (computer → speaker)|–|

## 2. Sampling rate and bit depth by medium

|Medium / System|Sampling rate|Bit depth|
|---|---|---|
|Telephone|8 kHz|8-bit|
|Audio CD|44.1 kHz|16-bit|
|DVD-Video|48 / 96 kHz|16 / 20 / 24-bit|
|**DVD-Audio**|**44.1 – 192 kHz**|**24-bit (max; also 16, 20)**|
|Blu-ray audio|up to 192 kHz|24-bit|
|Standard studio audio|48 kHz|24-bit|

## 7. Exam tip

If options are **16 / 18 / 20 / 24 bit** and the question mentions DVD-Audio or high quality → pick **24-bit**. If it mentions Audio CD → **16-bit**, **44.1 kHz**.
