1. **Read** the *Concept core* of one topic note (2 to 3 minutes). Most confusing questions are solved by the table there.

3. **Reveal**, then fix mistakes. Add misses to [[15 Progress Tracker]].
4. **Repeat** next day, then after 3 days, then after a week. Close the topic at 85%.
