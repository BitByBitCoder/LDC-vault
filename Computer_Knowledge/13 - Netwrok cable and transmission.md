# ⭐⭐⭐ Tier 1: Must know

## 1. The four facts that cover almost every cable question

|Fact|Cable|
|---|---|
|Uses **light** signals, not electricity 📌|**optical fibre**|
|**No electrical interference** 📌|**optical fibre**|
|Most **exposed to electrical interference** 📌|**UTP** (unshielded twisted pair)|
|Everything except fibre uses **electrical** signals 📌|twisted pair, coaxial|

## 2. The comparison table (learn this one table)

| Feature      | Twisted pair   | Coaxial      | Optical fibre           |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------ | ----------------------- |
| Signal       | electrical     | electrical   | **light**               |
| Speed        | lowest         | medium       | **highest**             |
| Cost         | **cheapest**   | medium       | **costliest**           |
| Interference | **most**       | less         | **none**                |
| Security     | easy to tap    | medium       | **hardest to tap**      |
| Used for     | LAN, telephone | **cable TV** | backbone, long distance |

## 3. Full forms and connectors

|Item|Answer|
|---|---|
|UTP|**Unshielded** Twisted Pair|
|STP|**Shielded** Twisted Pair|
|Twisted pair connector|**RJ-45**|
|Wires are twisted to|reduce crosstalk and noise|

## 4. Wired or wireless

|Class|Examples|
|---|---|
|Guided (wired)|twisted pair, coaxial, optical fibre|
|Unguided (wireless)|radio, microwave, satellite, infrared|

# ⭐⭐ Tier 2: Good to know

## 5. Wireless media (one line each)

|Medium|One fact|
|---|---|
|Microwave|**line-of-sight**, needs towers and dishes|
|Satellite|covers the **largest area**|
|Infrared|very short range, line of sight (TV remote)|
|Radio waves|go in all directions, pass through walls|
|Bluetooth 📌|**frequency hopping spread spectrum**, 2.4 GHz|

## 6. Devices linked to cables

|Item|Job|
|---|---|
|NIC 📌|board giving the computer its **physical network connection**|
|Modem 📌|lets computers communicate over **telephone lines**|
|DSL 📌|internet over **telephone wires**|
|Access Point (AP) 📌|connects wireless devices to the **wired** network|
|Repeater|boosts a weak signal over a long cable|

## 7. Small extras

|Item|Fact|
|---|---|
|RJ-11|telephone connector|
|BNC|coaxial connector|
|Coaxial vs UTP|coaxial is better shielded|
|Straight-through cable|different devices (PC to switch)|
|Crossover cable|same devices (PC to PC)|

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> 
> - **Fibre = light.** Twisted pair and coaxial = electricity.
> - **UTP** is the medium most exposed to interference.
> - Fibre has **no** interference, not just less.
> - Coaxial = cable TV. Twisted pair = LAN and telephone.
