# Hardware and Device Firsts

Back to [[00 Index]] · [[Priority - High]] · [[Priority - Medium]] · [[Priority - Low]]

Inventions of components and devices: transistor, IC, microprocessor, mouse, disks, USB and more.

## Questions (important first)

| Priority | Question | Answer | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 High | Transistor invented by? | **John Bardeen, Walter Brattain and William Shockley (Bell Labs, 1947)** | Nobel Prize in Physics 1956 |
| 🔴 High | Integrated circuit (IC) invented by? | **Jack Kilby (Texas Instruments, 1958) and Robert Noyce (Fairchild, 1959)** | Kilby won the 2000 Nobel Prize in Physics |
| 🔴 High | First microprocessor? | **Intel 4004 (1971)** | 4-bit; designed by Ted Hoff, Federico Faggin, Stanley Mazor and Masatoshi Shima |
| 🔴 High | First hard disk drive? | **IBM 350 disk storage in the RAMAC 305 (1956)** | About 5 MB on fifty 24-inch platters |
| 🔴 High | Inventor of the computer mouse? | **Douglas Engelbart (1964)** | Wooden prototype; patent 1970; public demo 1968 |
| 🔴 High | Inventor of the floppy disk? | **IBM team (1971), 8-inch** | Led by David Noble; Shugart's team made 5.25-inch (1976); Sony 3.5-inch (1981) |
| 🔴 High | Inventor of USB? | **Ajay Bhatt (Intel); USB 1.0 in 1996** | Indian-American engineer |
| 🔴 High | Who developed the Compact Disc? | **Sony and Philips (standard 1980, first product 1982)** | James Russell is credited with the underlying optical-storage idea |
| 🟡 Medium | Who invented the vacuum-tube diode and triode? | **John Ambrose Fleming (diode, 1904); Lee de Forest (triode, 1906)** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Moore's Law? | **Transistors on a chip double about every two years (Gordon Moore, 1965)** | Moore co-founded Intel with Robert Noyce in 1968 |
| 🟡 Medium | Who invented DRAM? | **Robert Dennard (IBM, 1966)** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Magnetic-core memory was developed by? | **Jay Forrester (MIT, about 1951-53)** | Used in second-generation machines |
| 🟡 Medium | Inventor of the laser printer? | **Gary Starkweather (Xerox, 1969-71)** | HP LaserJet (1984) made it mainstream |
| 🟡 Medium | Who made the first digital image scanner (1957)? | **Russell Kirsch (US National Bureau of Standards)** | First scanned image: his infant son; also credited with the pixel |
| 🟡 Medium | Who invented the QWERTY keyboard layout? | **Christopher Latham Sholes (1868)** | For the Sholes and Glidden typewriter |
| 🟡 Medium | CRT (cathode ray tube) invented by? | **Karl Ferdinand Braun (1897)** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Who made the first digital camera (1975)? | **Steven Sasson (Kodak)** | 0.01 megapixel |
| 🟡 Medium | QR code invented by? | **Masahiro Hara, Denso Wave (Japan, 1994)** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Bluetooth: developer and name origin? | **Ericsson (1994, Jaap Haartsen); named after King Harald Bluetooth** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Wi-Fi: standard and year? | **IEEE 802.11 (1997); the name Wi-Fi from 1999** | Vic Hayes is called the Father of Wi-Fi |
| 🟡 Medium | Ethernet inventor? | **Robert Metcalfe (Xerox PARC, 1973)** | Founded 3Com |
| 🟡 Medium | First flash (USB) drive sold? | **2000 (IBM sold the 8 MB DiskOnKey made by M-Systems)** | Dov Moran, M-Systems |
| 🟢 Low | Barcode patent (1952)? | **Norman Woodland and Bernard Silver** | First product scanned: Wrigley's chewing gum, 1974 |
| 🟢 Low | First LCD? | **George Heilmeier (RCA, 1968)** |  |
| 🟢 Low | Touchscreen inventor (1965)? | **E. A. Johnson (UK)** |  |
| 🟢 Low | First webcam? | **Trojan Room coffee pot camera, Cambridge (1991)** |  |
| 🟢 Low | DVD and Blu-ray introduced? | **DVD 1995-96 (Toshiba, Panasonic, Philips, Sony); Blu-ray 2006 (Sony)** |  |
