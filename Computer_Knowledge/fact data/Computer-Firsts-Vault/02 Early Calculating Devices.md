# Early Calculating Devices and Pioneers (before electronic computers)

Back to [[00 Index]] · [[Priority - High]] · [[Priority - Medium]] · [[Priority - Low]]

Mechanical era: abacus to Babbage to Hollerith. Pascal, Leibniz, Babbage and Ada Lovelace are the favourites.

## Questions (important first)

| Priority | Question | Answer | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 High | Oldest known calculating device? | **Abacus** | Used in Mesopotamia/Babylonia from about 2400 BC; the Chinese suanpan is a later form |
| 🔴 High | First mechanical calculator / adding machine? | **Pascaline, Blaise Pascal, 1642** | Built when Pascal was about 19, to help his tax-collector father |
| 🔴 High | Which early machine could add, subtract, multiply and divide? | **Stepped Reckoner, Gottfried Leibniz (1673 design)** | Leibniz also worked out the modern binary number system |
| 🔴 High | Father of the Computer? | **Charles Babbage** | Designed the Difference Engine (1822) and the Analytical Engine (1837) |
| 🔴 High | Which Babbage machine was the first design of a general-purpose computer? | **Analytical Engine (1837)** | Parts: Store (memory), Mill (CPU), punched-card input, printer output |
| 🔴 High | Purpose of Babbage's Difference Engine? | **Compute and print mathematical (polynomial) tables** | Designed 1822 |
| 🔴 High | World's first programmer? | **Ada Lovelace** | Wrote an algorithm for Bernoulli numbers for the Analytical Engine (1843); daughter of Lord Byron |
| 🔴 High | Who invented the punched-card tabulating machine used in the 1890 US census? | **Herman Hollerith** | His company later became part of IBM |
| 🔴 High | First use of punched cards to control a machine? | **Jacquard loom (Joseph Marie Jacquard, 1801)** | Inspired Babbage and Hollerith |
| 🟡 Medium | Who invented Napier's Bones (1617)? | **John Napier** | Also introduced logarithms (1614) |
| 🟡 Medium | Who invented the slide rule? | **William Oughtred (1622)** | Built on Edmund Gunter's logarithmic scale |
| 🟡 Medium | First commercially successful mechanical calculator (Arithmometer, 1820)? | **Thomas de Colmar** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Boolean algebra was introduced by? | **George Boole (1854)** | Book: The Laws of Thought; basis of digital logic |
| 🟡 Medium | Who described the binary number system (published 1703)? | **Gottfried Leibniz** |  |
| 🟡 Medium | Turing machine (1936) was proposed by? | **Alan Turing** | Theoretical model of computation; the ACM Turing Award is called the Nobel Prize of computing |
| 🟡 Medium | Hollerith's company became which firm? | **IBM** | Tabulating Machine Company (1896), CTR (1911), IBM (1924) |
| 🟢 Low | Oldest known analog computer? | **Antikythera mechanism (Greece, about 2nd-1st century BC)** | Recovered from a shipwreck in 1901 |
| 🟢 Low | First general-purpose analog computer (1931)? | **Differential Analyzer, Vannevar Bush (MIT)** |  |
| 🟢 Low | Who linked Boolean algebra to electrical switching circuits (1937)? | **Claude Shannon** | Also called the Father of Information Theory |
| 🟢 Low | Earliest use of perforated paper to control a loom (1725)? | **Basile Bouchon** |  |
