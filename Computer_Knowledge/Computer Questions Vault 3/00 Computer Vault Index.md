## How to study

1. Read **Part 1** of one topic note (tables).
2. Do **Part 2**: answer in your head, then open the Answer callout.
3. Log weak questions in [[15 Progress Tracker]].
4. Repeat after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days. Close a topic at 85%.
5. Before sleep: [[11 Repeated Questions]] and [[13 Mnemonics]].
