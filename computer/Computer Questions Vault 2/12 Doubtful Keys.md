# ⚠️ Doubtful Keys

The questions below are badly worded, or published keys disagree. The answer given is the most commonly accepted one. **If you have the official key, trust it over this note.**

### Q104 · 🔴 Hard
Generally Dynamic RAM is used as main memory because it:  
a) consumes less power  
b) has higher speed  
c) has lower cell density  
d) needs refreshing circuitry

> [!success]- Answer
> **a) consumes less power** ⚠️
> DRAM = 1 transistor + 1 capacitor per bit: dense, cheap, low power. Refreshing (d) is a drawback, 'lower density' (c) is false, and SRAM is faster (b). Keys differ on this one; (a) is the commonly accepted answer.

### Q254 · 🔴 Hard
The first printer was invented by:  
a) Chester Carlson  
b) David Gestetner  
c) Xerox co.  
d) None of the above

> [!success]- Answer
> **d) None of the above** ⚠️
> Carlson = photocopier (xerography), Gestetner = stencil duplicator, Xerox = company. First printer concept is credited to Charles Babbage, so 'None'.

### Q292 · 🔴 Hard

a) HAN

d) VSN

> [!success]- Answer
> **d) VSN** ⚠️

### Q710 · 🔴 Hard

> Name = what we seek, address = where it is, route = how to get there (RFC 791). All true.
