#computer #sec

## Concept core

- **IT Act 2000:** 13 chapters (original), 90 sections.
- IPR covers copyright, trademark/trade dress, patents, know-how.
- **Phishing** = fake email to steal data. **Phreaking** = hacking phone systems. **Pharming** = fake websites.
- **Stealth virus** hides from antivirus; **polymorphic** changes its own code.
- **Biometrics** = fingerprint, iris, face. **WPA** = Wi-Fi Protected Access.
