**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **has been** (he/she/it) or **have been** (I/you/we/they) + verb-**ing**
- Signal words: _since, for, all day/week, lately, recently, how long_

**Key contrast with plain Present Perfect:**

- "I **have written** three letters." (Present Perfect — focus on the _completed result_, how many)
- "I **have been writing** letters all morning." (Present Perfect Continuous — focus on the _ongoing activity/duration_, not the count)
