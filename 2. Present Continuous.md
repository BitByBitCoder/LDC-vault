Here are 10 Present Continuous examples, each with a different subject and signal word.

**1.** Q: I _____ my homework right now. a) do b) did c) am doing d) does **Identify:** "right now" = happening at this moment → Present Continuous. Subject "I" → **am** + verb-ing → **am doing**

**2.** Q: Listen! Someone _____ at the door. a) knock b) knocks c) is knocking d) knocked **Identify:** "Listen!" = it's happening as we speak → Present Continuous. Subject "Someone" (it) = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is knocking**

**3.** Q: They _____ football at the moment. a) play b) plays c) are playing d) played **Identify:** "at the moment" = happening now → Present Continuous. Subject "They" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are playing**

**4.** Q: She _____ dinner in the kitchen right now. a) cook b) cooks c) is cooking d) cooked **Identify:** "right now" = in progress this instant → Present Continuous. Subject "She" = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is cooking**

**5.** Q: We _____ currently for the LDC exam. a) prepare b) prepares c) are preparing d) prepared **Identify:** "currently" = ongoing at this point in time → Present Continuous. Subject "We" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are preparing**

**6.** Q: Look! The dog _____ across the road. a) run b) runs c) is running d) ran **Identify:** "Look!" = action visible right now → Present Continuous. Subject "The dog" (it) = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is running**

**7.** Q: You _____ too fast — slow down! a) drive b) drives c) are driving d) drove **Identify:** Being observed/commented on in the moment (immediate context, not habit) → Present Continuous. Subject "You" → **are** + verb-ing → **are driving**

**8.** Q: The children _____ in the garden at the moment. a) plays b) play c) are playing d) played **Identify:** "at the moment" = right now → Present Continuous. Subject "The children" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are playing**

**9.** Q: I _____ to you, so please listen. a) talk b) talks c) am talking d) talked **Identify:** Action in progress at the moment of speaking → Present Continuous. Subject "I" → **am** + verb-ing → **am talking**

**10.** Q: He _____ his bag currently; he'll be ready soon. a) pack b) packs c) is packing d) packed **Identify:** "currently" = ongoing right now → Present Continuous. Subject "He" = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is packing**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Signal words: _now, right now, at the moment, currently, Look!, Listen!_
- Structure: **am** (I) / **is** (he, she, it, singular noun) / **are** (you, we, they, plural noun) + verb-**ing**

**Careful distinction from Simple Present:** "He drinks tea every morning" (habit) vs. "He is drinking tea right now" (happening this instant) — same subject, different signal word, different tense.
