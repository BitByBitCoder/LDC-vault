## How to identify
==currently + happening ( right now ,listen,currently,look)==
**1.** Q: I --- my homework right now. a) do b) did c) am doing d) does **Identify:** "right now" = happening at this moment → Present Continuous. Subject "I" → **am** + verb-ing → **am doing**

**2.** Q: Listen! Someone --- at the door. a) knock b) knocks c) is knocking d) knocked **Identify:** "Listen!" = it's happening as we speak → Present Continuous. Subject "Someone" (it) = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is knocking**

**3.** Q: They --- football at the moment. a) play b) plays c) are playing d) played **Identify:** "at the moment" = happening now → Present Continuous. Subject "They" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are playing**

**4.** Q: She --- dinner in the kitchen right now. a) cook b) cooks c) is cooking d) cooked **Identify:** "right now" = in progress this instant → Present Continuous. Subject "She" = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is cooking**

**5.** Q: We --- currently for the LDC exam. a) prepare b) prepares c) are preparing d) prepared **Identify:** "currently" = ongoing at this point in time → Present Continuous. Subject "We" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are preparing**

**6.** Q: Look! The dog --- across the road. a) run b) runs c) is running d) ran **Identify:** "Look!" = action visible right now → Present Continuous. Subject "The dog" (it) = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is running**

**7.** Q: You --- too fast — slow down! a) drive b) drives c) are driving d) drove **Identify:** Being observed/commented on in the moment (immediate context, not habit) → Present Continuous. Subject "You" → **are** + verb-ing → **are driving**

**8.** Q: The children --- in the garden at the moment. a) plays b) play c) are playing d) played **Identify:** "at the moment" = right now → Present Continuous. Subject "The children" = plural → **are** + verb-ing → **are playing**

**9.** Q: I ---to you, so please listen. a) talk b) talks c) am talking d) talked **Identify:** Action in progress at the moment of speaking → Present Continuous. Subject "I" → **am** + verb-ing → **am talking**

**10.** Q: He --- his bag currently; he'll be ready soon. a) pack b) packs c) is packing d) packed **Identify:** "currently" = ongoing right now → Present Continuous. Subject "He" = singular → **is** + verb-ing → **is packing**

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Signal words: _now, right now, at the moment, currently, Look!, Listen!_
- Structure: **am** (I) / **is** (he, she, it, singular noun) / **are** (you, we, they, plural noun) + verb-**ing**

**Careful distinction from Simple Present:** "He drinks tea every morning" (habit) vs. "He is drinking tea right now" (happening this instant) — same subject, different signal word, different tense.

Here's the full list of Present Continuous signal words, grouped by category.

## 1. "Happening right now" words (the core group)

now, right now, at the moment, currently, at present, presently, this minute, at this time

## 2. Attention-grabbers (pointing at something happening as you speak)

Look!, Listen!, Watch!, See? — anything that draws attention to an action unfolding in front of you

> "Look! It **is raining**." / "Listen! Someone **is singing**."

## 3. Temporary situations (true only for a limited period, not permanently)

these days, this week, this month, this year, nowadays, at the moment (also fits here), for the time being

> "She **is staying** with her aunt this week." (temporary, not her permanent home) Contrast: "She **lives** with her aunt." (Simple Present — permanent arrangement)

## 4. Fixed future plans/arrangements (already decided, calendar-confirmed)

This is a special use — Present Continuous can describe a **future** event that's already arranged

> "I **am meeting** him tomorrow." / "We **are traveling** to Shillong next week." (Different from Simple Future "will," which is a fresh decision/prediction, not a pre-arranged plan)

## 5. A changing or developing situation

getting, becoming, increasingly — describing a trend that's in progress

> "The weather **is getting** colder." / "Prices **are rising** these days."

## 6. Repeated actions with annoyance/complaint (always + continuous — an exception worth knowing)

always, constantly, forever — combined with continuous tense (not simple present) to express irritation about a habit

> "He **is always losing** his keys!" (annoyance, not neutral habit) Note: this "always" pairs with continuous, unlike the neutral "always" that pairs with Simple Present ("The sun always rises in the east")

## 7. Direct commentary on an action in progress (someone narrating what's happening as it happens)

> "She **is walking** towards the stage now." / "He **is opening** the envelope."

---

### Quick self-test filter

Ask: _"Is this true only right now / only temporarily / only for this specific plan — or is it a permanent, general fact?"_

- Right now / temporary / already-arranged future plan / changing trend → **Present Continuous**
- Permanent, general, habitual → Simple Present

### The trickiest trap (worth marking specially)

"Always" can signal **either** tense depending on tone:

- "She **always arrives** on time." → neutral habit → Simple Present
- "She **is always arriving** late!" → annoyance/complaint → Present Continuous
