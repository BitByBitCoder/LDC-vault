Here are 10 Simple Present examples, each with a different subject/signal word so you see the pattern from every angle.

first person no ->s
third person -->s

**1.** Q: I -- coffee every morning. a) drink b) drinks c) drank d) drinking **Identify:** "every morning" = habit → Simple Present. Subject "I" → no -s → **drink**

**2.** Q: He --- his teeth twice a day. a) brush b) brushes c) brushed d) brushing **Identify:** "twice a day" = habit → Simple Present. Subject "He" = 3rd person singular → add -es (verb ends in -sh) → **brushes**

**3.** Q: We --- to the gym every evening. a) goes b) go c) went d) going **Identify:** "every evening" = habit → Simple Present. Subject "We" → no -s → **go**

**4.** Q: The train ---at 6 AM sharp. a) leave b) leaves c) left d) leaving **Identify:** "at 6 AM sharp" = a routine/timetable fact → Simple Present. Subject "The train" (it) = 3rd person singular → add -s → **leaves**

**5.** Q: My parents --- in Aizawl. a) live b) lives c) lived d) living **Identify:** No time word, but it's a general fact/current state → Simple Present. Subject "My parents" = plural → no -s → **live**

**6.** Q: Water --- at 100°C. a) boil b) boils c) boiled d) boiling **Identify:** General scientific truth (always true) → Simple Present. Subject "Water" (it) = 3rd person singular → add -s → **boils**

**7.** Q: You --too much sugar. a) eat b) eats c) ate d) eating **Identify:** Habitual criticism/observation (implied "always") → Simple Present. Subject "You" → no -s → **eat**

**8.** Q: She never ---late for work. a) is b) am c) are d) be **Identify:** "never" = habit signal → Simple Present. This uses "to be," and subject "She" → 3rd person singular → **is**

**9.** Q: Birds -- south in winter. a) fly b) flies c) flew d) flying **Identify:** Recurring natural pattern (fact about birds in general) → Simple Present. Subject "Birds" = plural → no -s → **fly**

**10.** Q: My brother usually _____ dinner on Sundays. a) cook b) cooks c) cooked d) cooking **Identify:** "usually" = habit signal → Simple Present. Subject "My brother" (he) = 3rd person singular → add -s → **cooks**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Signal words like _every, always, usually, never_ → Simple Present
- Subject is he/she/it OR a singular noun → verb + s/es
- Subject is I/you/we/they OR a plural noun → base verb, no s

## recurring word
Here's the full list of Simple Present signal words, grouped by category so the pattern sticks better than one long list would.

## 1. Frequency/habit words (the most common signal type)

always, usually, generally, normally, regularly, frequently, often, sometimes, occasionally, rarely, seldom, hardly ever, never, every day/morning/night/week/month/year, once a week, twice a day, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, every now and then, from time to time

## 2. Timetables, schedules, fixed routines

The train leaves at 6, the shop opens at 9, the movie starts at 8 — anything with a fixed, official schedule, even when talking about a future event

> "The bus **arrives** at 5 PM." (it's a timetable fact, not a prediction)

## 3. General facts / permanent truths

No signal word needed at all — just recognize the sentence is stating something permanently true, not a one-time event

> "The sun **rises** in the east." / "Fish **live** in water." / "Ice **melts** at 0°C."

## 4. Scientific/natural facts

Same as above — chemistry, physics, biology facts that are always true

> "Water **boils** at 100°C." / "Metals **expand** when heated."

## 5. Weather and seasonal patterns (your specific ask)

in winter, in summer, during monsoon, every winter, each season — describing what regularly/typically happens in a season, not today's forecast

> "It **snows** heavily in winter here." / "Birds **migrate** south in winter." (Contrast: "It **is snowing** right now" = Present Continuous, describing today only)

## 6. Current permanent states (no time word, but stable/ongoing fact)

Job, residence, relationship, possession — things that are just true right now and for the foreseeable future

> "My parents **live** in Aizawl." / "She **works** at a bank." / "He **has** two brothers."

## 7. Feelings, opinions, senses, mental states (these NEVER take continuous form)

know, believe, think (=opinion), want, like, love, hate, prefer, understand, remember, forget, need, seem, feel (=opinion), belong, own, doubt

> "I **know** the answer." (never "I am knowing")

## 8. Sports commentary / instructions / demonstrations (a special exam trap)

Used for play-by-play narration or step-by-step directions, even though it's happening "now"

> "He **passes** the ball... he **shoots**... and he **scores**!" "First, you **crack** the egg. Then you **whisk** it."

## 9. Quotes, headlines, and narrating a book/movie plot

> "The report **states** that..." / "In Chapter 3, the hero **discovers** the truth."

---

### Quick self-test filter

When you see a blank, ask: _"Is this describing something that keeps happening / is generally true / is a fixed schedule — or something happening in this one moment?"_

- Keeps happening / always true / scheduled → **Simple Present**
- Happening right now, this instant → Present Continuous (different tense)
