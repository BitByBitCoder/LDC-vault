---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **will** + base verb (V1) — same form for every subject (no he/she/it change, unlike present tense)
- Signal words: _tomorrow, next week/month/year, soon, I think/I'm sure/I guess (predictions), I promise/I will (spontaneous decisions), the result clause of an "if" sentence_

**Key trap to watch for:** "Will" is used both for **predictions** ("I think it will rain") and **decisions made on the spot** ("Don't worry, I'll help you") — but NOT for **plans already arranged** (that's usually "going to" or Present Continuous: "I am meeting him tomorrow" — already fixed, not a fresh decision).
