Here are 10 Simple Past examples, each with a different subject and signal word.

**1.** Q: I _____ my homework yesterday. a) do b) did c) done d) doing **Identify:** "yesterday" = specific finished past time → Simple Past. **do** is irregular → past form is **did**

**2.** Q: He _____ to the market last week. a) go b) goes c) went d) gone **Identify:** "last week" = specific past time → Simple Past. **go** is irregular → past form is **went**

**3.** Q: We _____ this house five years ago. a) buy b) buys c) bought d) buying **Identify:** "ago" = specific point in the past → Simple Past. **buy** is irregular → past form is **bought**

**4.** Q: She _____ English in 2015. a) study b) studies c) studied d) studying **Identify:** "in 2015" = specific past year → Simple Past. **study** is regular → add **-ed** (y→i before -ed) → **studied**

**5.** Q: They _____ the movie last night. a) watch b) watches c) watched d) watching **Identify:** "last night" = specific finished past time → Simple Past. **watch** is regular → add **-ed** → **watched**

**6.** Q: When I was a child, I _____ a lot of cartoons. a) watch b) watches c) watched d) am watching **Identify:** "when I was a child" = a period in the finished past → Simple Past. → **watched**

**7.** Q: The train _____ ten minutes ago. a) leave b) leaves c) left d) leaving **Identify:** "ago" = specific past time → Simple Past. **leave** is irregular → past form is **left**

**8.** Q: My father _____ in the army in 1990. a) serve b) serves c) served d) serving **Identify:** "in 1990" = specific past year → Simple Past. **serve** is regular → add **-ed** → **served**

**9.** Q: You _____ me yesterday, didn't you? a) call b) calls c) called d) calling **Identify:** "yesterday" = specific past time → Simple Past. **call** is regular → add **-ed** → **called**

**10.** Q: She _____ her keys somewhere last week. a) lose b) loses c) lost d) losing **Identify:** "last week" = specific past time → Simple Past. **lose** is irregular → past form is **lost**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Signal words: _yesterday, last week/month/year, ... ago, in [past year], when I was a child_
- Regular verbs: add **-ed** (watch → watched, study → studied)
- Irregular verbs: no fixed rule — you have to memorize them (go → went, buy → bought, leave → left, lose → lost)

**Key contrast so far:**

- "She **studies** English" (habit, Simple Present)
- "She **is studying** English right now" (in progress, Present Continuous)
- "She **studied** English in 2015" (finished, specific past time, Simple Past)
