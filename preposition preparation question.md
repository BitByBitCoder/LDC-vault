## **Comprehensive Exam Practice Test**

1. Mumbai is famous _______ its textiles.

  

(a) with      (b) for      (c) in      (d) about

  

2. Dishonesty is detrimental _______ the progress of a nation.

  

(a) for      (b) to      (c) against      (d) in

  

**3. He arrived _______ the station just before the train left.**

  

(a) in      (b) on      (c) at      (d) to

  

**4. She always studies hard _______ the morning.**

  

(a) at      (b) on      (c) in      (d) by

  

5. We live _______ Aizawl, Mizoram.

  

(a) at      (b) on      (c) in      (d) over

  

**6. The committee agreed _______ the new proposal.**

  

(a) with      (b) to      (c) on      (d) at

  

7. He managed to get away _______ cheating in the exam.

  

(a) from      (b) with      (c) off      (d) by

  

**8. He walked _______ the tunnel to reach the other side.**

  

(a) across      (b) through      (c) into      (d) over

  

**9. My opinion is very different _______ yours.**

  

(a) than      (b) from      (c) to      (d) of

  

**10. She always insists _______ paying for the dinner.**

  

(a) to      (b) for      (c) on      (d) with

  

11. The thief managed to get _______ before the police arrived.

  

(a) away      (b) off      (c) out      (d) over

  

**12. He poured hot water _______ the French press to brew his coffee.**

  

(a) in      (b) into      (c) onto      (d) through

  

**13. They went to the market _______ foot.**

  

(a) by      (b) on      (c) with      (d) in

  

**14. The cat crawled _______ the bed to hide.**

  

(a) over      (b) through      (c) under      (d) across

  

**15. He got carried _______ by his emotions during the speech.**

  

(a) away      (b) off      (c) down      (d) through
