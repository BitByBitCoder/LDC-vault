Here are 10 Future Continuous examples, each with a different subject and specific future time.

**1.** Q: This time tomorrow, she -- on a beach. a) will be relaxing b) is relaxing c) was relaxing d) will relax **Identify:** "This time tomorrow" = a specific future point when the action will be ongoing → Future Continuous → **will be relaxing**

**2.** Q: At 8 PM tonight, we -- dinner. a) will be having b) are having c) were having d) will have **Identify:** "At 8 PM tonight" = a specific future time, action in progress at that moment → **will be having**

**3.** Q: This time next week, they -- in Goa. a) will be traveling b) are traveling c) were traveling d) will travel **Identify:** "This time next week" = specific future point → ongoing action at that time → **will be traveling**

**4.** Q: Don't call me at 9 AM tomorrow — I -- in a meeting. a) will be sitting b) am sitting c) was sitting d) will sit **Identify:** "at 9 AM tomorrow" = specific future time → ongoing action at that moment → **will be sitting**

**5.** Q: He -- at his desk when you arrive tomorrow. a) will be working b) is working c) was working d) will work **Identify:** A future point marked by "when you arrive tomorrow" → ongoing action at that time → **will be working**

**6.** Q: This time next month, I -- for my final exams. a) will be preparing b) am preparing c) was preparing d) will prepare **Identify:** "This time next month" = specific future point → ongoing preparation → **will be preparing**

**7.** Q: At this time tomorrow, the plane -- over the ocean. a) will be flying b) is flying c) was flying d) will fly **Identify:** "At this time tomorrow" = specific future point → ongoing action → **will be flying**

**8.** Q: We -- when the movie starts, so text me later. a) will be eating b) are eating c) were eating d) will eat **Identify:** A future point marked by "when the movie starts" → ongoing action at that time → **will be eating**

**9.** Q: She -- her exam at this exact time next Friday. a) will be writing b) is writing c) was writing d) will write **Identify:** "at this exact time next Friday" = specific future point → ongoing action → **will be writing**

**10.** Q: By 6 PM tomorrow, I -- home from work. a) will be driving b) am driving c) was driving d) will drive **Identify:** "By 6 PM tomorrow" here describes an ongoing action expected at that time (not a completed one) → **will be driving**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **will be** + verb-**ing** (same for all subjects)
- Signal words: _at this time tomorrow, at [specific time] tomorrow, this time next week/month, when + future event_

**Key contrast with Simple Future:**

- "I **will finish** the report by 5 PM." (Simple Future — the action will be complete by then)
- "I **will be writing** the report at 5 PM." (Future Continuous — the action will be in progress at that exact moment)
