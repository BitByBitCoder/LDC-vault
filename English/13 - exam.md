## Tense
No need to go advance. In your **all_combine PDF - 169 pages**, tense level is **ONLY Simple + Medium**.

Here is the real breakdown from all papers:

**70% = SIMPLE LEVEL**
> She **goes** to church everyday
> He **drinks** tea every morning
> We all **are** playthings
> Bread and butter **is** his only food
> I **rode** a horse two years ago
> I **did not break** the vase

This is just Simple Present, Simple Past, is/are/was/were.

**25% = MEDIUM LEVEL**
> By the time we reached, everyone **had left**
> When Jack entered, he **had lost** so much weight
> The house **is being painted** / **was being stitched**
> You **will be punished**

This is Past Perfect and Passive - still LDC standard.

**Only 5% = Slightly Hard**
> He looks as if he **were** weary
> Lest she **fail**
> It's high time they **started**
> **Were** he to accept the offer

That is it. No Future Perfect Continuous, no advanced mixed conditionals, nothing.

**Conclusion for you:**
Don't study advance tense. If you master:

1.  Simple Present vs Simple Past
2.  Past Perfect [had + V3]
3.  Present Perfect [has/have + V3]
4.  is/am/are/was/were + being + V3 (passive continuous)

You will cover 95% of the tense questions in your PDF.

You can skip advanced books.

## Active Passive
For **Active-Passive Voice**, level is also **Simple + Medium only** — same as tense and preposition in your PDF. No advance.

Here is what actually comes:

### 60% = SIMPLE LEVEL

> They **are painting** the house → The house **is being painted** by them
> The teacher will teach a new lesson → A new lesson **will be taught** by the teacher
> Call him in → **Let him be called** in
> My dress **was being stitched** by the tailor → The tailor **was stitching** my dress
> You ___ if you disobey → **will be punished**

Just is/am/are + being + V3, will be + V3.

### 35% = MEDIUM LEVEL

> The firemen **rescued** the old woman → The old woman **was rescued**
> The book **had been read** by the time bell rang
> The doctor will have operated → The patient **will have been operated on**
> While I was playing, my mobile was stolen → **Somebody stole** my mobile
> Are the poor not being helped by rich? → **Are the rich not helping** the poor?

Past perfect passive and will have been — still LDC standard.

### Only 5% = Slight Tricky - LDC style

> Sugar sells by the kilogram → Sugar **is sold** by the kilogram
> He is not liked by anyone → **None likes** him
> What are you thinking of? → What **is being thought** of by you?
> It surprised me → I **was surprised at** it
> I remember them taking me to zoo → I remember **being taken** to zoo

These are quasi-passive / gerund passive — this is the max hard they ask.

**So total 3 topics you asked:**

- **Tense = 70% simple, 30% medium**
- **Preposition = 80% simple, 20% medium** 
- **Voice = 60% simple, 35% medium, 5% tricky**

**You DON'T need advance English.**

If you practice only:
`is being / was being / will be / will have been / let + be + V3`

You will cover 95% voice questions from your whole 169-page PDF.

## Direct Indirect Speech
For **Direct-Indirect (Narration)** — same as other 3, **SIMPLE + MEDIUM only**. No advance.

Here is what comes in your full 169 pages:

### 80% = SIMPLE LEVEL

> Rami said, 'Most people have breakfast daily' → **Rami said that** most people have breakfast daily
> He said, "The Earth **appears** round" → He said that the Earth **appears** round (universal truth = no tense change)
> He said to us, "You are all fools" → He **told** us that we **were** all fools
> They said that train was late → They said, "**The train was late**"

Just `said to → told`, `that` adding, simple past change.

### 20% = MEDIUM LEVEL

> The traveller said, "I **shall leave tomorrow**" → He **would leave the next day** (shall→would, tomorrow→next day)
> "Are you same person I met yesterday?" I asked → I asked the stranger **if he was** same person I **had met** the previous day (yes/no → if, past → had met)
> "I have been working for months" → She said she **had been working** for months
> He asked if I liked coffee → "**Do you like coffee?**" he asked
> He said, "Good-Bye friends" → He **bade** his friends goodbye
> Old lady said, "Thief! Thief! Catch him" → Old lady **shouted for the thief to be caught**

This is the max hard level — tomorrow/next day, yesterday/previous day, bade, shouted.

**What they DON'T ask:**
No advanced like:
- Let’s → proposed/suggested
- Complex exclamatory with what a / how
- Mixed imperative + interrogative in one sentence

**If you learn 5 rules, you cover 100% of your PDF:**

1.  Universal truth = no change (Earth appears)
2.  Habitual truth = no change (have breakfast daily)
3.  said to → told, tomorrow→next day, yesterday→previous day
4.  Yes/No question → if/whether
5.  Goodbye → bade, Thief! → shouted

So for all 4 topics you asked:
**Tense = Simple, Preposition = Simple, Voice = Simple-Medium, Narration = Simple-Medium**

You don't need advance books at all.
