**7.** Q: By the time she turns 30, she -- three degrees. a) will earn b) will have earned c) earns d) had earned **Identify:** "By the time she turns 30" = a future event as a deadline → **will have earned**

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **will have** + V3 (past participle) — same for all subjects
- Signal words: _by tomorrow/next week/next year, by the time + future event_
- The key test: an action will be **complete** before a specific point in the future

**Key contrast with Future Continuous:**

- "By 6 PM, I **will be driving** home." (Future Continuous — in progress at that time)
- "By 6 PM, I **will have driven** home." (Future Perfect — completed, already home by then)
