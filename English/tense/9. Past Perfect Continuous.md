Here are 10 Past Perfect Continuous examples, each with a different subject and duration/past event pair.

**1.** Q: He -- for three hours before the rain stopped. a) had been running b) has been running c) was running d) ran **Identify:** Duration ("for three hours") happening BEFORE another past event ("before the rain stopped") → Past Perfect Continuous → **had been running**

**2.** Q: I -- for two hours before she finally called. a) had been waiting b) have been waiting c) was waiting d) waited **Identify:** "for two hours" + "before she called" (a later past event) → the waiting was ongoing before that point → **had been waiting**

**3.** Q: They -- for five years before they got married. a) had been dating b) have been dating c) were dating d) dated **Identify:** "for five years" + "before they got married" (later past event) → ongoing duration before that point → **had been dating**

**4.** Q: She -- since morning before she finally took a break. a) had been working b) has been working c) was working d) worked **Identify:** "since morning" + "before she took a break" (later past event) → **had been working**

**5.** Q: We -- for an hour before the teacher arrived. a) had been studying b) have been studying c) were studying d) studied **Identify:** "for an hour" + "before the teacher arrived" → ongoing duration before that past point → **had been studying**

**6.** Q: The baby -- for a while before it finally fell asleep. a) had been crying b) has been crying c) was crying d) cried **Identify:** "for a while" + "before it fell asleep" (later past event) → **had been crying**

**7.** Q: He -- English for six months before he moved to London. a) had been learning b) has been learning c) was learning d) learned **Identify:** "for six months" + "before he moved" (later past event) → ongoing duration before that point → **had been learning**

**8.** Q: I -- for my keys for ten minutes before I found them in my bag. a) had been looking b) have been looking c) was looking d) looked **Identify:** "for ten minutes" + "before I found them" (later past event) → **had been looking**

**9.** Q: She -- all day before she finally felt tired. a) had been walking b) has been walking c) was walking d) walked **Identify:** "all day" + "before she felt tired" (later past event) → ongoing duration before that point → **had been walking**

**10.** Q: We -- since 2015 before the company shut down. a) had been working b) have been working c) were working d) worked **Identify:** "since 2015" + "before the company shut down" (later past event) → **had been working**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **had** + **been** + verb-**ing** (same for all subjects — no he/she/it exception)
- Signal words: _for, since (in a past context), before + another past event_
- The key test: an ongoing duration that was happening, and THEN another past event occurred

**Key contrast with plain Past Perfect:**

- "She **had finished** the report before he arrived." (Past Perfect — a completed action before another past point)
- "She **had been writing** the report for three hours before he arrived." (Past Perfect Continuous — emphasis on the ongoing duration leading up to that point)
