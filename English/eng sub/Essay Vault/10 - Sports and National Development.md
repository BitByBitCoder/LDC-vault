## Guide

**Introduction angle:** Frame sports as contributing to health, national pride, and economic opportunity.

**3 Points to build paragraphs around:**
1. Individual benefits — physical/mental health, discipline, teamwork
2. National benefits — international recognition, tourism, economic activity
3. Challenges — lack of infrastructure, funding, rural access

**Key Vocabulary:** camaraderie, discipline, meritocracy, holistic growth, infrastructure, grassroots development

**Tip:** Mention a specific scheme or achievement (Khelo India, Olympic medals) to ground the essay in something concrete.

---

## Model Essay (≈350 words)

Sports play a **vital** role not only in promoting individual well-being but also in driving broader national development, contributing to health, unity, and economic opportunity.

**To begin with**, sports offer **significant** benefits at the individual level. Regular participation improves physical and mental health, while team sports in particular foster **discipline**, **camaraderie**, and effective teamwork. **Furthermore**, engagement in sports from a young age contributes to **holistic growth**, shaping not just physical fitness but also character and resilience.

**Moreover**, the benefits extend well beyond the individual to the national level. International sporting achievements bring recognition and pride to the country, **as** demonstrated by India's growing medal tally at recent Olympic Games. **In addition**, major sporting events boost tourism and generate **substantial** economic activity, from infrastructure development to employment in related industries. **Notably**, government initiatives such as Khelo India have aimed to identify and nurture talent from the grassroots level upward.

**However**, **considerable** challenges remain. Many regions, particularly rural areas, continue to lack adequate sporting **infrastructure** and access to quality training facilities. **Moreover**, insufficient funding and support for lesser-known sports limit opportunities for talented athletes outside mainstream disciplines. **Consequently**, India's vast sporting potential remains only partially realized.

**In conclusion**, sports hold **immense** potential to drive both individual well-being and national progress, but this potential can only be fully unlocked through sustained investment in **grassroots development** and infrastructure. **The way forward lies in** ensuring that opportunity in sports is not limited by geography or economic background, allowing talent from every corner of the country to flourish.

### Exam Trap ⚠️
- Mention a specific scheme or achievement (Khelo India, Olympic medals) — grounds the essay in something concrete
- Include the infrastructure/rural-access challenge paragraph — don't write only about benefits
