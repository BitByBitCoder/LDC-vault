## Part 1: Grammar rules

|#|Rule|Difficulty|Incorrect|Correct|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Capital letters|Easy|_on monday, ravi went to aizawl._|_On Monday, Ravi went to Aizawl._|
|2|Full stop, question mark, comma|Easy|_Where is he. He left,_|_Where is he? He left._|
|3|Articles (sound, not spelling)|Easy|_an university, a hour_|_a university, an hour_|
|4|Apostrophe for possession only|Easy|_The book's are on the table. The students book is torn._|_The books are on the table. The student's book is torn._|
|5|No contractions|Easy|_I don't think it's right._|_I do not think it is right._|
|6|Commonly confused words|Medium|_The dog wagged it's tail. Their going to there school._|_The dog wagged its tail. They are going to their school._|
|7|Subject-verb agreement|Medium|_The list of items are long._|_The list of items is long._|
|8|Tense consistency|Medium|_She opened the door and walks in._|_She opened the door and walked in._|
|9|Pronoun agreement|Medium|_Students must bring his book._|_Students must bring their books._|
|10|Complete sentences|Medium|Fragment: _Because he was late._ Run-on: _He was late he missed the bus._|_Because he was late, he missed the bus._|

## Part 2: Essay-specific rules

|#|Rule|Difficulty|Incorrect|Correct|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|13|Introduction: topic, position, preview|Easy|_Today I will write about pollution._|_Pollution is a growing threat. This essay argues that stricter laws are needed and examines three main causes._|
|14|One idea per paragraph|Medium|One paragraph that mixes education, health, and jobs|Three paragraphs, each starting with its own topic sentence|
|15|Linking words between paragraphs|Medium|_Pollution harms health. Laws are weak._|_Pollution harms health. However, the laws meant to control it are weak._|
|16|Conclusion: no new points|Easy|_In conclusion, pollution is bad. Also, another problem is noise, which I did not discuss._|_In conclusion, stricter laws are the most effective way to reduce pollution._|
|17|Formal vocabulary|Easy|_Kids today are totally addicted to phones._|_Children today are highly dependent on phones._|
|18|Time plan|Medium|Writing until the last second with no proofreading|5 minutes planning, most time writing, last 5 minutes proofreading|
