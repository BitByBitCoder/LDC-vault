## Guide

**Introduction angle:** Frame as a social menace affecting individuals, families, and society at large.

**3 Points to build paragraphs around:**
1. Causes — peer pressure, unemployment, lack of awareness, family issues
2. Effects — health decline, crime, broken families
3. Solutions — rehabilitation, awareness campaigns, stricter enforcement

**Key Vocabulary:** menace, rehabilitation, deterrent, marginalized, vulnerable, eradicate, awareness campaigns

**Tip:** Keep tone factual and solution-focused rather than moralizing — examiners favor balanced, constructive essays over dramatic language.

---

## Model Essay (≈350 words)

Drug addiction has emerged as a growing **menace** in society, affecting not only individuals but also families and communities at large. **Since** the problem often begins subtly, understanding its causes is **essential** to addressing it effectively.

**To begin with**, several factors contribute to the spread of drug addiction. Peer pressure, particularly among **vulnerable** young people, often plays a **significant** role in initiating substance use. **Furthermore**, unemployment and a lack of awareness about the dangers of addiction leave many individuals, especially those from **marginalized** communities, at greater risk. **In addition**, unstable family environments can push individuals toward substance abuse as a coping mechanism.

**Moreover**, the effects of drug addiction are severe and far-reaching. Beyond physical and mental health decline, addiction frequently leads to increased crime rates, financial ruin, and broken families. **As a result**, the social cost extends well beyond the individual, straining community resources and support systems.

**However**, effective solutions do exist. Establishing accessible **rehabilitation** centers can help individuals recover and reintegrate into society. **In addition**, widespread **awareness campaigns**, particularly targeted at schools and colleges, can serve as an early **deterrent** against substance use. **Notably**, stricter enforcement against drug trafficking, combined with community support systems, can help address both the supply and demand sides of the problem.

**In conclusion**, drug addiction is a complex social issue requiring a multi-pronged response — combining prevention, treatment, and enforcement. **Only through concerted efforts** involving families, communities, and government institutions can society hope to **eradicate** this menace and support those affected in rebuilding their lives.

---

### Exam Trap ⚠️
- Keep the tone factual, not preachy — avoid excessive moral judgment
- Include rehabilitation as a solution, not just punishment — a balanced essay scores better
