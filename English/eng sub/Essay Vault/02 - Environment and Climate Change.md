## Guide

**Introduction angle:** Define climate change as a global crisis driven largely by human activity; state its urgency.

**3 Points to build paragraphs around:**
1. Causes — deforestation, fossil fuel use, industrial emissions
2. Effects — rising temperatures, extreme weather, loss of biodiversity
3. Solutions — renewable energy, afforestation, policy reform, individual responsibility

**Key Vocabulary:** sustainability, carbon footprint, ecological balance, degradation, conservation, renewable resources, biodiversity, mitigation, greenhouse emissions, ecosystem

**Tip:** End with a call to action linking individual responsibility to collective impact.

---

## Model Essay (≈350 words)

Climate change has emerged as one of the most **significant** challenges of our time, driven largely by human activity and threatening the **ecological balance** of the planet. **Since** industrialization began, rising greenhouse emissions have altered weather patterns across the globe, making this issue impossible to ignore.

**To begin with**, the causes of climate change are largely man-made. Rampant deforestation, excessive fossil fuel consumption, and unchecked industrial emissions have released enormous quantities of carbon into the atmosphere. **For instance**, large-scale clearing of forests for agriculture and urbanization has stripped the earth of its natural carbon absorbers, **thus** accelerating global warming.

**Furthermore**, the effects of this crisis are already visible. Rising global temperatures have led to extreme weather events — floods, droughts, and unpredictable monsoons — that disrupt agriculture and livelihoods. **Moreover**, the loss of **biodiversity** is alarming, as countless species struggle to survive in rapidly changing habitats. **Consequently**, entire ecosystems are being pushed toward collapse.

**However**, solutions do exist, and the way forward requires **concerted** effort. Transitioning to **renewable resources** such as solar and wind energy can significantly reduce our **carbon footprint**. **In addition**, large-scale afforestation drives and stronger environmental policies can help restore degraded land. **Notably**, individual responsibility also plays a **crucial** role — small actions like reducing plastic use, conserving water, and supporting sustainable practices can collectively create **substantial** change.

**In conclusion**, climate change is not a distant threat but a present reality demanding immediate attention. **Only through concerted efforts** — from governments, industries, and individuals alike — can we hope to preserve the planet for future generations. Every small effort, when multiplied by millions, becomes transformative.

---

### Exam Trap ⚠️
- Don't just list causes/effects — always follow with a solution paragraph; examiners reward balance
- Use "biodiversity" and "ecosystem" correctly — they are not interchangeable (biodiversity = variety of species; ecosystem = the system they live in)
