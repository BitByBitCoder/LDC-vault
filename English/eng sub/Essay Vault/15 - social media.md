# Social Media

**Introduction angle:** Frame social media as a double-edged sword that has changed how people communicate, do business, and form opinions.

**3 points to build paragraphs around:**

1. **Benefits:** instant communication, business and job opportunities, awareness and civic participation
2. **Challenges:** misinformation, cyberbullying, addiction, privacy risks
3. **Way forward:** media literacy, stronger cyber laws, responsible use

**Key vocabulary:** connectivity, virality, misinformation, echo chamber, digital footprint, influencer, outreach, cyberbullying, addiction, accountability

**Connecting words:** To begin with, Moreover, For instance, As a result, However, Nevertheless, Consequently, In addition, In conclusion

**Tip:** Name real platforms (Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram). For Mizoram, mention local entrepreneurs selling food, clothes, or crafts through Facebook and Instagram, and community groups sharing news and relief during crises. Local examples stand out in a Mizoram exam.

## Model Essay (≈350 words)

Social media has emerged as one of the most powerful forces of the modern age, transforming the way people communicate, trade, and think. Platforms such as Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, and Instagram now reach even remote corners of the country, making the world feel smaller than ever before.

To begin with, the benefits of social media are considerable. It allows people to connect instantly across distances and gives ordinary citizens a public voice. Moreover, it has created new business opportunities. Small entrepreneurs, including many young people in Mizoram, can now promote and sell their products to customers without the cost of a physical shop. As a result, social media has become a tool of self-employment and economic growth. It also spreads awareness quickly, whether about government schemes, health campaigns, or emergency relief.

However, this power comes with serious challenges. The rapid spread of misinformation can mislead people and even incite panic or hatred. In addition, cyberbullying and online harassment harm the mental well-being of many users, particularly the young. Excessive use also leads to addiction, reducing productivity and face-to-face interaction. Furthermore, the misuse of personal data raises concerns about privacy and security. Consequently, social media can be as harmful as it is helpful when used carelessly.

Nevertheless, the way forward lies in balance and responsibility. Schools and families must promote media literacy so that users can separate facts from rumours. Strong cyber laws and their proper enforcement are essential to punish online crimes and protect privacy. Above all, individuals must practise self-discipline, thinking before they post or share.

In conclusion, social media is a double-edged sword. Used wisely, it can empower individuals, strengthen businesses, and unite communities. Used carelessly, it can divide and harm. The responsibility therefore rests with every user to make the digital world a safer and more constructive place.

## Exam Trap ⚠️

- Don't write only about dangers or only about benefits. Cover both, then end with a solution.
- Include at least one concrete example (a platform, a local business, or a cyber law) rather than vague statements.
- Keep to the word limit. For the May 2026 paper the limit was 250-300 words, so trim the second and third paragraphs if needed.
