Here's the full categorization of every preposition covered so far:

## 1. Place / Position

at, in, on, above, below, under, over, beside, near/close to, behind, in front of, opposite, between, among, amid/amidst, against (physical contact)

## 2. Time

at, in, on, since, for, during, within, before, after, till/until, throughout, upon (time sense), between (time range)

## 3. Movement / Direction

across, through, into, onto, towards, along, past, over (across obstacle), under (beneath), out of, off, around/round, up/down, from...to, from (origin)

## 4. Manner / Means / Instrument

by (method), with (instrument), with (manner), as (role/function)

## 5. Agent (Passive Voice)

by

## 6. Cause / Reason

since (reason), from (cause), of (cause), because of

## 7. Purpose / Recipient / Support

for (purpose), for (recipient), for (support)

## 8. Comparison / Contrast / Similarity

to (comparison), like, unlike, despite/in spite of, against (opposition)

## 9. Possession / Association / Material

of (belonging), of (material), of (relationship), with (accompaniment)

## 10. Quantity / Measure / Approximation

of (quantity), about (approximation), over (more than a number), under (less than a number)

## 11. Exclusion

except/but, besides (except for)

## 12. Topic

about

---

**One thing worth noting honestly:** several prepositions appear in more than one category (e.g., "over," "under," "of," "by," "with," "for") — that's not double-counting, it's the same multi-job pattern we discussed earlier. The category tells you _which_ job is active in a given sentence, which is actually a useful memorization anchor: if you can identify what category a sentence is testing (place vs time vs cause), you can narrow down which preposition fits faster.
