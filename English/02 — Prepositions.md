## Place Prepositions — at / in / on

| Preposition | Use                                     | Example                                                             |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **at**      | \|\|Specific point/address\|\|          | at the door, at 10 Main Street (Exact point with a building number) |
| **in**      | \|\|Enclosed space / city / country\|\| | in the room, in Aizawl, in India                                    |
| **on**      | \|\|Surface / street name\|\|           | on the table, on Peter's Street (Along the line of the street)      |

## Exam Traps ⚠️
> Famous **for** NOT famous **with** or **about**
> Live **on** a street NOT live **in** a street
> On **foot** NOT by foot or with foot
> Sit **in** a chair NOT sit **on** a chair (you sit on a bench, in a chair)
> Arrive **in** city / arrive **at** station — different for size of place
> Confident **of** NOT confident **about** (in formal/exam English)
