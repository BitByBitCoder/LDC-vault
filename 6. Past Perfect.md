Here are 10 Past Perfect examples, each with a different subject and sequence of two past events.

**1.** Q: By the time I arrived, she -- already. a) leaves b) left c) had left d) has left **Identify:** Two past events; "by the time" flags a sequence → the one that happened FIRST (leaving, before arriving) → **had left**

**2.** Q: He -- his homework before he went to play. a) finish b) finished c) had finished d) has finished **Identify:** "before" = sequence marker; finishing happened FIRST, then playing → **had finished**

**3.** Q: After the guests -- , we cleaned the house. a) leave b) left c) had left d) have left **Identify:** "after" = sequence marker; leaving happened FIRST, then cleaning → **had left**

**4.** Q: I -- never -- such a beautiful sunset before that trip. a) have / seen b) has / seen c) had / seen d) did / see **Identify:** "never...before" set in a past context (before that trip) → the earlier of two past points → **had seen**

**5.** Q: When we reached the station, the train -- already. a) leaves b) left c) had left d) has left **Identify:** "when...already" + two past events (reaching, leaving) → the one that happened FIRST → **had left**

**6.** Q: She -- her degree by the time she was 20. a) completes b) completed c) had completed d) has completed **Identify:** "by the time" + a past age reference → the completing happened before that point → **had completed**

**7.** Q: They -- the movie twice before I watched it. a) see b) saw c) had seen d) have seen **Identify:** "before I watched it" = sequence marker; their watching happened FIRST → **had seen**

**8.** Q: We -- lunch by the time he called. a) finish b) finished c) had finished d) has finished **Identify:** "by the time" + a past event (his call) → finishing happened FIRST → **had finished**

**9.** Q: I realized I -- my keys at home. a) forget b) forgot c) had forgotten d) have forgotten **Identify:** Two past events (realizing, forgetting); the forgetting happened FIRST, before the realizing → **had forgotten**

**10.** Q: The patient -- before the doctor arrived. a) dies b) died c) had died d) has died **Identify:** "before" = sequence marker; dying happened FIRST, then the doctor's arrival → **had died**

---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **had** + V3 (same for all subjects — no he/she/it exception here)
- Signal words: _by the time, before, after, already (in past context), when (with two past events)_
- The key test: **two past actions exist, and you need the one that happened FIRST**

**Key contrast so far:**

- "He **left** before I arrived." (Simple Past + Simple Past — just states two past facts)
- "He **had left** before I arrived." (Past Perfect — emphasizes leaving was already complete when I arrived)
- Both are grammatically fine, but Past Perfect is required when the sequence needs to be crystal clear, especially with "by the time."
