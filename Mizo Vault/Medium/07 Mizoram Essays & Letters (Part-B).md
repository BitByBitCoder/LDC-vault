## 4 reusable essay skeletons (prepare these, cover ~90%)
1. **Water crisis / depletion** — hook (Aizawl summer shortage) → causes (jhum, deforestation, hill topography, urban growth) → impact → govt schemes (Jal Jeevan Mission) → solutions (rainwater harvesting, spring-shed revival) → conclusion
2. **Youth unemployment / skill development** — education vs jobs → govt-job dependence → skill & entrepreneurship (Bana Kaih scheme) → role of YMA/NGOs → way forward
3. **Culture & Tlawmngaihna** — define (selfless service, honour) → Zawlbuk, Hnatlang → erosion by globalisation/social media → preserve via school curriculum, festivals → conclusion
4. **Election / governance / corruption** — 2023 ZPM win (first non-MNF/INC govt) → trends → ethics, accountability → reforms
