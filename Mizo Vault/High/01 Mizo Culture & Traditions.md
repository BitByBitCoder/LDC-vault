> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Pawl Kut** = harvest thanksgiving · **Mim Kut** = honouring the dead · **Chapchar Kut** = spring (March, after jhum clearing)
> - **Puithiam** (village priest) vs **Sadawt/Bawlpu** (sacrificers for sickness) vs **Zawlnei** (prophet): this swap is a favourite
> - Month order: Pawlkut, Ramtuk, **Vau (Mar)**, **Tau (Apr)**, Tomir (May), Nikir (Jun), Vawkhniahzawn (Jul), Thitin (Aug), Mimkut (Sep), Khuangchawi (Oct), **Sahmulphah (Nov)**, **Pawltlak (Dec)**
