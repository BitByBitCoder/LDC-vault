---

**Pattern to lock in:**

- Structure: **will have been** + verb-**ing** — same for all subjects
- Signal words: _by [future time]...for [duration], by the time + future event...for [duration]_
- The key test: an action **started before now**, is **still ongoing**, and will **continue up to** a specific future point

**Key contrast across all three "will have..." forms:**

- "By 6 PM, I **will finish**." (Simple Future — just a future action)
- "By 6 PM, I **will have finished**." (Future Perfect — completed by that time)
- "By 6 PM, I **will have been working** for 8 hours." (Future Perfect Continuous — emphasis on the _duration_ leading up to that time)
