**Phase 1: High-Yield Descriptive Foundation (Part B – 40 Marks)**

  

- **Step 1: Master Passage Comprehension (20 Marks)**
    
      
    - Practice reading 1 dense prose passage daily.
        
          
        
    - Focus on extracting the main topic, answering explicit factual questions, and identifying contextual word meanings.
        
          
        
    - Practice precise, grammatically sound sentence writing when answering open-ended questions.
        
          
        
- **Step 2: Essay Writing Strategy (20 Marks)**
    
      
    - Practice writing 1 full-length essay (~500 words) per week on standard socioeconomic, environmental, or state-level administrative topics.
        
          
        
    - Structure every essay strictly into three parts: **Introduction** (hook + definition), **Body** (3 paragraphs covering causes, effects, and solutions), and **Conclusion** (balanced summary).
        
          
        

**Phase 2: Core Grammar Foundations (Part A – ~30% Objective Weightage)**

  

- **Step 3: Prepositions & Prepositional Phrases (17 Questions / 8.3%)**
    
      
    - Learn basic prepositions of time, place, and movement (`in`, `on`, `at`, `by`, `with`).
        
          
        
    - Memorize fixed prepositions (e.g., _accused of_, _deprived of_, _differ from_, _proficient in_).
        
          
        
- **Step 4: Tenses & Subject-Verb Agreement (16 Questions / 7.8%)**
    
      
    - Memorize the formulas for all 12 tenses and their specific time markers (e.g., _since/for_ with Perfect Continuous).
        
          
        
    - Master subject-verb agreement exceptions (e.g., _neither/nor_, collective nouns, indefinite pronouns like _everyone/each_).
        
          
        
- **Step 5: Parts of Speech Identification (15 Questions / 7.4%)**
    
      
    - Learn to classify underlined words in sentences across the 8 parts of speech (focus heavily on distinguishing Adjectives vs. Adverbs and Nouns vs. Verbs).
        
          
        
- **Step 6: Sentence Correction & Error Spotting (14 Questions / 6.9%)**
    
      
    - Apply tense, agreement, and preposition rules to spot structural errors, misplaced modifiers, and redundant words in sample sentences.
        
          
        

**Phase 3: Conversions & Core Vocabulary (Part A – ~15% Objective Weightage)**

  

- **Step 7: Sentence Transformation Rules (15 Questions Total / ~7.3%)**
    
      
    - **Active & Passive Voice (8 Questions):** Learn standard tense conversion formulas (e.g., Present Simple $\rightarrow$ `is/am/are + V3`) and imperative sentence rules.
        
          
        
    - **Direct & Indirect Speech (7 Questions):** Learn backshifting rules for tenses, pronoun changes, and time word shifts (e.g., _now_ $\rightarrow$ _then_, _today_ $\rightarrow$ _that day_).
        
          
        
- **Step 8: Core Synonyms & Antonyms (15 Questions Total / ~7.3%)**
    
      
    - Study 10 new high-frequency vocabulary words daily. Focus on previous year LDC and state exam papers.
        
          
        
    - Group words by root words, prefixes, and suffixes to solve unfamiliar words by elimination.
        
          
        

**Phase 4: Secondary Vocabulary & Grammar Mechanics (Part A – ~12% Objective Weightage)**

  

- **Step 9: Idioms, Phrases & One Word Substitutions (15 Questions Total / ~7.4%)**
    
      
    - Memorize 10 common English idioms and 10 one-word substitutions daily.
        
          
        
- **Step 10: Conjunctions, Connectors & Spelling (11 Questions Total / ~5.4%)**
    
      
    - Learn subordinate vs. coordinate conjunctions (`although`, `unless`, `not only... but also`).
        
          
        
    - Practice common tricky spelling rules (e.g., _`i` before `e` except after `c`_, double consonants in words like _accommodation_, _occurrence_).
        
          
        
- **Step 11: Articles (2 Questions / 1.0%)**
    
      
    - Review basic rules for definite (`the`) vs. indefinite (`a/an`) articles, focusing on vowel sound exceptions (e.g., _a university_, _an hour_) and omission of articles.
        
          
        

**Phase 5: Full Mock Tests & Time Management**

  

- **Step 12: Previous Year Questions (PYQs) & Timed Practice**
    
      
    - Solve full-length English objective sets within 30–40 minutes to build speed.
        
          
        
    - Review every mistake to categorize whether it was a rule failure (grammar) or lack of knowledge (vocab).
