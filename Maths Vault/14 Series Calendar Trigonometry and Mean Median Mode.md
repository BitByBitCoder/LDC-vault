# Series Calendar Trigonometry and Mean Median Mode

> [!abstract] 🟢 LOW · ≈2% of arithmetic questions · Target accuracy **80%+** · Budget **0.5 day**
> Low frequency — do this **after** every HIGH and MEDIUM note is above 85%. Single-formula questions.

## Core formulas

| Concept | Formula | Quick example / shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| GP n-th term | a·rⁿ⁻¹ | 3, 6, 12 → 8th = 384 |
| GP middle term | x² = a·c | 3, x, 0.75 → 1.5 |
| tan 30°, 45°, 60° | 1/√3, 1, √3 | h = d × tan θ |
| Odd days | Days mod 7 | 61 mod 7 = 5 → Mon + 5 = Sat |
| Mode | 3 Median − 2 Mean | Median 10, Mode 8 → Mean 11 |

## Exam Trap ⚠️

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - Odd-day problems: use **mod 7** and count forward from the given day.
> - tan 30° = 1/√3, tan 60° = √3 — the two are swapped often.
