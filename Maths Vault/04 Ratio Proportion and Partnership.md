# Ratio Proportion and Partnership

> [!abstract] 🔴 HIGH · ≈10% of arithmetic questions · Target accuracy **90%+** · Budget **2 days**
> Includes partnership and alligation. Bar models make most of these 20-second questions.

## Core formulas

| Concept | Formula | Quick example / shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Link two ratios | Make the **common term** equal | A:B = 4:7, B:C = 9:7 → 36:63:49 |
| Share in ratio | Share = total × part / sum of parts |  |
| 3rd proportional of a, b | b²/a | 7, 14 → 28 |
| 4th proportional of a, b, c | bc/a | 3, 7, 9 → 21 |
| Mean proportional | √(ab) | 4, 25 → 10 |
| Partnership | Profit ∝ Capital × Time | Join later → fewer months |
| Alligation | Cheaper : Dearer = (d − m) : (m − c) | ₹8 & ₹12 for ₹9 → 3:1 |

## Visual

```text
 Milk : Water = 7 : 3        add 10 L water  →  7 : 5
 [7x]  [3x]                  [7x] [3x+10]
 7x / (3x+10) = 7/5   →  x = 5   →  milk = 35 L
```

## Exam Trap ⚠️

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - To join A:B and B:C, **equalise the common term first** — never just multiply across.
> - 'Added to both' → write one equation; don't guess-and-check.
> - Partnership: multiply **capital × months**; a late joiner has fewer months.
> - Alligation: the mean price goes in the middle; ratio is the **cross-difference**.
