## Study order

1. **Day 1 cold mock** (25 mixed HIGH questions, no notes) → log in the tracker.
2. Work notes 01 → 09 in order. Per note: read formulas → study the visual → cover-and-solve the Q&A table (⭐ first) → read the Exam Trap.
3. Re-do each note's ⭐⭐⭐ questions after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days (spaced retrieval).
4. Only when 01–09 are ≥ 90%: notes 10 → 13, then 14.
5. Timed drill: HIGH questions in ≤ 60 seconds each.

## Source problems I skipped

These questions have no correct option or are underdetermined in the PDF, so they are not in the vault:

- X scored 10% more than Y, Y 5% more than Z (Z = 300): answer 346.5 is not listed
- 8 persons, average +2.5 kg, 60 kg replaced: answer 80 kg is not listed
- Square wire (side 22 cm) bent into a circle: radius 14 cm is not listed
- Remainder of 7²⁷ ÷ 340: answer 303 is not listed
- KHAWBEL arrangements: 7! = 5040 is not listed
- Mon–Wed / Tue–Thu temperature question: Thursday is underdetermined
- √(14+√49) ÷ (18−√81): works out to √21/9; the key printed √21/3
- Image-only questions (shaded grid, flight graph) cannot be rebuilt from text
