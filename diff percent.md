Imagine the **Cost Price (CP) = 100%**.

### Case 1: Sold at 15% profit

Profit means we **add** 15% to the original 100%.

100%+15%=115%100\%+15\%=115\%

So the selling price is **115% of CP**.

---

### Case 2: Sold at 17% loss

Loss means we **remove** 17% from the original 100%.

100%−17%=83%100\%-17\%=83\%

So the selling price is **83% of CP**.

---

### Now look at the two selling prices

One is **115%**:

```text
CP = 100%
       ↓
   +15%
       ↓
SP = 115%
```

The other is **83%**:

```text

       ↓
   -17%
       ↓
SP = 83%
```

The question says the **difference between these two selling prices is ₹96**.

So we need to find the distance between **115% and 83%**:

115%−83%=32%115\%-83\%=32\%

And **that is why we add 15% and 17%**:

15%+17%=32%15\%+17\%=32\%

Why?

Because one price is **15% above CP**, while the other is **17% below CP**.

So the total distance between them is:

15%+17%=32%\boxed{15\%+17\%=32\%}
