> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - Nobel Peace 2025 (Machado) and Sakharov 2025 (Poczobut, Amaglobeli) are different awards and different people.
> - 'Nobel Prize of Asia' = Magsaysay, NOT Dadasaheb Phalke or Arjuna.
> - Padma awards rank: Vibhushan > Bhushan > Shri. Bharat Ratna is separate and highest.
> - *Economics is the Sveriges Riksbank prize in memory of Alfred Nobel, added 1969.
