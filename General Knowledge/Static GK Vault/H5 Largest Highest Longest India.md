> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - Tallest statue = Statue of Unity (India). Don't confuse with Brazil's Christ the Redeemer or Malaysia's Murugan.
> - Largest forest cover by AREA = Madhya Pradesh; by PERCENTAGE = Mizoram.
> - Largest brackish = Chilika; largest inland salt lake = Sambhar.
> - Longest river in India is Ganga; Brahmaputra and Indus are longer overall but flow through other countries.
