> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️

> - Mim Kut ≠ Pawl Kut ≠ Chapchar Kut: Mim = dead/maize, Pawl = harvest thanksgiving, Chapchar = spring.
> - Kerala has two classical dances: Kathakali and Mohiniyattam.
