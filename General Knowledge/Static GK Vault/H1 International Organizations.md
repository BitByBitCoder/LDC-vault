> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - WHO and WTO are both in Geneva; IMF and World Bank are both in Washington; UN and UNICEF are in New York; UNESCO is in Paris, not Geneva.

> - G20 presidency rotates yearly: India 2023, Brazil 2024, South Africa 2025. Don't answer 'India' for 'current' unless the question year says 2023.
> - SAARC secretariat is Kathmandu; BIMSTEC is Dhaka.
