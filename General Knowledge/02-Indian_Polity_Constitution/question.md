## **Important observations for your LDC preparation**

These papers repeatedly test certain areas.

|Priority|Topic|What to master|
|---|---|---|
|Very high|Constitution|Parts, Articles, Schedules|
|Very high|Amendments|42nd, 73rd, 74th, 86th, 91st|
|Very high|Fundamental Rights|Articles 14–32|
|Very high|Parliament|Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, Speaker|
|Very high|President & Governor|Appointment, powers, resignation|
|High|Judiciary|Supreme Court, High Court, Attorney General|
|High|Constitutional bodies|UPSC, MPSC, CAG, Election Commission|
|High|Local government|Panchayati Raj, Municipalities|
|Very high|Mizoram polity|Assembly, Ministers, Accord, ADC, ILP|

**My recommendation:** Study the Constitution first, especially Articles, Parts, Schedules and Amendments. Then move to Parliament, President, Governor and Mizoram polity.
