> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Part VI, Art. 168-212**.
> - **Unicameral = 22 States + 3 UTs (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K)**; **Bicameral = 6 States**.
> - **6 Council States**: Karnataka, Bihar, UP, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana.
> - **169 = creation/abolition of Councils**; **178 = Speaker**; **182 = Chairman**; **194 = privileges**; **199 = money bills**; **202 = budget**.
> - **Compare with Parliament**: Budget = Art. 112 (Union) vs **Art. 202 (State)**.
