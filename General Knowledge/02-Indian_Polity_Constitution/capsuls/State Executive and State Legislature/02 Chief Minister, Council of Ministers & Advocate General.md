# 02 Chief Minister, Council of Ministers & Advocate General

## Chief Minister

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| CM is the? | \|\|Real Executive Head of the State\|\| |
| CM heads? | \|\|Council of Ministers\|\| |
| CM is leader of? | \|\|Majority Party (also of the Legislative Assembly)\|\| |
| CM is Chairman of? | \|\|State Planning Board\|\| |
| Citizenship? | \|\|Must be a citizen of India\|\| |
| Minimum age? | \|\|25 years\|\| |
| Membership? | \|\|Member of State Legislature (if not, must get elected within 6 months)\|\| |
| Appointment / Oath by? | \|\|Governor [Article 164(1)]\|\| |
| Term? | \|\|Pleasure of the Governor - usually 5 years\|\| |
| Salary & Allowances determined by? | \|\|State Legislature\|\| |

## Powers of Chief Minister

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Power 1? | \|\|Presides over meetings of Council of Ministers\|\| |
| Power 2? | \|\|Distributes portfolios among the ministers\|\| |
| Power 3? | \|\|Advises Governor to summon & dissolve the Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Power 4? | \|\|Recommends appointments & removals of ministers\|\| |
| Power 5? | \|\|Crisis Manager-in-Chief during Emergency\|\| |

## Council of Ministers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Role? | \|\|To aid and advise the Governor\|\| |
| Eligibility? | \|\|Must be member of State Legislature (if not, must get elected within 6 months)\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By Governor on advice of Chief Minister\|\| |
| Responsible to? | \|\|State Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Maximum strength? | \|\|Cannot exceed 15% of total members of Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Minimum ministers? | \|\|Not less than 12 ministers (as per 91st Constitutional Amendment)\|\| |
| Delhi? | \|\|Maximum 10% or 7 (as per 69th Constitutional Amendment)\|\| |
| Ministers in committees? | \|\|Can participate in committee discussions, but do not have voting rights\|\| |

## Advocate General (Art. 165)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article? | \|\|Art. 165\|\| |
| Position? | \|\|Highest Law officer of the State\|\| |
| Qualification? | \|\|Must be qualified to be a Judge of High Court\|\| |
| Appointed by? | \|\|Governor\|\| |
| Main role? | \|\|To advise the State Government on legal matters\|\| |
| Rights in Legislature? | \|\|Can participate in proceedings of the State Legislature but cannot vote\|\| |
| Term? | \|\|At the pleasure of the Governor\|\| |

| Feature | Governor | Chief Minister |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Constitutional / nominal head | Real executive head |
| Min age | 35 | 25 |
| Appointed by | President | Governor [Art. 164(1)] |
| Term | 5 years (pleasure of President) | Pleasure of Governor (usually 5 years) |
| Salary set by | Parliament | State Legislature |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **CM age 25**; **Governor age 35**.
> - **CM**: appointed by **Governor (Art. 164(1))**; must be member within **6 months**.
> - **Council of Ministers**: max **15%** of Assembly strength; min **12** (**91st CAA**); **Delhi**: **10% or 7** (**69th CAA**).
> - **CM = Chairman of State Planning Board**; **Crisis Manager-in-Chief**.
> - **Advocate General (Art. 165)**: HC-judge qualification, appointed by Governor, speaks but **cannot vote**.
