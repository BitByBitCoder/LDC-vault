| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Power 1? | \|\|Summon & prorogue sessions of State Legislature\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Age 35**; **term 5 years at pleasure of President**; **oath by Chief Justice of High Court**.
> - **Art. 153 = Governors; 155 = appointment; 161 = pardon; 213 = ordinances; 160 = contingency; 162 = extent of executive power**.
> - **Governor cannot pardon death sentence** or court-martial cases (only **President**).
> - **Nominates 1/6th** of Legislative Council members.
> - **Salary**: Rs 3.5 lakh/month by **Parliament** (textbook table); 7th CAA line says President determines it for a Governor of multiple States.
> - **1st woman Governor**: **Sarojini Naidu**.
