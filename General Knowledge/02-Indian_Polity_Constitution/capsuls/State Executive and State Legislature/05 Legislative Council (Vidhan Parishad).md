# 05 Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)

## Basics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Legislative Council is the? | \|\|Upper House of the State Legislature\|\| |
| Is it dissolved? | \|\|Permanent Body (Not subject to dissolution)\|\| |
| Who creates / abolishes it? | \|\|Parliament\|\| |
| Maximum strength? | \|\|Not more than 1/3rd of Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Minimum members? | \|\|40 members\|\| |
| Actual strength determined by? | \|\|Parliament\|\| |
| Formation requires? | \|\|Special Majority of Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Majority needed in Parliament? | \|\|Simple Majority for the motion to pass\|\| |
| Tenure of members? | \|\|6 years\|\| |
| Retirement? | \|\|1/3rd members retire every 2 years in rotation\|\| |

## Legislative Assembly vs Legislative Council

| Feature | Legislative Assembly | Legislative Council |
|---|---|---|
| House | Lower House | Upper House |
| Min / Max | 60 / 500 | 40 / 1/3rd of Assembly |
| Min age | 25 | 30 |
| Tenure | 5 years | 6 years (1/3 retire every 2 yrs) |
| Dissolution | Yes | No (permanent) |
| Presiding officer | Speaker | Chairman |
| Creation / abolition | - | Parliament |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Council is created/abolished by Parliament** (on the Assembly's special-majority resolution); **simple majority** in Parliament.
> - **Max 1/3rd of Assembly, min 40**.
> - **Election mix**: **1/3 MLAs, 1/3 local bodies, 1/12 teachers, 1/12 graduates, 1/6 Governor-nominated** (= 1).
> - **Tenure 6 years**, **1/3 retire every 2 years**.
> - **Salary: Art. 195**; **Chairman = Art. 182**.
