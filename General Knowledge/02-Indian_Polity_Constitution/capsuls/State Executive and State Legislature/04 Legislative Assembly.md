# 04 Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)

## Members & Election

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Number of members based on? | \|\|State Population\|\| |
| Minimum / Maximum members? | \|\|60 / 500\|\| |
| Largest Legislative Assembly? | \|\|Uttar Pradesh (403 members)\|\| |
| Sikkim members? | \|\|32\|\| |
| Goa members? | \|\|40\|\| |
| Mizoram members? | \|\|40\|\| |
| 1st e-Assembly Constituency? | \|\|Palampur, Kangra (Himachal Pradesh)\|\| |
| Election process? | \|\|Adult Suffrage (voting age - 18 years)\|\| |

## Tenure & Qualifications

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tenure? | \|\|Usually 5 years\|\| |
| Can it be dissolved earlier? | \|\|Yes\|\| |
| During emergency? | \|\|Extension up to 1 year at a time (not more than 6 months after emergency ends)\|\| |
| Citizenship? | \|\|Indian Citizen\|\| |
| Minimum age - Legislative Assembly? | \|\|25 years\|\| |
| Minimum age - Legislative Council? | \|\|30 years\|\| |
| Elector requirement? | \|\|Must be an elector of relevant Legislative Assembly Constituency\|\| |
| Oath administered by? | \|\|Governor\|\| |

## Speaker & Deputy Speaker

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How are they chosen? | \|\|Elected from among its own members\|\| |
| Tenure? | \|\|Usually till the assembly term\|\| |
| Resignation? | \|\|Speaker to Deputy Speaker and vice-versa\|\| |
| Removal? | \|\|By Legislative Assembly - 14 days' prior notice\|\| |
| If Speaker is absent? | \|\|Deputy Speaker acts as Speaker\|\| |

## Powers of Legislative Assembly

| Power | Detail |
|---|---|
| Executive | \|\|Controls the State Government and Council of Ministers\|\| |
| Legislative | \|\|Makes laws on subjects under State List & Concurrent List\|\| |
| Election-related | \|\|In election of President and Rajya Sabha members\|\| |
| Financial | \|\|Budget can only be introduced in Legislative Assembly; without Assembly's approval, no taxes can be levied, no money can be withdrawn\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Min 60, max 500**; exceptions: **Sikkim 32**, **Goa 40**, **Mizoram 40**; largest = **UP (403)**.
> - **Age: LA 25, LC 30**.
> - **Oath by Governor**; **Speaker removal: 14 days' notice**.
> - Emergency: **1 year at a time**, not beyond **6 months** after emergency ends (same as Lok Sabha).
> - **Voting age = 18**.
> - **1st e-Assembly**: **Palampur, Kangra (HP)**.
