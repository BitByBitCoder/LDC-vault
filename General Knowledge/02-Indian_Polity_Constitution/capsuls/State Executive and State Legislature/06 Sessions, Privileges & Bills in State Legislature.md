# 06 Sessions, Privileges & Bills in State Legislature

## Sessions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Minimum sessions every year? | \|\|2\|\| |
| Who prorogues? | \|\|Governor\|\| |
| Who adjourns? | \|\|Presiding Officer\|\| |
| Who dissolves? | \|\|Governor (only for Legislative Assembly)\|\| |
| When is the Assembly dissolved? | \|\|When tenure is over, or in case of failure to prove majority in the House\|\| |

## Privileges (Art. 194)

| Collective Privileges | Individual Privileges |
|---|---|
| \|\|Right to hold secret sessions\|\| | \|\|Immunity from Civil Arrest\|\| |
| \|\|Authority to arrest under simple warrant\|\| | \|\|Proceedings of the House cannot be challenged in Court\|\| |
| \|\|Punishment for Contempt of House (Member / Non-Member)\|\| | \|\|Freedom from being summoned as a witness\|\| |
| \|\|Right to publish or restrict proceedings\|\| | \|\|Freedom of speech (in house)\|\| |

## Ordinary Bills

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where introduced? | \|\|Either House - Legislative Assembly or Legislative Council\|\| |
| Final authority? | \|\|Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Council can delay the Bill by? | \|\|3 months on 1st instance; 1 additional month on re-passage\|\| |

## Money Bills

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where introduced? | \|\|Only in Legislative Assembly\|\| |
| Council's power? | \|\|Can only withhold for 14 days\|\| |

| Feature | Ordinary Bill | Money Bill |
|---|---|---|
| Introduced in | Either House | Only Legislative Assembly |
| Council's power | Delay 3 months (1st), +1 month (re-passage) | Withhold 14 days only |
| Final authority | Legislative Assembly | Legislative Assembly |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Min 2 sessions/year**; **prorogation = Governor**; **adjournment = Presiding Officer**; **dissolution = Governor (Assembly only)**.
> - **Art. 194** = privileges (**collective vs individual**).
> - **Council**: ordinary bill delay **3 months + 1 month**; money bill **14 days**.
> - **Money Bill** introduced **only in Assembly** (Art. 199).
> - Compare with Parliament: Rajya Sabha withholds a Money Bill for **14 days** too.
