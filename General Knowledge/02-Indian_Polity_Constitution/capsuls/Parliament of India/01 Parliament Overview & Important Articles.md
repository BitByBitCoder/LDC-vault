# 01 Parliament Overview & Important Articles

## Parliament - 3 Parts

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Parliament of India consists of which 3 parts? | \|\|President, Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha\|\| |
| Which part of Parliament is needed for a Bill to become an Act? | \|\|President - no Bill can become an Act without the assent of the President\|\| |
| Lok Sabha is also called? | \|\|Lower House / House of the People\|\| |
| Rajya Sabha is also called? | \|\|Upper House / Council of States\|\| |

## Articles Quick-Match (reverse)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which Article = Composition of Rajya Sabha? | \|\|Art. 80\|\| |
| Which Article = Composition of Lok Sabha? | \|\|Art. 81\|\| |
| Which Article = Duration of Houses? | \|\|Art. 83\|\| |
| Which Article = Sessions, prorogation, dissolution? | \|\|Art. 85\|\| |
| Which Article = President's address to both Houses? | \|\|Art. 86\|\| |
| Which Article = Special address by the President? | \|\|Art. 87\|\| |
| Which Article = Speaker & Deputy Speaker of LS? | \|\|Art. 93\|\| |
| Which Article = Secretariat of Parliament? | \|\|Art. 98\|\| |
| Which Article = Chairman of RS? | \|\|Art. 89 (Art. 89(1): Vice President is ex-officio Chairman)\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **80 vs 81**: 80 = Rajya Sabha, 81 = Lok Sabha (RS comes first).
> - **85 vs 86 vs 87**: 85 = sessions/prorogation/dissolution; 86 = normal address to both Houses; 87 = *special* address (start of first session after election + first session of each year).
> - **89 vs 93**: 89 = RS Chairman/Deputy Chairman; 93 = LS Speaker/Deputy Speaker.
> - **Art. 92 is not in the list** - do not confuse it with 91/93.
