| Question                                 | Answer                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Article for grounds of disqualification? | \|\|Art. 102\|\|                                                    |

## Disqualification at a Glance

| Ground           | Detail                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Mind             | Unsound mind                         |
| Money            | Insolvent                            |
| Jail             | Imprisonment (2 years)               |
| Absence          | 60+ days without permission          |
| Defection        | 52nd Amendment (1985), 10th Schedule |
| Who disqualifies | President (Art. 103) on EC advice    |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Art. 102 = grounds**; **Art. 103 = who decides** (President on EC advice).
> - **Defection**: decision by the **Presiding Officer**, not the President/EC.
> - **Imprisonment**: textbook says "not more than 2 years". In standard law (RP Act 1951) a sentence of **2 years or more** disqualifies. Read the question wording carefully.
> - **60+ days** absence without permission of the House - memorise the number 60.
