# 04 Speaker, Chairman & Presiding Officers

## Speaker of Lok Sabha

| Question                                          | Answer                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How many Speakers and Deputy Speakers in LS?      | \|\|1 Speaker and 1 Deputy Speaker, elected among Lok Sabha members\|\| |
| Who chairs the Joint Sitting of both Houses?      | \|\|Speaker of LS\|\|                                                   |
| Article for Joint Sitting?                        | \|\|Art. 108\|\|                                                        |
| Who calls the Joint Sitting?                      | \|\|President\|\|                                                       |
| If Speaker is absent at Joint Sitting?            | \|\|Chaired by Deputy Speaker\|\|                                       |
| If Deputy Speaker is also absent?                 | \|\|Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha\|\|                                  |
| Who appoints members to Parliamentary Committees? | \|\|Speaker\|\|                                                         |
| Who gives the casting vote in case of a tie?      | \|\|Speaker\|\|                                                         |
| Who decides adjournment of meetings?              | \|\|Speaker\|\|                                                         |
| To whom does the Speaker resign?                  | \|\|Deputy Speaker (and vice versa)\|\|                                 |
| How is the Speaker removed?                       | \|\|By a majority of the then members (14 days' prior notice)\|\|       |
| Article for vacancy of LS Speaker?                | \|\|Art. 94 ban theih dan\|\|                                           |

## Comparison

| Feature | Speaker (LS) | Chairman (RS) |
|---|---|---|
| Article | 93 | 89 |
| Who is it | Elected from among LS members | Vice President (ex-officio) |
| Member of House? | Yes | No |
| Deputy | Deputy Speaker | Deputy Chairman |
| Resignation to | Deputy Speaker | Deputy Chairman resigns to Chairman |
| Removal | Majority of then members, 14 days' notice | Deputy Chairman removed by majority of RS |
| Casting vote | Yes | Yes |
| Chairs Joint Sitting | Yes | No (only Deputy Chairman as last option) |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Speaker** is elected *from among* LS members; **Chairman** is the Vice President - *not* a member of either House.
> - **Joint sitting order**: Speaker -> Deputy Speaker -> Deputy Chairman of RS. The **Chairman of RS never chairs** it.
> - **Pro-tem Speaker** = senior-most MP, appointed by the **President**.
> - **Meira Kumar** = 1st woman Speaker (2009-14); **Mavalankar** = 1st Speaker (1952-56).
> - **Check with your teacher**: standard polity books say a member of the *Panel of Vice-Chairmen* presides in the Chairman's absence (not "appointed by President"). Learn the textbook line for this exam but be aware.
