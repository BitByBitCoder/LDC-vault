# 03 Rajya Sabha

## Basic Facts

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| First session of Rajya Sabha? | \|\|13 May 1952\|\| |
| Is Rajya Sabha subject to dissolution? | \|\|No - Permanent House\|\| |
| Maximum strength of RS? | \|\|250\|\| |
| Max strength split? | \|\|238 Elected + 12 Nominated\|\| |
| 238 elected members split? | \|\|230 from States + 8 from UTs (as per textbook)\|\| |

## Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha

| Feature | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|---|---|---|
| Other name | House of the People / Lower House | Council of States / Upper House |
| Article of composition | 81 | 80 |
| Max strength | 550 | 250 |
| Current strength | 543 | 245 |
| Election | Direct | Indirect (STV) |
| Min age | 25 | 30 |
| Term | 5 years (dissolvable) | 6 years (permanent house) |
| Retirement | Whole house dissolves | 1/3 retire every 2 years |
| Presiding officer | Speaker | Chairman (Vice President) |
| Budget | Votes on budget | Only discusses |
| No-confidence motion | Yes | No |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **250 = 238 + 12**; **245 current = 233 (225 States + 8 UTs) + 12 nominated**.
> - Textbook writes "225 (States) + 8 (UTs)" for current strength - the 12 nominated are on top.
> - **J&K = 4, Delhi = 3, Puducherry = 1** - commonly swapped.
> - RS = **indirectly** elected; LS = **directly** elected. Nominated members are **not** elected at all.
> - RS is not dissolved; only the *members'* 6-year terms roll over.
