> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Max vs Current**: 550 (max) vs 543 (current directly elected). Do not mix.
> - **Age**: LS = **25**, RS = **30**.
> - **Term**: LS = 5 years (can be dissolved) vs RS = permanent (6-year member term).
> - **Commissions (4)**: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002 - but **Acts (3)**: 1962, 1972, 2002.
> - **84th Amendment (2001)** freeze till **2026**; **104th** removed Anglo-Indian reservation.
