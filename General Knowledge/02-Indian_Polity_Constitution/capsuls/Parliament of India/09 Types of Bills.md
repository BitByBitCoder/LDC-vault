# 09 Types of Bills

## Parliamentary Bill

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article for Parliamentary Bill? | \|\|Art. 107\|\| |
| Where can a Bill be introduced? | \|\|Either House of Parliament (Art. 107)\|\| |

## Major Types

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Major types of Bills? | \|\|Ordinary Bill, Money Bill, Constitution Amendment Bill\|\| |
| Ordinary Bill has which two kinds? | \|\|Public Bill and Private Member Bill\|\| |

## Ordinary Bills

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Public Bill - where introduced? | \|\|Any House\|\| |
| Public Bill - notice period? | \|\|7 days prior notice\|\| |
| Public Bill - joint sitting? | \|\|Joint sitting possible\|\| |
| Private Member Bill - who introduces? | \|\|Non-minister\|\| |
| Private Member Bill - notice period? | \|\|30 days notice\|\| |

## Money Bill

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where can a Money Bill be introduced? | \|\|In LS only, with prior consent of President\|\| |
| Who decides if a Bill is a Money Bill? | \|\|LS Speaker\|\| |
| Rajya Sabha's power on a Money Bill? | \|\|Withhold for 14 days; no amendment power\|\| |
| Joint sitting on Money Bill? | \|\|No provision for joint sitting\|\| |

## Constitution Amendment Bill

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Joint sitting on Constitution Amendment Bill? | \|\|No provision for joint sitting\|\| |

## Joint Sittings (Art. 108)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How many joint sittings till now? | \|\|3\|\| |
| Joint sitting 1? | \|\|Dowry Prohibition Bill, 1961\|\| |
| Joint sitting 2? | \|\|Banking Service Commission (Repeal) Bill, 1977\|\| |
| Joint sitting 3? | \|\|Prevention of Terrorism Bill, 2002\|\| |

## Comparison

| Feature | Public Bill | Private Member Bill | Money Bill | Const. Amendment Bill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduced in | Any House | Any House | LS only | Either House |
| By | Minister | Non-minister | Minister (President's consent) | - |
| Notice | 7 days | 30 days | - | - |
| Joint sitting | Possible | Possible (ordinary) | No | No |
| RS power | Full | Full | Withhold 14 days, no amendment | - |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **7 days (public)** vs **30 days (private member)** notice.
> - **Money Bill**: LS only, **Speaker decides**, RS withholds only **14 days**, **no joint sitting**.
> - **Constitution Amendment Bill**: no provision for joint sitting.
> - **3 joint sittings**: Dowry (1961), Banking Service Commission Repeal (1977), POTA (2002).
