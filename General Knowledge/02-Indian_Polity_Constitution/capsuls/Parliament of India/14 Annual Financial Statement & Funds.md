# 14 Annual Financial Statement & Funds

## Annual Financial Statement (Art. 112)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article for Annual Financial Statement? | \|\|Art. 112\|\| |
| What is it? | \|\|Statement of estimate of receipts & expenditure of Government (by President)\|\| |
| Who presents it? | \|\|Finance Minister\|\| |
| Is the word 'Budget' in the Constitution? | \|\|No - not mentioned\|\| |
| First Budget presented by? | \|\|R.K. Shanmukham Chetty (26 Nov 1947)\|\| |
| Expenditure charged on Consolidated Fund - votable? | \|\|Not votable (e.g. Salary / Allowances)\|\| |
| Other expenditure? | \|\|Vote in Lok Sabha only\|\| |
| Rajya Sabha's role in budget? | \|\|Can only discuss, no voting rights\|\| |
| Who has voting rights related to budget? | \|\|Only Lok Sabha\|\| |

## Types of Funds

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Consolidated Fund of India - Article? | \|\|Art. 266(1)\|\| |
| Consolidated Fund - what? | \|\|All receipts are credited and all payments are debited\|\| |
| Contingency Fund of India - Article? | \|\|Art. 267\|\| |
| Contingency Fund - what? | \|\|Reserve for emergency expenditures\|\| |
| Public Accounts of India - Article? | \|\|Art. 266(2)\|\| |
| Public Accounts - what? | \|\|Savings accounts of various ministries, Disaster Management Fund, Provident Fund, Defence Fund\|\| |

| Fund | Article | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Consolidated Fund of India | 266(1) | All receipts credited, all payments debited |
| Contingency Fund of India | 267 | Reserve for emergency expenditures |
| Public Accounts of India | 266(2) | Savings accounts of ministries, Disaster Management Fund, Provident Fund, Defence Fund |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Art. 112 = Budget (Annual Financial Statement)** - but the word "Budget" never appears in the Constitution.
> - **266(1) = Consolidated Fund**, **266(2) = Public Accounts**, **267 = Contingency Fund**.
> - **Salary/Allowances (charged expenditure) = NOT votable**; everything else - **LS votes only**.
> - **RS** = discuss only.
> - First Budget: **R.K. Shanmukham Chetty, 26 Nov 1947**.
