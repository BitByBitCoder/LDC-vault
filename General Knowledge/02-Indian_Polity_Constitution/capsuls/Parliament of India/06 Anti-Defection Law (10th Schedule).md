# 06 Anti-Defection Law (10th Schedule)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which Amendment introduced the anti-defection law? | \|\|52nd Amendment (1985)\|\| |
| Which Schedule? | \|\|10th Schedule\|\| |
| Condition (a)? | \|\|Independent member joining a political party\|\| |
| Condition (b)? | \|\|Nominated member joining a party (after 6 months)\|\| |
| Condition (c)? | \|\|Not obeying the whip\|\| |
| Condition (d)? | \|\|Member joining another party\|\| |
| Exception to (d)? | \|\|2/3rd members joining or party merger\|\| |
| Who decides on defection? | \|\|Presiding Officer - Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS)\|\| |
| Who disqualifies the members (Art. 103)? | \|\|President, on the advice of Election Commission\|\| |
| Key case on defection? | \|\|Kihoto Hollohan v. Zachillhu case (1992)\|\| |
| Key argument 1 of the case? | \|\|Presiding Officer's decision is final\|\| |
| Key argument 2 of the case? | \|\|Judiciary can review\|\| |

## Conditions Table

| Condition | Meaning                                         | Exception                             |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| (a)       | Independent member joins a political party      | -                                     |
| (b)       | Nominated member joins a party (after 6 months) | -                                     |
| (c)       | Not obeying whip                                | -                                     |
| (d)       | Member joins another party                      | 2/3rd members joining or party merger |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Nominated member** may join a party within **6 months** safely; after 6 months -> disqualified.
> - **Independent** joining a party -> disqualified (no waiting period).
> - **Exception = 2/3rd merger** (not 1/3rd, not majority).
> - **Kihoto Hollohan (1992)**: Presiding Officer final **but** subject to **judicial review**.
> - **52nd Amendment (1985)** = 10th Schedule.
