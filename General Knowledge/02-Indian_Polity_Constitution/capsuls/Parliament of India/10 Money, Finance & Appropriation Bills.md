# 10 Money, Finance & Appropriation Bills

## Money Bill (Art. 110)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article of Money Bill? | \|\|Art. 110\|\| |
| Direction of Money Bill passage? | \|\|LS -> RS (limited power) -> President\|\| |
| Relation between Money & Financial Bills? | \|\|All Money Bills are Financial Bills, but all Financial Bills are not Money Bills\|\| |

## Finance Bill (Art. 117(3))

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article of Finance Bill? | \|\|Art. 117(3)\|\| |
| Who handles a Finance Bill? | \|\|President & Lok Sabha\|\| |
| How is it treated? | \|\|Like an Ordinary Bill\|\| |
| Related Article noted in the table? | \|\|Presidential Ordinance (Art. 123)\|\| |

## Appropriation Bill (Art. 114)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article of Appropriation Bill? | \|\|Art. 114\|\| |
| What does it authorize? | \|\|Government to withdraw funds from Consolidated Fund of India\|\| |
| Voting right? | \|\|No voting right (on the bill itself)\|\| |
| What is moved during voting? | \|\|Cut Motions\|\| |

## Cut Motions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Policy Cut Motion? | \|\|Amount of the demand be reduced to Rs 1/-\|\| |
| Economy Cut Motion? | \|\|Amount of demand be reduced by a specified amount\|\| |
| Token Cut Motion? | \|\|Amount of demand be reduced by Rs 100/-\|\| |

## Comparison

| Feature      | Money Bill                            | Finance Bill                      | Appropriation Bill        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Article      | 110                                   | 117(3)                            | 114                       |
| Route        | LS -> RS (limited power) -> President | President & Lok Sabha             | Withdraws funds from CFI  |
| Treated like | Special                               | Ordinary Bill                     | -                         |
| Extra note   | All Money Bills are Financial Bills   | Presidential Ordinance (Art. 123) | Cut Motions during voting |

## Cut Motions Comparison

| Cut Motion | Effect |
|---|---|
| Policy Cut | Demand reduced to Rs 1 |
| Economy Cut | Demand reduced by specified amount |
| Token Cut | Demand reduced by Rs 100 |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **110 = Money, 117(3) = Finance, 114 = Appropriation**.
> - **All Money Bills are Financial Bills - not vice versa.**
> - **Policy Cut = Rs 1**; **Token Cut = Rs 100**; Economy Cut = specified amount.
> - **Art. 123 = Presidential Ordinance** (separate from Finance Bill - noted in the table beside it).
