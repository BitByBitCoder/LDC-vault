## Who Does What

| Action | Authority |
|---|---|
| Summoning | President |
| Prorogation | President |
| Adjournment | Speaker of LS |
| Special Address | President (Art. 87) |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Summon/Prorogue = President**; **Adjourn = Speaker** (do not swap).
> - **Question Hour = 11-12**, **Zero Hour = 12-1** (Zero Hour follows).
> - **Winter** = smallest session; **Budget** = longest (Feb-May).
> - **Sine Die** = no next date fixed.
> - Textbook defines *Lame Duck* as a member who has lost / not been re-elected (concept from USA).
