# 08 National Commissions for SC, ST & Backward Classes

## National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Original body (before bifurcation)? | \|\|National Commission for Scheduled Castes & Scheduled Tribes (NCSC & NCST)\|\| |
| Created by? | \|\|65th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1990\|\| |
| Formed under? | \|\|Article 338\|\| |
| Composition? | \|\|Chairperson + Vice Chairperson + other 3 Members\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Tenure? | \|\|3 years\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|To protect the interests of Scheduled Castes\|\| |

## National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Established in? | \|\|1993, as a Statutory Body\|\| |
| Constitutional status by? | \|\|102nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2018\|\| |
| Formed under? | \|\|Article 338(B)\|\| |
| Composition? | \|\|Chairperson + Vice Chairperson + other 3 Members\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Tenure (Chairman)? | \|\|3 years\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Duties & powers related to safeguarding the rights & interests of Backward Classes in India\|\| |

| Commission | Article | Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| NCSC | 338 | 65th CAA 1990 |
| NCST | 338A | 89th CAA 2003 (effective 2004) |
| NCBC | 338B | 102nd CAA 2018 (statutory since 1993) |

## Linguistic Minorities Commission

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Formed under? | \|\|Article 350(B)\|\| |
| Which Amendment? | \|\|7th Constitutional Amendment, 1956\|\| |
| Nature? | \|\|A special officer for linguistic minorities (one-membered body)\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Works under? | \|\|Union Ministry of Minority Affairs\|\| |
| Headquarters? | \|\|New Delhi\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **338 = SC, 338A = ST, 338B = Backward Classes**; **350B = Linguistic Minorities**.
> - **65th (1990)** joint SC/ST -> **89th (2003)** split; **102nd (2018)** gave NCBC constitutional status.
> - All three Commissions: **Chairperson + Vice Chairperson + 3 members**, tenure **3 years**, appointed by **President**.
> - **Linguistic Minorities** body is **one-membered**, **7th Amendment 1956**.
