| Party | Year | Founder / Origin | Key Fact |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **BSP 1984 (Kanshi Ram)** vs **AAP 2012 (Kejriwal)** vs **NPP 2013 (P.A. Sangma)** - memorise the year-founder pairs.
> - **NPP** = the Eastern/North-East party (relevant for Mizoram-region questions).
> - "Ruling", "main opposition", "currently in power" lines can change - answer as per your exam's cutoff.
