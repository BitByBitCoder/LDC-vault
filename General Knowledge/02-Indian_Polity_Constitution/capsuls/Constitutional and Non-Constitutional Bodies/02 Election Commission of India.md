# 02 Election Commission of India (ECI)

## Basics

| Question                                   | Answer                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Which Part of the Constitution covers ECI? | \|\|Part XV\|\|                           |
| Articles for ECI?                          | \|\|Articles 324-329\|\|                  |
| Nature of the body?                        | \|\|Quasi-judicial body\|\|               |
| Date of establishment of ECI?              | \|\|25 January 1950\|\|                   |
| National Voters' Day is on?                | \|\|25 January every year, since 2011\|\| |
| 1st Chief Election Commissioner?           | \|\|Sukumar Sen\|\|                       |

## Composition

| Question                     | Answer                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ECI was originally a?        | \|\|Single-member body until 16 October 1989\|\|                             |
| Became multi-member from?    | \|\|1 October 1993\|\|                                                       |
| Present composition?         | \|\|1 Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) + 2 other Election Commissioners\|\| |
| Who changes the composition? | \|\|President\|\|                                                            |

## Appointment, Tenure, Removal

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who appoints the CEC & ECs? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Based on recommendation of? | \|\|Committee headed by Prime Minister\|\| |
| Two other members of the committee (textbook)? | \|\|Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha and Chief Justice of India\|\| |
| Source of this rule? | \|\|Supreme Court judgement in Anoop Baranwal vs Union of India, 2023\|\| |
| Tenure of CEC? | \|\|6 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Tenure of other ECs? | \|\|6 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Resignation to? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Grounds of removal? | \|\|Misconduct or incapacity\|\| |
| Removal of CEC? | \|\|Like a Supreme Court Judge\|\| |
| Removal of other ECs? | \|\|On CEC's recommendation\|\| |
| Post-tenure appointment (textbook)? | \|\|Eligible for further appointment by the Union government\|\| |

## Functions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Function 1? | \|\|Conducting or supervising elections to Parliament, State Legislatures & offices of President & Vice-President\|\| |
| Function 2? | \|\|Preparing the Electoral Rolls\|\| |
| Function 3? | \|\|Allotment of election symbols to political parties\|\| |
| Function 4? | \|\|Delimitation of constituencies (as listed in textbook)\|\| |
| Function 5? | \|\|Granting recognition to national and state parties\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Part XV, Art. 324-329**; established **25 Jan 1950**; National Voters' Day since **2011**.
> - Single -> multi-member: **1989 (briefly) / 1993 (permanent)**; now **1 CEC + 2 ECs**.
> - **CEC removal = like SC Judge**; other ECs removed only on **CEC's recommendation**.
> - **2023 update**: the textbook line (PM + LoP + CJI) comes from *Anoop Baranwal (2023)*. Parliament's **2023 Act** later replaced the CJI with a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM - check your syllabus.
> - **Delimitation** of constituencies is actually done by the **Delimitation Commission**; textbook lists it under ECI functions.
