# 05 SPSC, JPSC & Tribunals

## State Public Service Commission

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Part & Articles? | \|\|Part XIV; Articles 315-323\|\| |
| Who appoints SPSC Chairman & members? | \|\|Governor\|\| |
| Term? | \|\|6 years or until age 62, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Article for removal & suspension? | \|\|Art. 317\|\| |
| Who removes? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Who suspends? | \|\|Governor\|\| |
| Salary & allowances charged on? | \|\|Consolidated Fund of the State\|\| |
| SPSC reports to? | \|\|Governor\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Conducting examinations for appointment to State Services\|\| |

## Joint Public Service Commission

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who establishes a JPSC? | \|\|Parliament, at the request of two or more States\|\| |
| Who appoints Chairman & members? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Reports to? | \|\|Governor (textbook)\|\| |
| Does any JPSC exist currently? | \|\|No - currently no JPSC exists in India\|\| |

## Restrictions on Chairman / Members

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| UPSC Chairman after retirement? | \|\|No appointment to Union or State Service\|\| |
| SPSC Chairman after retirement (textbook)? | \|\|Eligible for appointment to Union or State Service\|\| |
| Second term? | \|\|Chairman or member is not eligible for a second term in the same office\|\| |

## Tribunals

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Mentioned in the original Constitution? | \|\|No\|\| |
| Introduced by? | \|\|42nd Amendment (1976)\|\| |
| Part? | \|\|Part XIVA\|\| |
| Articles? | \|\|323A, 323B\|\| |
| Nature? | \|\|Quasi-judicial bodies\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Handle disputes in specific governmental departments (e.g. administrative, service, taxation)\|\| |

| Feature | UPSC | SPSC |
|---|---|---|
| Appointed by | President | Governor |
| Age limit | 65 | 62 |
| Term | 6 years | 6 years |
| Removal | President | President |
| Suspension | - | Governor |
| Reports to | President | Governor |
| Expenses from | CFI | State Consolidated Fund |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **SPSC**: appointed by **Governor**, removed by **President**, suspended by **Governor**.
> - **62 (SPSC)** vs **65 (UPSC)**.
> - **Tribunals = 42nd Amendment (1976), Part XIVA, Art. 323A & 323B**.
> - **Check**: JPSC members are appointed by the President and it reports to the President in standard books; textbook says Governor. Also standard books say an SPSC chairman is eligible only for UPSC chairman/member or another SPSC chairman - not any Union/State service.
