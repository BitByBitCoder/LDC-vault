# 06 CAG & Attorney General

## Comptroller & Auditor General of India (CAG)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article for CAG? | \|\|Art. 148\|\| |
| Who appoints the CAG? | \|\|President\|\| |
| 1st CAG? | \|\|V. Narahari Rao (1948)\|\| |
| Present CAG (textbook)? | \|\|K. Sanjay Murthy\|\| |
| Role of CAG? | \|\|Guardian of public finances\|\| |
| Audit function 1? | \|\|Audit of expenditure of the Consolidated Fund\|\| |
| Audit function 2? | \|\|Auditing Union & State expenditures & revenues\|\| |
| Salary & allowances determined by (textbook)? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Removal? | \|\|Same as a Supreme Court Judge\|\| |
| Tenure? | \|\|6 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Reappointment? | \|\|No reappointment\|\| |

## Attorney General of India

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Constitutional provision? | \|\|Part V; Article 76\|\| |
| Role? | \|\|1st Law Officer / Chief Legal Advisor\|\| |
| Rights in Parliament? | \|\|Speaks in Parliament but no voting\|\| |
| Private legal practice? | \|\|Not barred from private legal practice\|\| |
| Who appoints? | \|\|President under Art. 76(1)\|\| |
| 1st Attorney General? | \|\|M.C. Setalvad\|\| |
| Current Attorney General (textbook)? | \|\|R. Venkataramani\|\| |
| Salary & allowances determined by? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Qualification? | \|\|Eligible to be appointed as Judge of Supreme Court\|\| |

| Feature | CAG | Attorney General |
|---|---|---|
| Article | 148 | 76 (Part V) |
| Appointed by | President | President |
| Tenure | 6 yrs / age 65 | Not fixed |
| Removal | Like SC Judge | - |
| Reappointment | Not allowed | - |
| Speaks in Parliament | - | Yes, no vote |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **CAG = Art. 148**; **AG = Art. 76**.
> - CAG: **no reappointment** & removal **like SC Judge**.
> - AG: **speaks but cannot vote**; **can do private practice**; qualified as **SC Judge**.
> - **1st CAG: V. Narahari Rao (1948)**; **1st AG: M.C. Setalvad**.
> - Office-holders (CAG, AG, Chairpersons) change - confirm the current name close to your exam.
> - Standard books: CAG's salary is set by Parliament (Second Schedule); textbook says President.
