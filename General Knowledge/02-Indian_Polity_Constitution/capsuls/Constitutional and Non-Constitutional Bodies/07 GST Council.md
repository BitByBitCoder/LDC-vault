> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **279A / 101st / 2016**.
> - **Centre 1/3, States 2/3, decision 75%** - the Centre alone has a veto effect (1/3 > 25%).
> - Vice-Chairman is **elected by the members**, not appointed.
