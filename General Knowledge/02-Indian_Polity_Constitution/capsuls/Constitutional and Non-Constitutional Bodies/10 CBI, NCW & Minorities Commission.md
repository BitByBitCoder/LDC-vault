# 10 CBI, NCW & National Commission for Minorities

## Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Founded in? | \|\|1963, on recommendation of K. Santhanam Committee\|\| |
| Functions under? | \|\|Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946\|\| |
| Motto? | \|\|"Industry, Impartiality, and Integrity"\|\| |
| Under control of? | \|\|Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Pension & Public Grievances\|\| |
| Director appointed on recommendation of? | \|\|Selection Committee headed by PM\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Investigates corruption & specific crimes\|\| |
| CBI acts as in India? | \|\|"National Central Bureau" of Interpol\|\| |

| Composition? | \|\|1 Chairperson + 5 members + 1 member secretary\|\| |
| Appointed by? | \|\|Central Government\|\| |
| Headquarters? | \|\|New Delhi\|\| |
| Term of Chairperson? | \|\|3 years\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|To protect & advise on women's rights\|\| |
| 1st Chairperson? | \|\|Jayanti Patnaik\|\| |
| Current Chairperson (textbook)? | \|\|Vijaya Kishore Rahatkar\|\| |

## National Commission for Minorities (NCM)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Established in? | \|\|1978 (by resolution of Central Government)\|\| |
| Statutory status? | \|\|1992 (by the Commission for Minorities Act, 1992)\|\| |
| Composition? | \|\|1 Chairperson + 1 Vice Chairperson + 5 other members\|\| |
| Minority communities notified? | \|\|Jain, Buddhist, Muslim, Sikhs, Christians, Parsi\|\| |
| Year of notification? | \|\|1993 for Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Buddhist, Parsi; 2014 for Jain\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **CBI founded 1963** (Santhanam) but works under the **Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946**.
> - **CBI Director**: selection committee **headed by PM**.
> - **NCW**: 1992 (Act of **1990**), appointed by **Central Government**; **NCM**: 1978 resolution -> **1992 Act**.
> - **NCM minorities**: 6 communities; **Jain added in 2014**.
> - NCW = **5 members + 1 member secretary**; NCM = **5 members + Chair + Vice-chair**.
