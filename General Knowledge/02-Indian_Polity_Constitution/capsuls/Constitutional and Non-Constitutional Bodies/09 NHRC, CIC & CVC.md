# 09 NHRC, CIC & CVC

## National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Nature? | \|\|Statutory body under the Human Rights Protection Act, 1993\|\| |
| Established on? | \|\|12 October 1993\|\| |
| In effect from? | \|\|28 September 1993\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Protecting human rights in the country\|\| |
| Members? | \|\|Chairperson + 11 Members\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President (both Chairperson & Members)\|\| |
| Chairperson must be? | \|\|A former Chief Justice of India or Judge of Supreme Court\|\| |
| Chairperson tenure? | \|\|3 years or till age 70\|\| |
| Re-appointment? | \|\|Eligible for re-appointment but not eligible for further appointment in GoI or state\|\| |
| 1st Chairperson? | \|\|Ranganath Misra\|\| |
| Present Chairperson (textbook)? | \|\|Justice V. Ramasubramanian\|\| |
| International Human Rights Day? | \|\|10 December\|\| |

## Central Information Commission (CIC)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Nature? | \|\|Statutory body under Right to Information Act, 2005\|\| |
| Effective from? | \|\|12 October 2005\|\| |
| Composition? | \|\|1 CIC + up to 10 CICs (Information Commissioners)\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Tenure? | \|\|3 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier (determined by Central Government)\|\| |

## Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Established in? | \|\|1964\|\| |
| Recommended by? | \|\|K. Santhanam Committee on prevention of corruption\|\| |
| Statutory status given in? | \|\|2003 through an Act passed by Parliament\|\| |
| In 2004? | \|\|Designated as Central Agency by the Central Government\|\| |
| Composition? | \|\|Multimember body: 1 Chairman + two or less than two Vigilance Commissioners\|\| |
| Appointment? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Selection committee (3 members)? | \|\|PM, Leader of Opposition, Union Home Minister\|\| |
| Tenure? | \|\|4 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Function? | \|\|Investigation of offences related to corruption\|\| |
| Further appointment? | \|\|Not eligible for further appointment\|\| |
| Nature of CVC? | \|\|Apex vigilance body, but advisory in nature (cannot directly prosecute)\|\| |

| Feature | NHRC | CIC | CVC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Act | Human Rights Protection Act 1993 | RTI Act 2005 | Act of 2003 (est. 1964) |
| Members | Chairperson + 11 | 1 CIC + up to 10 | 1 Chairman + up to 2 VCs |
| Tenure | 3 yrs / age 70 | 3 yrs / age 65 | 4 yrs / age 65 |
| Appointed by | President | President | President |
| First head | Ranganath Misra | Wajahat Habibullah | - |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Tenure**: NHRC **3 / 70**, CIC **3 / 65**, CVC **4 / 65** - the three most swapped numbers.
> - **CVC and CBI** are both linked to the **K. Santhanam Committee** (CVC 1964, CBI 1963).
> - **CVC is advisory** - it cannot prosecute.
> - **NHRC Chairperson** = former **CJI / SC Judge**; NHRC dates: **established 12 Oct 1993**, **in effect 28 Sept 1993**.
> - Textbook says "CIC + up to 10 CICs" - the 10 are Information Commissioners.
