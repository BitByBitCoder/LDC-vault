# 04 Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

## Basics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which Part & Articles? | \|\|Part XIV; Articles 315-323\|\| |
| 3 types of Public Service Commissions? | \|\|UPSC (Union), SPSC (State), JPSC (two or more States)\|\| |
| All India Civil Services (only 3)? | \|\|IAS, IPS, IFoS\|\| |
| Which was added in 1966? | \|\|Indian Forest Service (IFoS)\|\| |
| Father of Civil Services in India? | \|\|Lord Cornwallis\|\| |
| 1st Indian ICS? | \|\|Satyendra Nath Tagore\|\| |
| 1st Civil Services Examination held in? | \|\|London & Allahabad in 1922\|\| |
| 1st UPSC Chairman? | \|\|H.K. Kriplani (1950)\|\| |
| Civil Services Day? | \|\|21 April\|\| |
| Public Service Commission established? | \|\|1926\|\| |
| Recommended by? | \|\|Lee Commission\|\| |

## Qualification, Appointment, Tenure

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Number of members & conditions of service decided by? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Half of the members must have served at least? | \|\|10 years under Central or State Government at time of appointment\|\| |
| The other half? | \|\|From other areas\|\| |
| Salary & allowances from? | \|\|Consolidated Fund of India\|\| |
| Article for appointment & tenure? | \|\|Art. 316\|\| |
| Who appoints Chairman & Members? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Term? | \|\|6 years or 65 years of age, whichever is earlier\|\| |
| Removal of Chairperson? | \|\|By President\|\| |
| Removal of Chairperson & members? | \|\|By President, after Supreme Court inquiry\|\| |
| If Chairman post is vacant? | \|\|Acting Chairman\|\| |
| Resignation? | \|\|By writing to the President of India\|\| |

## Functions & Post-tenure

| Question                     | Answer                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Art. 320?                    | \|\|Conduct recruitment examinations for Union Services\|\|                    |
| Art. 321?                    | \|\|Power of expansion of work area - Parliament\|\|                           |
| Art. 322?                    | \|\|All expenses charged from Consolidated Fund of India\|\|                   |
| Annual Report submitted to?  | \|\|President\|\|                                                              |
| Post-tenure - Chairman?      | \|\|Not eligible for any further employment\|\|                                |
| Post-tenure - other members? | \|\|Eligible as chairman of UPSC or a State Public Service Commission only\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **UPSC term: 6 yrs / age 65**; **SPSC: 6 yrs / age 62**.
> - **Art. 316** appointment & tenure; **317** removal; **320** functions; **321** expansion; **322** expenses.
> - UPSC Chairman: no further employment. Members: only UPSC/SPSC chairman.
> - Removal needs **Supreme Court inquiry** (President removes).
