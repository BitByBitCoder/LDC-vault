## 3-Tier (Balwant Rai Mehta) vs 2-Tier (Ashok Mehta)

| Level | Balwant Rai Mehta (3-Tier) | Ashok Mehta (2-Tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Village | Village Panchayat | Mandala Panchayat (populations of 15,000-20,000) |
| Block | Panchayat Samiti | - |
| District | Zila Panchayat | Zila Parishad / District Councils |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Balwant Rai Mehta (1957)** = architect, **3-tier** (Village / Samiti-Block / Zila-District).
> - **Ashok Mehta (1977-78)** = **2-tier**: Mandala Panchayat (15,000-20,000) + Zila Parishad.
> - **L.M. Singhvi (1986)** = constitutional status + **Nyaya Panchayats**.
> - **Thungan (1988-89)** = timely elections + 5-year tenure; **Gadgil (1988)** = direct election to all 3 tiers.
> - **Nagaur, Rajasthan, 2 Oct 1959**, by Nehru; **Lord Ripon, 1882** = Father of Local Self Government.
