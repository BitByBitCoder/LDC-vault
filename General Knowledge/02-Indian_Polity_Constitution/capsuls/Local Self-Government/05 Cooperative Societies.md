# 05 Cooperative Societies

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which Amendment gave Constitutional status to Cooperative Societies? | \|\|97th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2011\|\| |
| Part? | \|\|Part IX(B)\|\| |
| Articles? | \|\|Articles 243(ZH)-243(ZT)\|\| |
| Constituted through which Fundamental Right? | \|\|Art. 19(c) - right to form associations/unions\|\| |

| Amendment | Subject | Part | Articles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 73rd (1992) | Panchayats | IX | 243-243(O) |
| 74th (1992) | Municipalities | IX-A | 243(P)-243(ZG) |
| 97th (2011) | Cooperative Societies | IX-B | 243(ZH)-243(ZT) |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **97th Amendment (2011)** = Cooperative Societies, **Part IX-B**, **243(ZH)-243(ZT)**.
> - Cooperative societies are tied to **Art. 19(c)** (formation of associations/unions).
> - **Part IX / IX-A / IX-B** = Panchayats / Municipalities / Cooperatives.
