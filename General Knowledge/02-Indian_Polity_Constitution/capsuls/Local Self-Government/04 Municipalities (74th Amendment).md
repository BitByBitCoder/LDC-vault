> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **74th = Part IX-A, Art. 243P-243ZG, 12th Schedule (18 subjects), effective 1 June 1993**.
> - **Population**: Nagar Panchayat **10,000-20,000**; Council **20,000-3 lakh**; Corporation **above 3 lakh**.
> - **Ward Committees (243S)** = population **over 3 lakh**.
> - **243ZD = District Planning Committee**; **243ZE = Metropolitan Planning Committee (1 million+)**.
> - **Cantonment Boards** = **Ministry of Defence**.
> - **73rd = 11th Schedule (29)**; **74th = 12th Schedule (18)**.
