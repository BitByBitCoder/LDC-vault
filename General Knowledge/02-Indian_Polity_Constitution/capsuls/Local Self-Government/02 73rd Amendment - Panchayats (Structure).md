# 02 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 - Panchayat Structure

## Basics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which PM? | \|\|P.V. Narasimha Rao\|\| |
| Took effect on? | \|\|24 April 1993\|\| |
| Celebrated as? | \|\|Panchayati Raj Diwas (beginning 2010)\|\| |
| Part & Articles? | \|\|Part IX; Articles 243-243(O)\|\| |
| Schedule added? | \|\|11th Schedule - 29 subjects\|\| |

## Gram Sabha - Art. 243(A)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who forms the Gram Sabha? | \|\|All registered voters in a village according to electoral rolls\|\| |
| Does it include groups of villages? | \|\|Yes - includes village or group of villages\|\| |
| Acts on? | \|\|Powers & duties as delegated under State Legislation\|\| |

## Formation - Art. 243(B)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Three tiers in every State? | \|\|Village Panchayat (Village level), Block / Janpad Panchayat (Intermediate level), District / Zila Panchayat (District level)\|\| |
| States with population below 20 lakhs? | \|\|No need to form Kshetra (intermediate) Panchayat\|\| |
| Which State has a 4-tier system? | \|\|West Bengal\|\| |

## Composition - Art. 243(C)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who frames laws for Panchayat formation? | \|\|State Legislatures\|\| |
| Gram Panchayat election? | \|\|Direct (Mukhiya)\|\| |
| Panchayat Samiti election? | \|\|Indirect (Chairman / Block Pramukh)\|\| |
| District Panchayat election? | \|\|Indirect (Chairperson)\|\| |
| States with no Panchayats? | \|\|Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland - Tribal councils exist\|\| |

## Reservation - Art. 243(D)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| SC/ST seats? | \|\|Reserved in proportion to population\|\| |
| Women's reservation? | \|\|1/3rd for women\|\| |
| Women's seats include? | \|\|1/3rd seats for all categories of women, including SC/ST\|\| |
| Allocation of reserved seats? | \|\|Based on rotation\|\| |
| Panchayat presidents? | \|\|Subject to the same reservation norms\|\| |
| 2009 change? | \|\|Central Government allowed 50% reservation for women in Panchayats\|\| |
| First implemented in? | \|\|Bihar\|\| |
| Now extended to? | \|\|21 States\|\| |

## Term - Art. 243E

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Term? | \|\|5 years\|\| |
| Premature dissolution with more than 6 months remaining? | \|\|Election within 6 months\|\| |
| Minimum age for Panchayat election? | \|\|21 years\|\| |

| Tier | Election | Head |
|---|---|---|
| Gram Panchayat | Direct | Mukhiya |
| Panchayat Samiti | Indirect | Chairman / Block Pramukh |
| District Panchayat | Indirect | Chairperson |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **73rd = Part IX, Art. 243-243(O), 11th Schedule (29 subjects), effective 24 April 1993** (Panchayati Raj Diwas since 2010).
> - **243A = Gram Sabha; 243B = formation; 243C = composition; 243D = reservation; 243E = term (5 yrs)**.
> - **No Panchayats**: **Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland**.
> - **Min age 21**; **election within 6 months** if more than 6 months remain.
> - **Gram = direct**; **Samiti & District = indirect**.
> - **2009**: 50% women's reservation, **first Bihar**, now **21 States**.
> - **West Bengal = 4-tier**.
