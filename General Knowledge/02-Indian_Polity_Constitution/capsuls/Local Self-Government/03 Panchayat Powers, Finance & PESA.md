# 03 Panchayat Powers, Finance & Other Provisions

## Power & Finance

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Art. 243G? | \|\|To empower Panchayats to function as institutions of self-government - authorised by State Legislature\|\| |
| Art. 243H? | \|\|Financial Provisions - authorised by State Legislature to collect taxes, tolls & fees\|\| |
| Art. 243I? | \|\|State Finance Commission - constituted by Governor every 5 years to review Panchayat finances\|\| |
| Art. 243J? | \|\|Audit Provisions - State Legislatures may legislate for auditing Panchayat accounts\|\| |
| Art. 243K? | \|\|State Election Commission - appointed by the Governor; conducts Panchayat elections\|\| |

## Article Map (Panchayats)

| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 243G | Powers & responsibilities |
| 243H | Financial provisions (taxes, tolls, fees) |
| 243I | State Finance Commission (Governor, every 5 years) |
| 243J | Audit of accounts |
| 243K | State Election Commission (appointed by Governor) |
| 243L | Applicability to Union Territories |
| 243M | Non-application in certain areas |
| 243N | Continuance of existing laws |
| 243O | Bar on court interference in elections |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **243I = SFC (5 years)**; **243K = SEC (Governor appoints)**.
> - **243G = powers; 243H = taxes; 243J = audit**.
> - **243L = UTs; 243M = exclusions (tribal areas in North-East); 243O = bar on courts**.
> - **Nyaya Panchayats**: fine **Rs 50-1000**; recommended by **L.M. Singhvi**.
> - **PESA 1996**: **5th Schedule areas**, **Bhuria Commission**, **10 States**.
> - **Mizoram falls under the 6th Schedule**, not PESA's 5th-Schedule areas.
