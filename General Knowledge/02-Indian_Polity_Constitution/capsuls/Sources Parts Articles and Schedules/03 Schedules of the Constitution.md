# 03 Schedules of the Constitution

## Overview

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Schedules in the original Constitution? | \|\|8\|\| |
| Schedules presently? | \|\|12\|\| |

## 12 Schedules

| Schedule | Subject |
|---|---|
| 1st | \|\|Union & State Territories (28 States, 8 UTs)\|\| |
| 2nd | \|\|Salaries & Allowances and Privileges (President, VP, Judges, CAG, etc.)\|\| |
| 3rd | \|\|Oaths & Affirmations (Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Judges, CAG)\|\| |
| 4th | \|\|Allocation of Rajya Sabha's Seats\|\| |
| 5th | \|\|Administration of Scheduled Areas & Tribes\|\| |
| 6th | \|\|Tribal Area Administration in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura & Mizoram\|\| |
| 7th | \|\|Union-State Relations (Union, State & Concurrent Lists)\|\| |
| 8th | \|\|Recognized Languages (originally 14, now 22)\|\| |
| 9th | \|\|Land Reforms (added by 1st Amendment, 1951)\|\| |
| 10th | \|\|Anti-defection provisions (added by 52nd Amendment, 1985)\|\| |
| 11th | \|\|29 subjects of Panchayats (added by 73rd Amendment, 1992)\|\| |
| 12th | \|\|18 subjects of Municipalities (added by 74th Amendment, 1992)\|\| |

## Trick: "TEARS OF OLD PM"

| Letter | Schedule | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| T | \|\|1st\|\| | Territories (Union & States) |
| E | \|\|2nd\|\| | Emoluments (Salaries) |
| A | \|\|3rd\|\| | Affirmation & Oath |
| R | \|\|4th\|\| | Rajya Sabha Seats |
| S | \|\|5th\|\| | Scheduled Areas & Tribes |
| O | \|\|6th\|\| | Other Tribal Areas |
| F | \|\|7th\|\| | Federal List (Lists of Powers) |
| O | \|\|8th\|\| | Official Languages |
| L | \|\|9th\|\| | Land Reforms |
| D | \|\|10th\|\| | Defection |
| P | \|\|11th\|\| | Panchayats |
| M | \|\|12th\|\| | Municipalities |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Original = 8; now = 12**; added: **9th (1951), 10th (1985), 11th (1992), 12th (1992)**.
> - **5th = Scheduled Areas & Tribes**; **6th = Tribal areas of Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura & Mizoram** (Mizoram is in the 6th, not the 5th).
> - **3rd = oaths**; **2nd = salaries/emoluments**; **4th = RS seats**.
> - **11th = Panchayats (73rd)**; **12th = Municipalities (74th)** - both 1992.
> - **9th Schedule** laws became **challengeable in court from 1973 onwards** (Kesavananda era).
> - **TEARS OF OLD PM** = T-E-A-R-S-O-F-O-L-D-P-M (12 letters = 12 schedules).
