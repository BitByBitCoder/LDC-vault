## 8th Schedule - Recognized Languages

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Languages originally? | \|\|14\|\| |
| Languages now? | \|\|22\|\| |
| 21st Amendment, 1967 added? | \|\|Sindhi\|\| |
| 71st Amendment, 1992 added? | \|\|Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali\|\| |
| 92nd Amendment, 2003 added? | \|\|Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali\|\| |

| Amendment | Year | Languages added | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original | 1950 | - | 14 |
| 21st | 1967 | Sindhi | 1 |
| 71st | 1992 | Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali | 3 |
| 92nd | 2003 | Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali | 4 |
| Total | - | - | 22 |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **14 + 1 + 3 + 4 = 22**.
> - **Sindhi = 21st (1967)**; **Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali = 71st (1992)**; **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali = 92nd (2003)**.
> - **Concurrent List** examples: Forests, Education, Marriage, Succession, Population Control.
> - **Police, Health, Agriculture, Trade** = **State List**.
> - **Banking, Defence, Foreign Affairs, Communication, Railways, Inter-State Trade** = **Union List**.
