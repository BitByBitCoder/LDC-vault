> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Part 9 / 9A / 9B** = Panchayats / Municipalities / Cooperative Societies.
> - **Part 14 = Services; Part 14A = Tribunals; Part 15 = Elections; Part 20 = Amendment (Art. 368).**
> - **Part 4A = Art. 51A only (Fundamental Duties).**
> - **Part 7 = repealed (Art. 238).**
> - **Part 12** ends at **Art. 300(A)** (Right to Property, legal right).
> - **Part 18 = Emergency (Art. 352-360)**; **Part 17 = Official Language (Art. 343-351)**.
