> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **6 rights** now (7 initially); property -> **Art. 300A**, **44th Amendment (1978)**.
> - **Art. 12 = State; Art. 13 = law violating FR is void.**
> - **Citizens only**: 15, 16, 19, 29, 30. **Everyone (incl. foreigners)**: 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22-28.
> - **Art. 32 is the 6th right** - Art. 31 is not in the list (property removed).
> - Mnemonic order: **E**very **F**ool **E**ats **R**ipe **C**ustard **R**egularly = Equality, Freedom, Exploitation, Religion, Cultural, Remedies.
