# 05 Right to Constitutional Remedies, Writs & Art. 33-35

## Article 32 & 226

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article 32? | \|\|Right to Constitutional Remedies\|\| |
| Art. 32 called? | \|\|"Heart & Soul of the Constitution" - Dr. B.R. Ambedkar\|\| |
| What does it provide? | \|\|The guarantee of protection of fundamental rights\|\| |
| Remedy under Art. 32? | \|\|Supreme Court (only for Fundamental Rights)\|\| |
| Remedy under Art. 226? | \|\|High Courts (for Fundamental + Legal Rights)\|\| |
| Which has wider jurisdiction? | \|\|High Courts (Art. 226)\|\| |

## Five Writs

| Writ | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Habeas Corpus | \|\|"To have the body of"\|\| | \|\|Issued to release a person unlawfully detained\|\| |
| Mandamus | \|\|"We command"\|\| | \|\|Issued to direct a public official to perform a duty if failed to do\|\| |
| Quo-Warranto | \|\|"By what authority"\|\| | \|\|Issued to challenge a person's right to hold a public office\|\| |
| Certiorari | \|\|"To be certified"\|\| | \|\|To quash an order of a lower court\|\| |
| Prohibition | \|\|"To forbid"\|\| | \|\|To stop lower court from exceeding its jurisdiction\|\| |

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Hierarchy of jurisdiction? | \|\|Supreme Court -> High Courts -> Subordinate Courts\|\| |
| How many types of writs? | \|\|5\|\| |

## Articles 33-35

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article 33? | \|\|Parliament's power to restrict rights of armed forces\|\| |
| Article 34? | \|\|Restrictions during Martial Law - FRs can be restricted\|\| |
| Article 35? | \|\|Parliament's power to make laws for enforcement of certain fundamental rights\|\| |

| Feature | Art. 32 (Supreme Court) | Art. 226 (High Courts) |
|---|---|---|
| Rights covered | Fundamental Rights only | Fundamental + Legal Rights |
| Jurisdiction | Narrower | Wider |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Art. 32 = SC (FR only)**; **Art. 226 = HC (FR + legal rights, wider)**.
> - **Habeas Corpus = release**; **Mandamus = direct a duty**; **Quo-Warranto = right to hold office**; **Certiorari = quash**; **Prohibition = stop exceeding jurisdiction**.
> - **Certiorari quashes** an order already passed; **Prohibition prevents** it before.
> - **33 = armed forces**; **34 = martial law**; **35 = enforcement laws**.
> - **"Heart & Soul"** of the Constitution = **Art. 32** (Ambedkar).
