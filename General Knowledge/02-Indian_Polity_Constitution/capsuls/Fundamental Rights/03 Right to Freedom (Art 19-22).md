# 03 Right to Freedom (Art. 19-22)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|

| Protection 3? | \|\|No Self-incrimination\|\| |

## Article 22 - Protection against Arrest & Detention

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Right 1? | \|\|Right to be informed of the reason for arrest\|\| |
| Right 2? | \|\|To be presented before magistrate within 24 hours\|\| |
| Right 3? | \|\|To consult a lawyer of one's choice\|\| |

| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 19 | 6 Freedoms |
| 20 | Protection in respect of conviction |
| 21 | Life & personal liberty |
| 21A | Free & compulsory education (6-14) |
| 22 | Protection against arrest & detention |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Art. 20 (3 protections)**: existing law, **no double jeopardy**, **no self-incrimination**.
> - **Art. 21A**: age **6-14**, **86th Amendment (2002)**, **RTE Act 2009**, enforced **2010**.
> - **Art. 22**: **24 hours** before magistrate; **lawyer of choice**.
> - **Maneka Gandhi = travel abroad**; **Olga Tellis = livelihood**.
> - **Art. 19** is for **citizens only**; **Art. 20, 21, 21A, 22** are for **everyone**.
> - Art. 24 (children below 14) and Art. 21A (6-14) both use age 14 - do not mix.
