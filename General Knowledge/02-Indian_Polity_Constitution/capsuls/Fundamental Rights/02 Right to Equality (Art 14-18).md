> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **15(1) = State**; **15(2) = private entities** (shops, hotels, roads).
> - **EWS = 15(6) & 16(6)**, **103rd CAA 2019**, **10%**.
> - **Art. 15 grounds**: religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth. **Art. 16 adds**: descent, residence.
> - **Art. 17**: Untouchability not defined; Act = **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955**.
> - **50% ceiling** cases: **Balaji (1963)** and **Indra Sawhney (1992)**; Mandal Commission = **1979**.
> - **Check**: standard books say the 50% cap applies to total reservations; Indra Sawhney also barred reservation in promotions (later overridden by the 77th Amendment). Learn the textbook line for your exam.
