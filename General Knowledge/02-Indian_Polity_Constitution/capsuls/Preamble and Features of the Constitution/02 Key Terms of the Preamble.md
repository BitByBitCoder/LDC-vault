# 02 Key Terms of the Preamble

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sovereign | \|\|Free to take internal & external decisions without any external influence\|\| |
| Socialist | \|\|Close to democratic socialism; mixed economy (co-existence of public + private sectors)\|\| |
| Secular | \|\|No state religion; equal respect for all religions\|\| |
| Justice | \|\|Social, Political, Economic\|\| |
| Democratic | \|\|Power lies with the people; ruled by elected representatives (Parliamentary System)\|\| |
| Republic | \|\|Head of the State is elected, not hereditary\|\| |

## Democracy Types

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Direct Democracy? | \|\|People directly participate (e.g. Switzerland)\|\| |
| Indirect Democracy? | \|\|People elect representatives (e.g. India)\|\| |

## Republic

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Head of the State in India? | \|\|President of India (elected, not hereditary)\|\| |
| How does the President rule? | \|\|With the aid and advice of the Council of Ministers\|\| |
| Socialism in India is closer to? | \|\|Democratic socialism (mixed economy)\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Switzerland = direct democracy**; **India = indirect (representative)**.
> - **Socialist** = **democratic socialism / mixed economy**, not state ownership of everything.
> - **Secular** = **no state religion, equal respect** (not anti-religion).
> - **Republic** = head of State **elected, not hereditary**; President acts on **aid and advice of Council of Ministers**.
