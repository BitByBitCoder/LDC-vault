# 03 Landmark Cases on the Preamble

| Case | Year | Held |
|---|---|---|
| Berubari Union | \|\|1960\|\| | \|\|Preamble is not a part of the Constitution; cannot be amended\|\| |
| Sajjan Singh case | \|\|1965\|\| | \|\|Suggested reconsideration\|\| |
| Golaknath case | \|\|1967\|\| | \|\|The basic spirit of the Constitution is eternal & non-negotiable\|\| |
| Kesavananda Bharati case | \|\|1973\|\| | \|\|Preamble is part of the Constitution; can be amended but not its basic structure\|\| |
| S.R. Bommai case | \|\|1994\|\| | \|\|Integral Part\|\| |
| LIC v. Union of India | \|\|1995\|\| | \|\|Integral Part; but not directly applicable in court\|\| |

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Case that said Preamble is NOT part of the Constitution? | \|\|Berubari Union (1960)\|\| |
| Case that said Preamble IS part of the Constitution? | \|\|Kesavananda Bharati (1973)\|\| |
| Case that suggested reconsideration? | \|\|Sajjan Singh (1965)\|\| |
| Case: "basic spirit eternal & non-negotiable"? | \|\|Golaknath (1967)\|\| |
| Cases calling Preamble an integral part? | \|\|S.R. Bommai (1994) and LIC v. Union of India (1995)\|\| |
| Is the Preamble directly applicable in court? | \|\|No (LIC v. Union of India, 1995)\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Berubari (1960)**: NOT a part, cannot be amended. **Kesavananda (1973)**: IS a part, amendable but **not basic structure**.
> - **Direction of change**: 1960 (not part) -> 1973 (part) -> 1994/1995 (integral part).
> - **LIC (1995)**: integral part **but not directly enforceable** in court.
> - Year order: **1960, 1965, 1967, 1973, 1994, 1995**.
