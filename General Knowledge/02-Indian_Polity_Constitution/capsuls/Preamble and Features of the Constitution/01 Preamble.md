# 01 Preamble

## Basics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is the Preamble? | \|\|Introduction / Preface of the Constitution\|\| |
| What does it mention? | \|\|Main elements of Constitution (ideals, objectives, structure, source, date)\|\| |
| What else does it mention? | \|\|The essence of the Constitution\|\| |
| Source of the Preamble idea? | \|\|American Constitution\|\| |
| Language of the Preamble adopted from? | \|\|Australian Constitution\|\| |
| Inspiration for the Preamble? | \|\|Objective Resolution (proposed by J.L. Nehru on 13 December 1946)\|\| |
| Adopted by Constituent Assembly on? | \|\|26 November 1949\|\| |
| Is the Preamble justiciable? | \|\|Non-Justiciable (cannot be enforced in court)\|\| |
| How many times amended? | \|\|Only once - 42nd Amendment (1976)\|\| |
| Words added by the 42nd Amendment? | \|\|Socialist, Secular & Integrity\|\| |
| Effective from? | \|\|3 January 1977\|\| |
| Original words before amendment? | \|\|"Sovereign Democratic Republic" and "Unity of the Nation"\|\| |
| Does the Preamble give power to Government? | \|\|Neither provides power to Government nor acts as a hurdle to it\|\| |

## Preamble Text (Key Lines)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| India is constituted into a? | \|\|Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic\|\| |
| Justice? | \|\|Social, economic and political\|\| |
| Liberty of? | \|\|Thought, expression, belief, faith and worship\|\| |
| Equality of? | \|\|Status and of opportunity\|\| |
| Fraternity assures? | \|\|Dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation\|\| |
| Date mentioned in the Preamble? | \|\|26 November 1949\|\| |

## Elements of the Preamble

| Element | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Source | \|\|We, the people of India\|\| | - |
| Justice | \|\|Social, Economic & Political\|\| | Inspired by 1917 Russian Revolution |
| Liberty | \|\|Thought, Expression, Belief, Faith, Worship\|\| | Inspired by the French Revolution |
| Equality | \|\|No special privileges, everyone equal before the Law\|\| | - |
| Fraternity | \|\|Brotherhood\|\| | - |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Amended once**: **42nd Amendment (1976)** added **Socialist, Secular, Integrity**.
> - **Source** of Preamble = **American Constitution**; **Language** = **Australian Constitution**.
> - **Objective Resolution**: **Nehru, 13 Dec 1946**; Preamble **adopted 26 Nov 1949**.
> - **Justice -> Russian Revolution (1917)**; **Liberty -> French Revolution**.
> - **Non-justiciable**: neither a source of power nor a limit on it.
> - Experts: **Palkhivala = identity card**; **Munshi = horoscope**; **Baker = key**; **Ayyar = long-term dreams**; **Bhargava Das = soul**.
