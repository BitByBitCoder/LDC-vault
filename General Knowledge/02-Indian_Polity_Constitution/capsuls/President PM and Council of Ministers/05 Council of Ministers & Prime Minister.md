# 05 Union Council of Ministers & Prime Minister

## Council of Ministers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Three kinds of ministers? | \|\|Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State, Deputy Ministers\|\| |
| Advice of the Council to the President? | \|\|Not binding for the first time but binding on second time\|\| |
| 44th Amendment? | \|\|President cannot act without the advice of the Cabinet\|\| |
| Responsible to? | \|\|Lok Sabha\|\| |
| After losing confidence of Lok Sabha? | \|\|Resign collectively\|\| |
| Maximum number of ministers? | \|\|15% of Lok Sabha members including Prime Minister (91st Constitutional Amendment)\|\| |
| Term? | \|\|5 years or until Lok Sabha is dissolved\|\| |
| Salaries paid from? | \|\|Consolidated Fund of India [Art. 75(6)]\|\| |

## Eligibility Criteria

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Citizenship? | \|\|Must be a citizen of India\|\| |
| Minimum age for Lok Sabha? | \|\|25 years or more\|\| |
| Minimum age for Rajya Sabha? | \|\|30 years or more\|\| |
| Membership? | \|\|Must be a Member of Parliament (if not, must become within 6 months)\|\| |

## Appointment / Oath

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who appoints the PM? | \|\|President\|\| |
| PM gives resignation to? | \|\|President\|\| |
| Other ministers appointed by? | \|\|President on PM's advice\|\| |

| Article | Subject |

| 75(2) | \|\|Ministers hold office during the pleasure of President\|\| |
| 77 | \|\|Conduct of Govt. Business in the name of President\|\| |

## Prime Minister's Role

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| PM is head of? | \|\|Council of Ministers\|\| |
| PM is Leader of? | \|\|The House (Lok Sabha)\|\| |
| PM is a key figure in? | \|\|Parliamentary Democracy\|\| |
| PM is Chairperson of? | \|\|NITI Aayog, National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), National Integration Council, Indian Board of Wildlife\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Max ministers = 15% of Lok Sabha incl. PM** (**91st CAA**).
> - **Advice**: not binding the **first** time, **binding the second** time (**44th Amendment**).
> - **Responsible to Lok Sabha**; **collective resignation** on losing confidence.
> - **Art. 74 = CoM advise President; 75 = appointment/tenure; 77 = business in President's name; 78 = PM's duty to inform President**.
> - **Minister not an MP**: must become one within **6 months**.
> - **PM chairs**: **NITI Aayog, NDMA, National Integration Council, Indian Board of Wildlife**.
