> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **V.V. Giri** appears twice: **acting (1969)** then **elected (1969-74, first Independent)**.
> - **Hidayatullah** = **acting**, 20 July - 24 Aug 1969; **B.D. Jatti** = **acting 1977**.
> - **Zakir Hussain (1969)** = first to die in office; **Fakhruddin (1977)** = second.
> - **Giani Zail Singh** = **pocket veto** (India Post Office Amendment Bill).
> - **Check**: K.R. Narayanan is more commonly remembered as the **first Dalit President**; the textbook lists "oldest President to be elected" against his row. Learn it as the textbook says, but cross-check.
