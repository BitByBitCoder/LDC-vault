# 01 President - Basics, Eligibility & Election

## Basics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| President is the? | \|\|Head of the State (Chief of State)\|\| |
| President is called? | \|\|First Citizen of India\|\| |
| President is? | \|\|Supreme Commander of Armed Forces\|\| |
| President is Chancellor of? | \|\|Central Universities\|\| |
| President holds? | \|\|All Executive Powers (Nominal Executive)\|\| |
| Real Authority lies with? | \|\|Prime Minister + Council of Ministers\|\| |

## Eligibility Criteria (Art. 58)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Citizenship? | \|\|Must be a citizen of India\|\| |
| Minimum age? | \|\|35 years\|\| |
| Other qualification? | \|\|Must qualify the eligibility of a Lok Sabha member\|\| |
| Office of profit? | \|\|Must not hold any office of profit\|\| |

## Election Procedure (Art. 54-55)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Type of election? | \|\|Indirect Election by Electoral College\|\| |
| System? | \|\|Proportional Representation system, Single Transferable Vote\|\| |
| Voting? | \|\|Secret Ballot\|\| |
| Security deposit? | \|\|Rs 15,000\|\| |
| Proposers and seconders? | \|\|50 Proposers + 50 Seconders\|\| |
| Dispute resolution? | \|\|Supreme Court (Article 71)\|\| |

## Electoral College

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who forms the Electoral College? | \|\|Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha + Legislature of State or UT (Delhi and Puducherry)\|\| |
| Who has the right to vote? | \|\|Elected MPs and MLAs\|\| |
| Who has no right to vote? | \|\|Legislative Council + Nominated Members\|\| |

## Oath, Resignation, Term, Salary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Oath administered by? | \|\|Chief Justice of India (or senior-most judge)\|\| |
| Resignation to? | \|\|Vice-President\|\| |
| Term of office? | \|\|5 years (can resign, die in office, or be impeached)\|\| |
| Presidential salary? | \|\|Rs 5 Lakh / month (from Consolidated Fund of India)\|\| |
| Conditions of office? | \|\|Not hold any office of profit under the Union or State Government\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Age 35**; same eligibility as **Lok Sabha member**; **no office of profit**.
> - **Election**: indirect, **STV**, secret ballot; **deposit Rs 15,000**; **50 proposers + 50 seconders**.
> - **Disputes** go to the **Supreme Court (Art. 71)**.
> - **Electoral college**: **elected** MPs + MLAs (incl. **Delhi & Puducherry**); **nominated MPs and Legislative Council members cannot vote**.
> - **Oath by CJI**; **resignation to Vice-President**; **salary Rs 5 lakh/month**.
