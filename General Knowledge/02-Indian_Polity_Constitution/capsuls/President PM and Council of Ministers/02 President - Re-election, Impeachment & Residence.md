# 02 President - Re-election, Impeachment & Residence

## Re-election

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How many times can a President be re-elected? | \|\|Any number of times (no limit)\|\| |
| Longest tenure? | \|\|Dr. Rajendra Prasad (elected twice)\|\| |
| USA limit? | \|\|Maximum 2 times\|\| |
| Naturalized citizen in USA? | \|\|Not eligible to be Presidential candidate\|\| |
| Naturalized citizen in India? | \|\|Also eligible\|\| |

## Impeachment Procedure (Art. 61)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Article? | \|\|Art. 61\|\| |
| Nature of process? | \|\|Quasi-judicial process\|\| |
| Notice? | \|\|Any House (Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha) gives 14 days' prior notice\|\| |
| Majority? | \|\|Special Majority in both Houses (not less than 2/3rd of the total membership of the House)\|\| |
| Result? | \|\|If passed in both Houses, the President stands removed from office\|\| |

## Who Votes?

| Voted by | Allowed in Impeachment | Allowed in Election |
|---|---|---|
| MPs (Elected) | \|\|Yes\|\| | \|\|Yes\|\| |
| MLAs | \|\|No\|\| | \|\|Yes\|\| |
| Nominated MPs | \|\|Yes\|\| | \|\|No\|\| |

> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **MLAs vote in election but NOT in impeachment**; **Nominated MPs vote in impeachment but NOT in election**.
> - **14 days' notice**; **2/3rd of total membership** in **both Houses**.
> - **No limit** on re-election (USA = 2 terms); **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** = twice elected.
> - **Rashtrapati Bhawan**: **Edwin Lutyens**, formerly **Viceroy's House**.
> - **Renamed**: Mughal Garden -> **Amrit Udyan**; Durbar Hall -> **Gantantra Mandap**; Ashoka Hall -> **Ashok Mandap**.
