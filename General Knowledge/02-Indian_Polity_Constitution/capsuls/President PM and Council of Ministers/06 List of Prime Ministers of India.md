> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Nanda acted twice**: after **Nehru (1964)** and after **Shastri (1966)**.
> - **Indira Gandhi** served **two stints**: **1966-77** and **1980-84**.
> - **Vajpayee** served **two stints** too: **1996 (13 days)** and **1998-2004**.
> - **VP Singh** = no-confidence; **Chandra Shekhar** = Congress withdrew support.
> - **Rao = South India**; **Deve Gowda = RS + Karnataka CM**; **Manmohan Singh = RS + first Sikh**.
> - The right-side list is "as of July 2025" in the textbook.
