# 03 President - Powers & Important Articles

## Rights & Duties of President

| Power | Detail |
|---|---|
| Executive | \|\|Appoints Prime Minister & Council of Ministers, Governors, Judges of SC & HCs, Chairpersons of Commissions, Diplomats\|\| |
| Judicial | \|\|Grants Pardon, Reprieve, Respite, Remission (Art. 72)\|\| |
| Legislative | \|\|Summon & prorogue Parliament; Dissolution of Lok Sabha; Assent to bills; Issue Ordinances\|\| |
| Emergency | \|\|Declare National Emergency (Art. 352); Impose President's Rule in States (Art. 356); Financial Emergency (Art. 360)\|\| |
