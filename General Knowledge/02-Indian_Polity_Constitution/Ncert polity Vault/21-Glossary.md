# Glossary of Constitutional Terms

| Term                        | Meaning                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Act of God                  | A direct, violent, sudden, irresistible act of nature that could not be reasonably foreseen or resisted                                          |
| Adjournment Motion          | Motion to adjourn House proceedings to discuss an urgent matter of public importance; needs 50 members' support                                  |
| Admonition                  | A judicial or ecclesiastic censure or reprimand                                                                                                  |
| Affirmation                 | A solemn declaration without oath                                                                                                                |
| Anglo-Indian                | Of British birth but living/having lived long in India                                                                                           |
| Appropriation Bill          | Bill devoting/reserving money for a specific/distinct purpose                                                                                    |
| Backward Classes            | OBC list prepared by Central Govt, revised every 10 years                                                                                        |
| Beggar                      | Labour/service exacted by court or a person in power, without remuneration                                                                       |
| Bill                        | A draft of a proposed law placed before a lawmaking body                                                                                         |
| Breach of Privilege         | Disregard of a Parliament member's/House's privileges, rights, immunities                                                                        |
| Bulletin                    | Official notice of a public transaction or matter of public importance                                                                           |
| Censure Motion              | Motion against govt/a minister censuring policy in some direction                                                                                |
| Chief Whip                  | Head of the whips of different political parties in Parliament                                                                                   |
| Closure                     | Parliamentary procedure by which debate is closed and the matter put to immediate vote                                                           |
| Coalition                   | Alliance in a multi-party system where no single party commands a working majority                                                               |
| Concurrent List             | List of subjects on which both federal and state legislatures can make laws; federal law prevails in conflict                                    |
| Constituent Assembly        | Legislative body charged with framing/revising a Constitution                                                                                    |
| Contempt of Court           | Disobedience/disregard of a court's rules, orders, or dignity, punishable by committal                                                           |
| Delegated Legislation       | Rules/regulations made by Executive under power given by Parliament                                                                              |
| Doctrine of Severability    | Only the offending part of a statute (not the whole) is void if it conflicts with a constitutional limitation                                    |
| Double Jeopardy             | Subjecting an accused to repeated trial for the same offence (prohibited)                                                                        |
| Due Process of Law          | Concept (from US Constitution) that judiciary can strike down a law if the procedure is unfair, even if the law is within legislative competence |
| Electoral College           | An intermediary body chosen by electors to elect representatives (indirect election)                                                             |
| Electoral Roll              | The voters' list — names of all eligible voters                                                                                                  |
| Equal Protection            | All individuals/classes equally subject to ordinary law administered by courts                                                                   |
| Expulsion                   | Unseating a member for offences/grave misdemeanours against the House                                                                            |
| Gazette                     | Official newspaper of the government                                                                                                             |
| Hung Parliament             | A Parliament where no party has won a working majority                                                                                           |
| Judicial Review             | Power of courts to review statutes/administrative acts and determine constitutionality                                                           |
| Legislature                 | Body authorised to make, alter, repeal laws — may have 1 or 2 Houses                                                                             |
| Locus Standi                | Right to be heard; place for standing                                                                                                            |
| Martial Law                 | Law that is arbitrary in its decisions, not built on settled principles                                                                          |
| Minority                    | A racial, religious, or political group smaller than and differing from a larger controlling group                                               |
| Motion                      | A proposal made in a legislature to elicit its decision on a subject                                                                             |
| Office of Profit            | Employment with fees/emoluments attached, making it disqualifying for certain offices                                                            |
| Petition                    | A solemn, earnest request to a superior authority                                                                                                |
| Pith and Substance          | Doctrine (from Privy Council) for interpreting statutes to resolve conflicts between competing legislatures                                      |
| Preamble                    | Introduction to a constitutional statute, stating its reason and purpose                                                                         |
| Proportional Representation | Method securing candidate election in proportion to each political opinion's numerical strength                                                  |
| Question Hour               | Time fixed for asking/answering oral questions in a legislature sitting                                                                          |
| Quorum                      | Minimum number of members required to be present to transact business                                                                            |
| Res Judicata                | A final judgement already decided between the same parties cannot be raised again                                                                |
| Rule of Law                 | Supremacy of regular law over arbitrary power; equal subjection of all classes to ordinary courts                                                |
| Shadow Cabinet              | Body of opposition leaders ready to take office                                                                                                  |
| State                       | Comprises people, territory, government, and sovereignty with authority to enforce final legal decisions                                         |
| Subordinate Legislation     | Statutory instruments/orders made by a body subordinate to the legislature                                                                       |
| Untouchability              | A social disability historically imposed on certain classes by birth in certain castes                                                           |
| Vote on Account             | Advance payment to govt departments to function till Appropriation Act is passed                                                                 |
| Vote of Credit              | Lok Sabha grants funds for expenditure whose amount/details cannot be precisely stated                                                           |
| Walk Out                    | Leaving a meeting/organisation as an expression of disapproval                                                                                   |
| Zero Hour                   | Noisy interregnum between Question Hour and rest of the day's business (12–1 pm)                                                                 |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- "Due Process of Law" (US concept, fairness of the law itself) vs "Procedure Established by Law" (original Indian concept, only fairness of PROCEDURE) — Maneka Gandhi case effectively moved India closer to "due process" in practice. See [[18-Important-Cases]].
- Adjournment Motion needs 50 members; No-Confidence Motion also needs 50 members to be admitted — same number, different purposes, easy to conflate.
