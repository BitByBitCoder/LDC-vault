## 73rd Amendment Act, 1992 (Panchayats)

| Question                                                                           | Answer                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What status did the 73rd Amendment give Panchayati Raj institutions?               | Constitutional status                                                               |
| Which new Part and Schedule did it add?                                            | Part IX and 11th Schedule                                                           |
| What is Part IX entitled, and what Article range does it cover?                    | "The Panchayats", Articles 243 to 243(O)                                            |
| How many functional items of Panchayats does the 11th Schedule contain (Art 243G)? | 29                                                                                  |
| This Panchayati Raj system applies to states with what minimum population?         | More than 20 lakhs                                                                  |
| Tenure of every Panchayat?                                                         | 5 years                                                                             |
| Within what time must new Panchayat elections be held after dissolution?           | Before expiry of tenure, or within 6 months of dissolution                          |
| Is reservation for SC/ST/women mandatory in Panchayats?                            | Yes — seats reserved for SCs/STs (proportionate, Art 243D), 1/3rd for women overall |
| Who conducts/supervises Panchayat elections?                                       | The State Election Commission                                                       |
| Who appoints the State Election Commissioner, and how can he be removed?           | Governor appoints; removed same manner as a High Court Judge                        |
| Who is qualified to be a Panchayat member, under which Article?                    | Persons above 21 years, qualified to be State Legislature members, Article 243F     |
| How often must States appoint a Finance Commission for Panchayats?                 | Every 5 years (after the 73rd Amendment, from 25 April 1993)                        |
| Under which Article is the State Election Commission constituted?                  | Article 243K                                                                        |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- 73rd Amendment = Panchayats (rural), Part IX, 11th Schedule, 29 subjects.
- 74th Amendment = Municipalities (urban), Part IX-A, 12th Schedule, 18 subjects.
- Both amendments took effect 1992 but implementation effectively from 1993 — a common date-mixing trap.
- G.V.K. Rao & Ashok Mehta committee recommendations were NOT implemented — only Balwant Rai Mehta's three-tier system took root historically, later constitutionalised by the 73rd Amendment.
