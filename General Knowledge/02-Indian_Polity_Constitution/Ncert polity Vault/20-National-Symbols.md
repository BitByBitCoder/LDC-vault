# National Symbols

| Symbol                  | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| National Flag           | Horizontal tricolour — deep saffron (top), white (middle), dark green (bottom); ratio of width:length = 2:3; navy-blue Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes) in centre of white band; adopted by Constituent Assembly 22 July 1947                    |
| Flag court ruling       | January 2004: Supreme Court (CJI B.N. Khare) held unfurling/hoisting of National Flag is a fundamental right under Article 19(1)(a)                                                                                                       |
| State Emblem            | Adaptation of the Sarnath Lion Capital of Ashoka; adopted 26 January 1950; only 3 lions visible (4th hidden); wheel in relief with bull (right) & horse (left); "Satyameva Jayate" (from Mundaka Upanishad) inscribed in Devanagari below |
| National Anthem         | "Jana-gana-mana", composed by Rabindranath Tagore (originally in Bengali); adopted 24 January 1950; first sung 27 Dec 1911 at Kolkata Session of INC; music composed by Captain Ram Singh Thakur (INA sepoy); playing time ~52 seconds    |
| National Song           | "Vande Mataram", composed in Sanskrit by Bankim Chandra Chatterji; equal status with National Anthem; first sung at 1896 INC session; published in novel 'Anandmath'; adopted as National Song 26 January 1950                            |
| National Calendar       | Based on Saka Era, Chaitra as first month, normal year 365 days; adopted 22 March 1957, alongside Gregorian calendar                                                                                                                      |
| National Animal         | Tiger (Panthera tigris)                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| National Bird           | Indian Peacock (Pavo cristatus)                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| National Flower         | Lotus (Nelumbo nucifera)                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| National Tree           | Banyan Tree (Ficus benghalensis)                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| National Fruit          | Mango (Mangifera indica)                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| National Aquatic Animal | Ganges River Dolphin (Platanista gangetica)                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Q&A

| Question                                                                                | Answer                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Who published "Bharat Bhagya Vidhata" in 1912, and what was it later titled in English? | Rabindranath Tagore, in 'Tatvabodhini'; English title "Morning Song of India" (1919) |
| Which title/tune-composer credit goes to an INA sepoy?                                  | Captain Ram Singh Thakur — composed the present tune of the National Anthem          |
| National Song vs National Anthem — same status?                                         | Yes, "Vande Mataram" has equal status with "Jana-gana-mana"                          |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- National Anthem (Jana-gana-mana) = Bengali origin, by Tagore, adopted 24 Jan 1950. National Song (Vande Mataram) = Sanskrit, by Bankim Chandra Chatterji, adopted 26 Jan 1950 — different dates, different languages, different authors — a classic confusion pair.
- Flag ratio = 2:3 (width:length), Chakra has 24 spokes — both individually testable numbers.
