# The State Legislature

| Question                                                                                     | Answer                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which 6 states have a Bicameral (double house) legislature per this source?                  | Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Jammu & Kashmir, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh                                                                           |
| Under which Article can Legislative Council be created/abolished, what majority needed?      | Article 169; Assembly resolution needs majority of total membership + 2/3rds present & voting; then simple majority in Parliament                       |
| Legislative Council size limits?                                                             | Not more than 1/3rd of Assembly's membership, not less than 40                                                                                          |
| How is Legislative Council membership divided (electorally)?                                 | 5/6th indirectly elected, 1/6th nominated by Governor                                                                                                   |
| Breakdown of the 5/6th elected portion?                                                      | 1/3rd from local bodies; 1/12th from graduates (3 yrs standing); 1/12th from teachers (3 yrs standing); 1/3rd from MLAs (electing non-Assembly-members) |
| What does the nominated 1/6th represent?                                                     | Persons with special knowledge/experience of literature, science, art, co-operative movement, social service                                            |
| Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) size limits?                                             | Not more than 500, not less than 60 members                                                                                                             |
| Which states/UTs have fewer than 60 Assembly members?                                        | Sikkim, Goa, Mizoram, Puducherry                                                                                                                        |
| Which Amendment discontinued Anglo-Indian reserved seats in Parliament & State Legislatures? | 126th Constitutional Amendment Bill 2019, enacted as 104th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019                                                           |
| Duration of the Legislative Assembly?                                                        | 5 years, can be dissolved sooner by Governor                                                                                                            |
| By how much/how can the 5-year term be extended?                                             | By Parliament during Emergency, max 1 year at a time                                                                                                    |
| Is the Legislative Council dissolved?                                                        | No — permanent body; 1/3rd retire every second year                                                                                                     |
| Presiding officers of Assembly / Council respectively?                                       | Speaker & Deputy Speaker (Assembly, Art 178) / Chairman & Deputy Chairman (Council, Art 182)                                                            |
| Under which Article are State Legislature membership qualifications listed?                  | Article 173                                                                                                                                             |
| Minimum age for Legislative Council / Assembly membership?                                   | 30 years (Council) / 25 years (Assembly)                                                                                                                |
| Grounds for vacation of seat?                                                                | Double membership; disqualification; resignation; absence >60 days without permission                                                                   |

## Speaker of Assembly

| Question                                                              | Answer                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Who elects the Speaker of the Assembly?                               | The Assembly itself, from amongst its members |
| Notice period for a removal resolution?                               | 14 days' advance notice (Article 179)         |
| Can Speaker vote in case of a tie?                                    | Yes, casting vote                             |
| Whose decision is final on Money Bill status?                         | Speaker's                                     |
| Who decides disqualification questions for defection (10th Schedule)? | The Speaker                                   |

## Chairman of Legislative Council

| Question                              | Answer                                |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Who elects the Chairman?              | The Council, from amongst its members |
| Notice period to remove the Chairman? | 14 days' advance notice               |

## Legislative Procedure (State)

| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Where can a Money Bill be introduced?                              | Only the Legislative Assembly |
| Max period Legislative Council can hold an Ordinary Bill?          | 3 months                      |
| Is there provision for joint sitting between Assembly and Council? | No                            |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- Number of bicameral states shifts over time (source lists 6: AP, Bihar, J&K, Karnataka, Maharashtra, UP) — verify current count as J&K's status changed post-2019 (now a UT, no longer has this legislature structure the same way).
