## Money Bills & Financial Bills

| Question                                                                              | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can a Money Bill be introduced in Rajya Sabha?                                        | No — only in Lok Sabha                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Whose certificate accompanies a Money Bill sent to Rajya Sabha?                       | Speaker's certificate                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Can Rajya Sabha reject or amend a Money Bill?                                         | No — can only recommend, within 14 days                                                                                                                                                   |

## Parliamentary Terms

| Question                                                         | Answer                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is a Starred Question?                                      | Requires an oral answer on the floor of the House                                                                                                                                  |
| What is an Unstarred Question?                                   | Minister lays a written answer on the table                                                                                                                                        |
| What is a Short Notice Question?                                 | Asked on urgent matters of public importance                                                                                                                                       |
| Quorum for either House, under which Article?                    | 1/10th of total members, Article 100(c)                                                                                                                                            |
| Three types of cut motions?                                      | Disapproval of policy cut (reduce by ₹1), Economy cut (reduce by specific amount), Token cut (reduce by ₹100)                                                                      |
| What is an Adjournment Motion, how many members must support it? | Motion to adjourn House for urgent public matter; needs 50 members' support                                                                                                        |
| What is a Calling Attention Motion?                              | Member calls Minister's attention to urgent public matter, with Speaker's permission                                                                                               |
| What is a Privilege Motion?                                      | Moved when a Minister has committed a breach of privilege of the House                                                                                                             |
| What is Vote on Account?                                         | Advance payment enabling govt depts to function till Appropriation Act is passed                                                                                                   |
| What is Zero Hour, what time?                                    | 12–1 pm daily, for miscellaneous business/call-attention/adjournment matters                                                                                                       |
| Difference: Censure Motion vs No-Confidence Motion?              | Censure: against govt/individual minister for policy failure, needs reasons stated; No-confidence: against Council of Ministers only, needs 50 members to admit, no reasons needed |
| Where are Cut Motions moved?                                     | Lok Sabha only                                                                                                                                                                     |
| What is a Whip?                                                  | Directive by a political party to ensure members vote a certain way                                                                                                                |
| What is Guillotine?                                              | Undiscussed demands for grants put to vote directly on last allotted day                                                                                                           |
| Who was the first official Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha?    | Y.B. Chavan (Congress-I), with rank of Cabinet Minister                                                                                                                            |

## Stages of a Bill

| Question                                                                      | Answer                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What constitutes the First Reading of a Bill?                                 | Introduction + publication in the Gazette of India                                                          |
| What are the two stages of the Second Reading?                                | (i) General discussion of principles (ii) Clause-by-clause discussion                                       |
| What happens in Third Reading?                                                | Final formal reading — accept/reject as a whole, no substantial changes                                     |
| What happens if the second House rejects the Bill or sits on it for 6 months? | A deadlock is deemed to have taken place                                                                    |
| What are the President's options after both Houses pass a Bill?               | Assent (becomes Act); withhold assent (Bill ends); return for reconsideration (must assent if passed again) |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- Money Bill: introduced ONLY in Lok Sabha; Rajya Sabha's 14-day window is a RECOMMENDATION power only, never a veto.
- Rajya Sabha's TWO special/exclusive powers (Art 249 State List law-making, Art 312 new All-India Service) both need a 2/3rd majority resolution — easy MCQ pairing.
- Joint sitting held only 3 times (1960 Dowry Prohibition Act, 1977 Banking Service Commission (Repeal) Bill, 2002 POTA) — exam sometimes asks "which bills."
- No-Confidence Motion needs 50 members just to be ADMITTED (not to pass) — commonly confused with the 2/3rd majority needed to pass an impeachment.
