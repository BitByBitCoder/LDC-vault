## Exam Trap ⚠️
- Article 21 vs 21A: 21 = life & personal liberty (all persons); 21A = Right to Education specifically for children 6–14, added later (2002).
- Prohibition vs Certiorari: both against courts/tribunals — Prohibition = STOP (forbids continuing beyond jurisdiction); Certiorari = QUASH (already-made decision undone).
- Right to Property: NOT a Fundamental Right since 1978 (44th Amendment) — it's a legal right under Art 300A. Common trap: papers list it as an FR by mistake.
