## Exam Trap ⚠️
- DPSP vs Fundamental Rights: DPSP non-justiciable, FR justiciable; in conflict FR prevails (established in courts, though 42nd Amendment tried to reverse this — struck down in Minerva Mills).
- Article 43B (co-operative societies) is a LATE addition (2011, 97th Amendment) — don't confuse with the original 1976 batch (39A, 39F, 43A, 48A).
