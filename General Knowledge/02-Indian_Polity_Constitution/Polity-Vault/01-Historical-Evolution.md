## Exam Trap ⚠️
- Regulating Act 1773 vs Pitts India Act 1784: 1773 = parliamentary control + Governor-General role created; 1784 = Board of Control created (dual government begins).
- 1919 Act = Dyarchy in PROVINCES; 1935 Act = Dyarchy abolished in provinces but introduced at CENTRE.
- Charter Act 1833 vs 1853: 1833 = GG of India title created, Law Commission (Macaulay); 1853 = competitive exams + local representation, separate Governor for Bengal.
