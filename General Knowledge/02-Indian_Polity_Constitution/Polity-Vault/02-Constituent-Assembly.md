## Exam Trap ⚠️
- Sachidanand Sinha = FIRST/temporary president; Rajendra Prasad = permanent President — commonly confused.
- Drafting Committee Chairman (Ambedkar) ≠ Constitutional Advisor (B.N. Rao) — different roles.
- Australian Constitution appears twice in source list (Concurrent List + trade/commerce) — some papers list "Constitution of Australia" separately from "Australian Constitution", treat as same source, different feature.
