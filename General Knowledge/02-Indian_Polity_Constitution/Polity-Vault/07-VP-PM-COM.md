# Vice-President, Prime Minister & Union Council of Ministers

## Vice-President

| Question                                                                        | Answer                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How is the Vice-President elected?                                              | Indirectly, by single transferable vote                                                           |
| Do State Legislatures take part in VP's election?                               | No                                                                                                |
| Who forms the Electoral College for VP?                                         | Elected and nominated members of both Houses of Parliament                                        |
| Under which Article are VP qualifications listed?                               | Article 66(3)                                                                                     |
| Minimum age to be VP?                                                           | 35 years                                                                                          |
| Who administers the oath of office to VP, under which Article?                  | President (or person appointed by him), Article 69                                                |
| VP's term of office, under which Article?                                       | 5 years, Article 67                                                                               |
| How can VP be removed?                                                          | Resolution of Rajya Sabha by absolute majority, agreed to by Lok Sabha [Art 67(b)]                |
| VP's primary function/ex-officio role?                                          | Ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha                                                                |
| Can VP be removed for actions as Chairman without being removed as VP?          | No, only removable via removal from VP office                                                     |
| Maximum period VP can act as President (on vacancy)?                            | 6 months                                                                                          |
| Who acts as President if BOTH President and VP offices are vacant?              | Chief Justice of India (or seniormost SC Judge)                                                   |
| When did this (CJI acting as President) happen for the first time?              | 1969 — Chief Justice M. Hidayatullah, when President Zakir Hussain died and VP V.V. Giri resigned |
| Who was elected VP for a second term (1957)?                                    | Dr. S. Radhakrishnan                                                                              |
| Under which Article are disputes on President/VP election decided, and by whom? | Article 71, by the Supreme Court (exclusive & final jurisdiction)                                 |

## Prime Minister

| Question                                                                                      | Answer                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who appoints the Prime Minister, under which Article?                                         | President, Article 75                                                                                    |
| Is PM's term fixed?                                                                           | No — holds office during President's pleasure, but cannot be dismissed while enjoying Lok Sabha majority |
| Who allocates/reshuffles ministerial portfolios?                                              | The Prime Minister                                                                                       |
| What can PM do if a minister disagrees with him?                                              | Ask the minister to resign, or advise the President to dismiss him                                       |
| Under which Article must PM communicate all Council of Ministers' decisions to the President? | Article 78                                                                                               |
| What is the PM's role regarding leadership of party/coalition?                                | Leader of his/her party or coalition, usually Leader of the Lok Sabha                                    |
| Is "Deputy Prime Minister" mentioned in the Constitution?                                     | No                                                                                                       |

## Attorney General of India

| Question                                                 | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Who is the first Law Officer of the Government of India? | Attorney General                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Who appoints the Attorney General, under which Article?  | President, Article 76                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| What qualifications does the AG need?                    | Same as a Judge of the Supreme Court                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Is the AG a member of the Cabinet?                       | No, but has right to speak in Houses of Parliament (no right to vote)                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Notable Attorney Generals with long tenures (table)?     | Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (1947–50), Morarji Desai (1967–69), Charan Singh & Jagjivan Ram (1979), Y.B. Chavan (1979–80), Devi Lal (1989–90, 1990–91), L.K. Advani (2002–04) — Note: this table in source actually lists Deputy PMs, not AGs |

## Union Council of Ministers

| Question                                                                           | Answer                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who appoints Ministers?                                                            | President, on the advice of the Prime Minister                |
| Must Ministers be members of Parliament?                                           | Yes, or become members within 6 months of appointment         |
| Who administers the oath to Ministers?                                             | President                                                     |
| Under which Article is Council of Ministers collectively responsible to Lok Sabha? | Article 75                                                    |
| Are Ministers individually responsible to anyone besides the Legislature?          | Yes, individually responsible to the President                |
| Can a Minister vote in a House he is not a member of?                              | No — can speak in both Houses, but vote only in his own House |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
- PM's term is NOT fixed (unlike President's fixed 5 years) — "pleasure of President" but practically majority-dependent.
- "Deputy Prime Minister" is NOT a constitutional post — commonly asked as a trick T/F question.
- This source's PM/VP/President lists END around 2014–2019 — always cross-check against current officeholders for the actual exam, since Anthropic's most current knowledge should be used for "who is the current X" type questions.
