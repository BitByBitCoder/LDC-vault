## Economy Types

| Type | Key feature | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Capitalist / Market | Private ownership, competition | USA |
| Socialist | State ownership, **no competition** | USSR |
| Mixed | Public + private together | **India** |
| Closed | No trade with world | — |

## Inflation Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Inflation | Prices rising |
| Deflation | Prices falling |
| Stagflation | Stagnation + inflation together |
| Reflation | Reviving prices after deflation |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
> Stagflation = stagnation + **inflation** (NOT deflation, NOT reflation)
> Socialist = no competition. Mixed = both sectors (India)
> Gresham = **bad** drives out **good** money
