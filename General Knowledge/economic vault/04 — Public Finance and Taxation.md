## Revenue vs Capital

| | Revenue | Capital |
|---|---|---|
| Expenditure | Salaries, pensions, subsidies, interest, rent, repairs | Roads, bridges, machinery, loans given |
| Receipts | Tax, dividend, fees | Borrowings, **disinvestment**, grants-loan recovery |

## Direct vs Indirect Tax

| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| Income tax, corporate tax | GST, excise, customs |

## Deficits

| Deficit | Formula |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Revenue exp − revenue receipts |
| **Fiscal** | Total exp − total receipts (excl. borrowing) = **borrowings** |
| Primary | Fiscal deficit − interest payments |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
> Fiscal deficit = **borrowings**. Primary deficit = fiscal − interest
> Income tax = **direct**; GST/excise/customs = indirect
> Salaries, pensions, interest = revenue exp. Machinery, roads = **capital**
> GST passed 2016 (amendment) but **started 2017**
