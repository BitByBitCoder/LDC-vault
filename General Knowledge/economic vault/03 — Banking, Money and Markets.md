## Policy Rates

| Rate | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repo | RBI **lends** to banks (short-term) |
| Reverse repo | RBI **borrows** from banks |
| Bank rate | RBI long-term lending rate |
| CRR / SLR | Reserves banks must keep (cash / liquid assets) |

## Open Market Operations

| RBI action | Liquidity |
|---|---|
| **Sells** securities | ↓ Reduces |
| **Buys** securities | ↑ Increases |

## Exam Trap ⚠️
> Commercial banks **borrow** at **repo**; RBI **borrows** at **reverse repo**
> RBI sells securities = liquidity **falls**
> Repo is the answer to both "RBI lends" and "banks borrow" wording
> Cashless country: options differ by paper — Sweden is the usual answer
