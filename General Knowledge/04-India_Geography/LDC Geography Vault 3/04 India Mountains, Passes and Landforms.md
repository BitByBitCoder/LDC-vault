> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - Anamudi (Anaimudi) = highest in peninsular India. Doddabetta = highest in Nilgiris. Do not swap.
> - Nathu La (Sikkim), Shipki La (Himachal), Zoji La (J&K/Ladakh), Lipulekh (Uttarakhand).
> - Tri-point India-China-Myanmar = Diphu Pass (Arunachal).
> - Barren Island is in the Andaman Sea (Andaman group), not Nicobar.
