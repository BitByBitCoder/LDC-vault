> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Longest river in India** appears twice. Ganga = longest within India. Brahmaputra and Indus are longer overall but flow through other countries.
> - Sambhar = highest salinity (inland salt lake, Rajasthan). Chilika = largest brackish lagoon (Odisha). Dal and Wular = Kashmir freshwater.
> - Palk Strait = India-Sri Lanka. Duncan Passage = South and Little Andaman. Ten Degree Channel = Andaman and Nicobar.
> - West-flowing rivers into the Arabian Sea: Narmada, Tapi.
