> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - Coal rank (best to worst): **Anthracite > Bituminous > Lignite > Peat**.
> - Regur = black cotton soil (Deccan lava, good for cotton). Red soil colour = iron oxide.
> - Digboi = oldest oil field (Assam). Bombay High = largest offshore field.
> - Kharif: cotton, rice, maize. Rabi: wheat, gram, pea, mustard.
> - Rooftop-solar and solar-capacity questions change every year. Re-check before the exam.
