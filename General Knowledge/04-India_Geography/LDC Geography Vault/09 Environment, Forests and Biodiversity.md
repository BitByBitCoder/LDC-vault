> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Greenhouse gases appeared 3 times.** True GHGs: CO2, CH4, N2O, water vapour, ozone, CFCs. **SO2 is not a GHG** (it causes acid rain).
> - Acid rain = SO2 and NOx. Ozone depletion = CFCs.
> - Chipko = tree hugging (Uttarakhand). Silent Valley = Kerala. Narmada Bachao = dam protest.
> - Forest cover (area) = Madhya Pradesh. Forest cover (percentage) = Mizoram and other NE states.
