> [!warning] Exam Trap ⚠️
> - **Atmosphere layers appeared 5 times.** Order from ground: Troposphere (weather) > Stratosphere (ozone) > Mesosphere > Thermosphere (temperature rises, ionosphere inside it) > Exosphere.
> - Temperature falls with height in troposphere and mesosphere. It rises in stratosphere and thermosphere.
> - **Time**: IST = GMT + 5:30. So GMT is 5h30 *behind* IST. Direction is the trap.
> - Equator = the only great circle among latitudes. Tropic of Cancer = 23.5° N. Horse latitudes = 30°. Doldrums = near 0°.
