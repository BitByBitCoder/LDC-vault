# **5. How to study each chapter**

Use this method for every chapter:

1. Read the textbook once to understand the topic.
    
2. Underline important facts, locations, rivers, states and numbers.
    
3. Make short notes of facts that can become one-mark MCQs.
    
4. Solve old-paper questions related to that chapter.
    
5. Revise the facts after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days.
    

**My recommendation:** Finish Priority 1 first. Don't spend several days memorising minor details from geological structure or industries while you haven't completed rivers, physical divisions, agriculture and population.

One important point: this is a priority plan based on the old questions we have located, not a guarantee that other chapters won't appear.
