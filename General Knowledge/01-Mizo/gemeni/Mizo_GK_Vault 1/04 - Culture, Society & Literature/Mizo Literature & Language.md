# Mizo Language & Literature

## ✍️ Language & Script Origins
- Language Family: **Tibeto-Burman family** (Kuki-Chin-Mizo branch).
- Script: **Roman Script** (introduced in **April 1894** by missionaries Rev. F.W. Savidge and Rev. J.H. Lorrain).

### The Mizo Alphabet (A AW B)
The Mizo alphabet consists of 25 letters:
`A, AW, B, CH, D, E, F, G, NG, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, U, V, Z`

*(Note: "AW" and "NG" are treated as distinct single letters in Mizo orthography).*
