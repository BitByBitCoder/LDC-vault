# Social Institutions, Ethos & Customs

## 🤝 The Ethos of *Tlawmngaihna*
The central ethical code of Mizo society is **Tlawmngaihna**.

> [!QUOTE] Definition of Tlawmngaihna
> An unwritten moral code that mandates being **unselfish, hospitable, courageous, helpful to others**, and putting the welfare of the community above individual self-interest without expecting reward.

---

## 🤝 Community Institutions

### 1. Hnatlang
- Traditional system of **voluntary community labor**.
- Villagers unite without pay to build roads, assist grieving families, repair houses, or perform community work.

### 2. Young Mizo Association (YMA)
- Established on **June 15, 1935** (originally as *Young Lushai Association* - YLA; renamed YMA in 1947).
- Largest non-governmental, voluntary social organization in Mizoram.
- Plays a vital role in community service, disaster relief, funeral assistance, and social welfare.

---

## 💍 Marriage & Inheritance Customs
- **Bridewealth (*Ministers / Inhnang / Man*):** Traditional bride price (*Man*) paid by the groom's family to the bride's family.
- **Inheritance Law:** Traditionally followed **ultimogeniture** (the youngest son - *Luta* - inheriting the family estate), though modern legal frameworks and wills have introduced flexibility.
