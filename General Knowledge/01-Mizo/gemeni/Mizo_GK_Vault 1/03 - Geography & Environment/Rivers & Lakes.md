# Rivers and Lakes of Mizoram

Mizoram is drained by two major drainage networks: those flowing **Northward** towards the Barak Basin (Assam) and those flowing **Southward/Westward** towards Bangladesh and Myanmar.

---

## 🌊 Major Rivers

| River Name | Local Name | Flow Direction | Key Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chhimtuipui** | Kaladan | Southward | Longest river in Mizoram (~138.46 km inside Mizoram); originates in Myanmar |
| **Tlawng** | Dhaleswari | Northward | Longest river originating within/flowing through major valleys (~185 km inside state) |
| **Tuirial** | Sonai | Northward | Location of Tuirial Hydroelectric Project (60 MW) |
| **Tuivawl** | Tuivawl | Northward | Major tributary flowing through eastern Aizawl/Saitual |
| **Khawthlangtuipui**| Karnaphuli | Westward | Flows into Bangladesh |

## 💧 Famous Natural & Artificial Lakes

### 1. Palak Dil (Palak Lake)
- **Location:** Siaha District (Mara Autonomous District Council).
- **Status:** **Largest Natural Lake in Mizoram**.
- Designated as a **Ramsar Site** (Wetland of International Importance) in 2021.
- Oval-shaped lake surrounded by tropical evergreen forest.

### 2. Tam Dil (Lake of Mustard)
- **Location:** Near Saitual / Dilkhan village (~110 km from Aizawl).
- Legend says it originated from a giant mustard plant (*Tam*).
- Major tourist attraction and inland fishery spot.

### 3. Rungdil & Rih Dil
- **Rungdil:** Twin lakes in Saitual district surrounded by lush forest.
- **Rih Dil:** Historically considered the most sacred lake in Mizo folklore (where souls pass into *Mithi Khua*), located **just across the international border inside Myanmar** (Chin State), but culturally inseparable from Mizo tradition.
