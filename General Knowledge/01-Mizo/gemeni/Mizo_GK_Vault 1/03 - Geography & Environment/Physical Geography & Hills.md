# Physical Geography & Mountain Ranges

Mizoram is part of the **Patkai-Arakan Yoma hill system**, characterized by north-to-south trending parallel hill ranges with deep v-shaped valleys.

---

### Top Highest Peaks in Mizoram:
1. **Phawngpui (Blue Mountain):** 2,157 meters
   - Located in **Lawngtlai District** (near Myanmar border).
   - Revered as the abode of Mizo folklore spirits.
   - Declared a National Park in 1992.
2. **Lengteng Peak:** 2,141 meters (Champhai District).
3. **Swarang Peak:** ~1,997 meters.
4. **Lurh Peak:** ~1,935 meters (Champhai District).

---

## 🌄 Topographical Features
- **Ridge and Valley Topography:** Gives rise to numerous steep slopes and natural waterfalls.
- **Geological Composition:** Mostly young tertiary molasse rocks consisting of sandstone, siltstone, and shale.
- **Highest Waterfalls:** **Vantawng Falls** (near Thenzawl in Serchhip district), height approx 229 meters (750 ft) — the highest waterfall in Mizoram.
