# Flora, Fauna & Protected Areas

Mizoram forms part of the **Indo-Burma Biodiversity Hotspot**, hosting rare endemic species of plants, orchids, mammals, and birds.

> [!INFO] Forest Cover
> Forest area covers over **85%** of Mizoram's total geographical area.

---

## 🏞️ National Parks of Mizoram

1. **Phawngpui National Park (Blue Mountain NP)**
   - **Established:** 1992 | **Area:** 50 sq. km
   - **Location:** Lawngtlai District
   - **Key Fauna:** Blyth's Tragopan, Mrs. Hume's Pheasant (*Vavu*), Mountain Goat (*Saza*), Clouded Leopard.

2. **Murlen National Park**
   - **Established:** 1991 | **Area:** 100 sq. km
   - **Location:** Champhai District (near Myanmar border)
   - Often referred to as the "Amazon of Mizoram" due to dense canopy cover where sunlight barely touches the ground.

## 🐅 Wildlife Sanctuaries & Tiger Reserve

| Protected Area | Type | District | Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dampa Tiger Reserve** | Tiger Reserve / WLS | Mamit | Largest sanctuary in Mizoram (500 sq. km); declared Tiger Reserve in 1994 |
| **Ngengpui WLS** | Wildlife Sanctuary | Lawngtlai | Rich virgin rainforest, Asian Elephant habitat |
| **Tawi WLS** | Wildlife Sanctuary | Aizawl/Saitual | Central Mizoram protected hill ranges |
| **Lengteng WLS** | Wildlife Sanctuary | Champhai | High peak area with rare bird species |
| **Thorangtlang WLS** | Wildlife Sanctuary | Lunglei | Corridor for elephants migrating from Bangladesh |
| **Pualreng WLS** | Wildlife Sanctuary | Kolasib | Hoolock gibbon and hornbills |

## 🌺 Flora Highlights
- **Orchids:** Over 250 species of orchids recorded (including *Senhri* - Red Vanda).
- **Bamboo Species:** More than 20 species of bamboo, dominant species being *Melocanna baccifera* (*Mautak*).
