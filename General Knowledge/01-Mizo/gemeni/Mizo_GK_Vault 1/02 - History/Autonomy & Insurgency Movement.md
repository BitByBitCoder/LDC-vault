# Autonomy & The Insurgency Movement (1959–1986)

## 🍂 The Mautam Famine (1959)
The catalyst for the Mizo political movement was the catastrophic **Mautam Famine** of 1959–1960.
- Caused by the cyclical flowering of bamboo (*Melocanna baccifera*), leading to a massive surge in rat population.
- The rats devoured crops, leaving the region in extreme famine.
- Dissatisfaction with the Assam State Government's slow relief measures led **Laldenga** to form the **Mautam Front**, which became the **Mizo National Famine Front (MNFF)** in 1960.
- On **October 22, 1961**, MNFF dropped the word "Famine" and transformed into a political party: the **Mizo National Front (MNF)** led by Laldenga, demanding independent statehood/sovereignty for Mizoram.

---

## 💥 The 1966 Uprising & Conflict
- **March 28, 1966:** Operation Jericho launched by MNF, declaring independence from India and attacking government posts in Aizawl, Lunglei, Champhai.
- **March 5–6, 1966:** Indian Air Force aircraft bombed targets in Aizawl to relieve besieged government forces — the only air strike by India against its own territory.
- **Grouping of Villages:** The Indian Army implemented "Operation Security" / "Grouping Villages" (PPA - Protected Progressive Villages) to isolate insurgents from civilians, drastically altering social structures.

---

## 🛣️ Path to Peace & Union Territory
- **1972:** Mizoram was made a **Union Territory** under the North-Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act, 1971.
- **Mizo District Council** upgraded, and political negotiations commenced with the Central Government.
