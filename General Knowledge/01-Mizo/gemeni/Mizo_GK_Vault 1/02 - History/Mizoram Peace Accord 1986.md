# Mizoram Peace Accord 1986 & Statehood

> [!SUCCESS] Landmark Peace Agreement
> The **Mizoram Peace Accord** is widely recognized as one of the most successful peace agreements in modern global history, completely ending 20 years of armed insurgency.

---

## ✍️ Key Details of the Accord

- **Date of Signing:** June 30, 1986
- **Signatories:**
  1. **Laldenga** (Leader of Mizo National Front)
  2. **R.D. Pradhan** (Home Secretary, Government of India)
  3. **Lalkhama** (Chief Secretary, Government of Mizoram)
- **Prime Minister of India at the time:** Rajiv Gandhi

---

## 📜 Key Terms & Outcomes

1. **Surrender of Arms:** MNF agreed to lay down arms, return to constitutional democracy, and end underground insurgency.
2. **Full Statehood:** Mizoram was granted full statehood, becoming the **23rd State of the Union of India** on **February 20, 1987**.
3. **Special Safeguards (Article 371G):**
   - No Act of Parliament regarding Mizo religious/social practices, customary law, or land ownership applies to Mizoram unless approved by the Legislative Assembly.
4. **Interim Government:** Laldenga became the Chief Minister of the interim state government in 1986 with Congress leader Lalthanhawla stepping down to serve as Deputy Chief Minister.

---

## 🗓️ Important Commemorative Days
- **June 30:** Celebrated annually in Mizoram as **Remna Ni** (Peace Day).
- **February 20:** Celebrated as **Statehood Day**.
