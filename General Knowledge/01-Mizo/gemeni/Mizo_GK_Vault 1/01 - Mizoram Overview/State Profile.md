# Mizoram State Profile

Mizoram is a landlocked state in Northeast India, sharing international borders with **Myanmar** and **Bangladesh**, and domestic borders with **Tripura**, **Assam**, and **Manipur**.

> [!INFO] Quick Facts at a Glance
> - **Date of Statehood:** 20th February 1987 (23rd State of India)
> - **Capital:** Aizawl
> - **Official Languages:** Mizo, English
> - **State Motto:** *Tlawmngaihna* (Selflessness / Helping Spirit)
> - **State Emblem Text:** *Truth Alone Triumphs* (Satyameva Jayate)

## 📊 Demographics & Key Statistics (Census Reference)

- **Total Area:** 21,081 sq. km
- **Literacy Rate:** ~91.33% (2nd highest in India as per 2011 Census)
- **Forest Cover:** Over 85% of total geographical area (one of the highest in India)
- **High Court Jurisdiction:** Gauhati High Court (Aizawl Bench)
- **State Legislative Assembly Seats:** 40 seats (Unicameral)
- **Parliamentary Representation:**
  - **Lok Sabha:** 1 seat
  - **Rajya Sabha:** 1 seat
