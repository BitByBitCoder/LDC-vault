# Administrative Districts of Mizoram

Mizoram is currently divided into **11 Administrative Districts**. Originally 3, expanded to 8 in 1998, and 3 new districts (Hnahthial, Khawzawl, Saitual) were officially operationalized in 2019.

> [!TIP] Map & Location Breakdown
> - **Northern Districts:** Kolasib, Aizawl, Mamit
> - **Central Districts:** Serchhip, Champhai, Khawzawl, Saitual
> - **Southern Districts:** Lunglei, Hnahthial, Lawngtlai, Siaha

---

## 📍 District Summary Table

| District | Headquarter | Key Features / Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aizawl** | Aizawl | State Capital, largest population, administrative hub |
| **Lunglei** | Lunglei | 2nd largest town, major southern commercial center |
| **Siaha (Saiha)** | Siaha | Headquarters of **Mara Autonomous District Council (MADC)**; Lake [[Rivers & Lakes#Palak Dil\|Palak Dil]] |
| **Lawngtlai** | Lawngtlai | Headquarters of **Lai (LADC)** & **Chakma (CADC)** Autonomous District Councils |
| **Champhai** | Champhai | "Rice Bowl of Mizoram", major border trade link with Myanmar (Zokhawthar) |
| **Kolasib** | Kolasib | Northern gateway to Mizoram, borders Cachar district of Assam |
| **Serchhip** | Serchhip | Highest literacy district in India (2011 Census), Vantawng Falls nearby |
| **Mamit** | Mamit | Home to [[Flora, Fauna & Protected Areas#Dampa Tiger Reserve\|Dampa Tiger Reserve]], largest district by area |
| **Hnahthial** | Hnahthial | Created in 2019 (carved out from Lunglei) |
| **Khawzawl** | Khawzawl | Created in 2019 (carved out from Champhai) |
| **Saitual** | Saitual | Created in 2019 (carved out from Aizawl/Champhai); [[Rivers & Lakes#Tam Dil\|Tam Dil]] nearby |

---

## 💡 Important Exam Tips
1. **Largest District by Area:** Mamit
2. **Most Populous District:** Aizawl
3. **District with 2 ADCs:** Lawngtlai (LADC and CADC)
4. **District with Highest Literacy Rate:** Serchhip (97.91% in 2011 Census)
