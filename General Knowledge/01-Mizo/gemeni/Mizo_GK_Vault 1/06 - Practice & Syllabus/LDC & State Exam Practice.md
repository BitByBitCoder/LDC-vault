# Mizo GK Practice Questions (State Exams / LDC)

Test your knowledge with these multiple-choice questions frequently asked in Mizoram State exams (MPSC, MSSSB, LDC, UDC).

---

### Question 1
**When did Mizoram become the 23rd State of the Union of India?**
- A) June 30, 1986
- B) February 20, 1987
- C) January 26, 1972
- D) August 15, 1947
> [!SUCCESS]- View Answer
> **Correct Answer:** B) **February 20, 1987**

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following is the highest peak in Mizoram?**
- A) Lengteng
- B) Reiek
- C) Phawngpui
- D) Hmuifang
> [!SUCCESS]- View Answer
> **Correct Answer:** C) **Phawngpui (2,157 meters)**

---

### Question 3
**What is the official State Animal of Mizoram?**
- A) Hoolock Gibbon
- B) Clouded Leopard
- C) Serow (*Saza*)
- D) Mithun
> [!SUCCESS]- View Answer
> **Correct Answer:** C) **Serow (*Saza*)**

### Question 4
**The historic Mizoram Peace Accord was signed in which year?**
- A) 1966
- B) 1972
- C) 1986
- D) 1990
> [!SUCCESS]- View Answer

### Question 5
**Palak Dil (Palak Lake) is located in which administrative district?**

- B) Lunglei

> **Correct Answer:** C) **Siaha District**

### Question 6

- D) Laldenga and Ch. Chhunga

### Question 7
