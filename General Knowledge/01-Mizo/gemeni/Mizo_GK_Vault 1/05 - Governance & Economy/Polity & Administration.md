# Polity & Governance in Mizoram

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Statehood:** 20th February 1987
- **Legislature:** Unicameral (**Mizoram Legislative Assembly** - 40 seats)
- **High Court:** Gauhati High Court (Aizawl Permanent Bench established in 1990)

---

## 👑 Executive Leadership Landmarks

- **First Chief Minister:** Ch. Chhunga (1972–1977, Union Territory era)
- **First Chief Minister of Statehood Era:** Laldenga (1986–1988)
- **Longest Serving Chief Minister:** Pu Zoramthanga / Pu Lalthanhawla
- **First Governor:** S.J. Das (UT era Chief Commissioner) / **H.S. Dubey** (UT Lt. Governor) / **S.N. Kohli** (1st Governor upon statehood in 1987)

---

## 🏛️ Autonomous District Councils (ADCs)
Under the **Sixth Schedule** of the Indian Constitution, Mizoram contains 3 Autonomous District Councils in the southern region:

1. **Mara Autonomous District Council (MADC)**
   - Headquarter: **Siaha**
   - Community: Mara people
2. **Lai Autonomous District Council (LADC)**
   - Headquarter: **Lawngtlai**
   - Community: Lai people
3. **Chakma Autonomous District Council (CADC)**
   - Headquarter: **Kamalanagar (Lawngtlai)**
   - Community: Chakma people
