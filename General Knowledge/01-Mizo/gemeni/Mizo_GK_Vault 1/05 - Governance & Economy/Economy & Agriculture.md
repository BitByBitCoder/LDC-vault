# Economy & Agriculture of Mizoram

## 🌾 Agriculture & *Jhuming*
Agriculture is the primary occupation of over 60% of Mizoram's population.

> [!WARNING] Jhum Cultivation (*Lo/Jhum*)
> Traditional slash-and-burn agriculture (*Jhum*) is widely practiced on hill slopes. Farmers clear land, burn vegetation, and cultivate mixed crops (paddy, maize, vegetables) for 1–2 years before moving to another plot.

### New Land Use Policy (NLUP) & SEDP:
- Government schemes designed to transition farmers from destructive *Jhum* practice towards sustainable settled farming, horticulture, animal husbandry, and handloom crafts.

---

## 🎋 Bamboo Economy & *Mautam*
- Mizoram accounts for a significant portion of India's bamboo reserves.
- **Mautam Famine Cycle:** Occurs every 48–50 years due to gregarious flowering of *Melocanna baccifera* (*Mautak*) bamboo species. Historical years: 1862, 1911, 1959, 2007.
- **Thingtam Famine Cycle:** Occurs when *Bambusa tulda* flowers (approx 18 inside 30-year offsets).

---

## 🧵 Major Industries & Exports
- **Handloom & Handicrafts:** Famous for *Puan* weaving and bamboo craft.
- **Horticulture:** Anthurium flowers, Passion fruit, Dragon fruit, Mizo Chilli (*Bird's Eye Chilli* - GI Tagged), Ginger, and Turmeric.
- **GI Tag Product:** **Mizo Chilli** (*Mizo Vaimim / Mizo Hmarcha*).
