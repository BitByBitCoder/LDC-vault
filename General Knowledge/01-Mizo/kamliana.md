Here are the answers to the historical and general knowledge questions about Mizoram discussed in the video:

*   **When was the Mizoram Board of School Education (MBSE) established?** 1975.
*   **Who was the first Mizo Chief Engineer of the Mizoram PWD?** Er. R. Thangmawia.
*   **What is the oldest church in Mizoram?** The Presbyterian Church.
*   **What was the Christian population percentage in Mizoram in 2014?** Approximately 87%.
*   **When were the Mizo Chiefs (Lalte) abolished?** 1954.
*   **Who was the first Superintendent of the Lushai Hills?** Captain J. Shakespeare.
*   **Who was the first Chief Minister of Mizoram (UT)?** Ch. Chhunga.
*   **Who was the first Chief Minister of Mizoram (State)?** Lalsangzuala.
*   **What was the first political party in Mizoram?** Mizo Union.
*   **When was the Mizo Zirlai Pawl (MZP) founded?** 1935.
*   **When did the first Mautam (bamboo flowering) occur?** 1862.
*   **Who was the first recipient of the Mizo Academy of Letters (MAL) Academy Award?** Pastor Lalsawma.
*   **Who was the first Mizo to receive the Padma Shri Award?** P. Siamliana.
*   **Who was the first Mizo to pass the IAS exam?** Lalhmingthanga.
*   **What was the Kolasib District literacy rate in the 2011 Census?** 93.5%.
*   **Which monthly magazine in Mizoram had the highest circulation?** Kristan Tlangau.
*   **Who was the 'Poet of the Century' (1900-99)?** Rokunga.
*   **Who was the first Mizo officer?** Lalhmingthanga.
*   **Who was the first Mizo to receive the Padma Bhushan?** P.B.L. Chhunga.
*   **Who wrote the 1991 Book of the Year (MAL) 'Zoram Khawvel'?** B. Lalthangliana.
*   **When was artificial grass installed at the AR Ground in Mizoram?** 2014.
*   **How many districts are currently in Mizoram?** 11.
*   **What was the population of Hnahthial District in the 2011 Census?** 21,399.
*   **Into how many districts was Mizoram divided when it became a UT in 1972?** 3 districts.
*   **Which was the smallest district in Mizoram (area-wise) before the creation of the 3 new districts?** Kolasib.
