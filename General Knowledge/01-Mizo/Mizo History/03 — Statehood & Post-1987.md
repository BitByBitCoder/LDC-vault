# Statehood & Post-1987

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers Mizoram's transition from Union Territory to full statehood and major political developments since.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Mizoram became Union Territory — 21 January 1972 (carved out of Assam)
- Mizo Accord signed — 30 June 1986
- Mizoram attained full statehood — 20 February 1987 (53rd Amendment Act, 1986)
- Laldenga became the first Chief Minister of Mizoram (1987)
- Subsequent political history: MNF and Congress alternating power; Zoram People's Movement (ZPM) emergence in recent elections

## Exam Traps
- UT date (1972) vs Statehood date (1987) — frequently swapped in options
- Amendment Act number (53rd) tested directly in some papers
- **Current officeholder questions (Deputy Speaker, Chief Secretary, State Election Commissioner, department Ministers) are the single riskiest category** — the source PDF combines papers from 2023 through May 2026, and several of these show DIFFERENT "current" answers across papers because officeholders changed between exam cycles. Always verify against the most recent notification/news before your exam, never trust an older paper's answer for these.
