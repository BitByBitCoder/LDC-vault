# Print & Socio-Cultural Orgs

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the first Mizo-language publications and the formation of major socio-cultural organizations.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- First Mizo newspaper — **Mizo leh Vai Chanchin Laibu**, 1897 or 1898 (source-conflicted, verify)
- Young Mizo Association (YMA) founded — 1935 (originally as Young Lushai Association)
- YMA remains the largest and most influential civil society organization in Mizoram
- Mizo Academy of Letters and other literary bodies formed later to promote Mizo literature
