# MNF & Insurgency

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the Mautam famine backdrop, formation of the Mizo National Front, the 1966 uprising, and the eventual Mizo Accord.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Mautam famine (bamboo flowering-driven famine) — mid-1959 onward — key grievance behind Mizo National Famine Front (precursor to MNF)
- Mizo National Front formally founded — 1961, led by Laldenga
- MNF declared independence / uprising began — **28 Feb / 1 March 1966** (source-conflicted — verify against your notification)
- Operation Jericho — Indian Air Force response to the uprising (Aizawl bombing, 1966) — notable as rare case of aerial bombing on Indian soil
- Mizoram made a Union Territory — 1972 (as a political response)
- Mizo Accord signed — **30 June 1986**, between Laldenga and Rajiv Gandhi government
- Mizoram became a full state — 20 February 1987, per the Accord
