## Exam Traps
- Confirmed answers (Chawm as bad demon, Mautlawn zawn as paddy measure) — memorize directly
- Many terms are near-synonyms distinguished only by exact Mizo vocabulary — no logical shortcut exists for most of these, they require direct memorization from a Mizo-culture glossary
- "Not the name of..." questions (stars, organisms, traps, baskets) test recognition of the ODD ONE OUT — practice eliminating options whose meaning you DO know rather than needing to know all four
