# British Contact & Colonial Period

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the Lushai Expeditions, annexation of the Lushai Hills, and early British administrative setup.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Lushai Expedition (1871–72) — punitive expedition following raids into British territory
- Chin-Lushai Expedition (1889–90) — led to fuller British control
- Lushai Hills formally brought under British administration — 1890s
- District split into North Lushai Hills and South Lushai Hills, later unified (1898) under a single Superintendent
- Administered initially under Assam, later reorganized
