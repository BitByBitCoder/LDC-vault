# Christianity & Missionaries

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the arrival of Christian missionaries, script development, and the spread of Christianity in the Lushai Hills.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- First missionaries to arrive — J.H. Lorrain ("Pu Buanga") and F.W. Savidge ("Pu Zauva") — 11 January 1894
- Introduced the Mizo script using Roman alphabet (based on Hunter's system)
- Welsh Presbyterian Mission and Baptist Missionary Society both active in different regions (North/South Lushai Hills split)
- Mizoram Presbyterian Church and Baptist Church of Mizoram trace roots to this period
- Translation of the Bible into Mizo language — major literacy driver
