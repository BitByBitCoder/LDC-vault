# Chieftainship

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the traditional Lal (chief) system, its powers, and its eventual abolition.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Traditional Mizo society organized around village chiefs (Lal) with hereditary authority
- Chiefs held judicial, administrative, and land-allocation powers over their villages
- Circle administration introduced by the British, working alongside chiefs
- Chieftainship abolished via the Assam-Lushai District (Acquisition of Chief's Rights) Act — 1954
- Abolition driven largely by Mizo Union's push for democratic village councils
