## Exam Traps
- Confirmed answers (Zawlpala & Tualvungi, Rev. Liangkhaia, S.T. Rualyapa = Politics) — memorize directly
- Folktale character-matching questions draw from a shared pool of ~8-10 names (Lalruanga, Chhura, Kungawrhi, Sichangneihi, etc.) — build one master character/story table instead of learning each question in isolation
- The April and November month-name questions share two overlapping options (Pawltlak thla, Tomir thla) — this is a strong signal you need the FULL Mizo calendar, not just two entries, since the exam clearly recycles the same option pool across different month questions
