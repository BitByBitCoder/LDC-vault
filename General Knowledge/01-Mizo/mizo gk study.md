### High-Priority Topics (Must-Study First)

These topics carry the highest weightage in Mizoram LDC (MSSSB/MPSC) General Knowledge paper. Focus maximum preparation time here:

  

**Mizoram Section (Then 1-Na)**

  

- **Mizoram Polity & History:** Political History (Topic 22), Chief Minister & Speaker (23), Lt. Governor & Governor (25), and Historical conflicts/battles (28).
     
      
    
- **Mizo Culture & Heritage:** Traditional customs (26), Traditional attire (29), Marriage & divorce rules (30, 35), Religious rites/sacrifices (_Serh leh Sang_) (40, 56).
    
      
    
- **Mizo Literature & Language:** Idioms/Phrases (_Mizo Ţawng Upa_) (57), Books & Authors (54), Early newspapers (52), and Literature history (51).
    
      
    
- **Mizo Firsts & Honors:** Notable pioneers (_Mi Hmasa Ber te_) (49), Padma Award recipients (53), and prominent early figures (48).
    
      
    
- **Geography & Demographics:** Demographics/Census (_Mizoram Chhiarpui_) (6), Rivers, mountains, and lakes (7), and Key historical dates (1).
    
      
    

**India Section (Then 2-Na)**

  

- **Indian Polity & Governance:** Constitution of India (Topic 27), Union Government structure (31), Prime Minister & President (24), Election Commission (22), and 2024 Lok Sabha Elections (2).
    
      
    
- **National Demographics & States:** Census 2011 (6), States, Capitals, Governors & Chief Ministers (8, 10, 11).
    
      
    
- **Planning & Economy:** NITI Aayog and Five-Year Plans (13), Agricultural Revolutions (12).
    
      
    
- **National Firsts & Awards:** First in India (17), First Women in India (19), National Honors/Awards (37).

### Suggested 3-Step Study Plan

1. **Phase 1 (Core Focus):** Master Indian Constitution (India #27), Mizoram Political History (Mizoram #22), Mizo Customs/Culture (Mizoram #26, #30, #56), and Mizo Ţawng Upa (Mizoram #57).
    
      
    
2. **Phase 2 (Facts & Data):** Memorize Census data (Mizoram #6, India #6), Firsts in Mizoram/India (Mizoram #49, India #17, #19), and Constitutional/Political Heads.
    
      
    
3. **Phase 3 (Revision):** Solve previous years' MCQs on Mizo Literature (Mizoram #51, #54) and Indian Economy/NITI Aayog (India #13).
