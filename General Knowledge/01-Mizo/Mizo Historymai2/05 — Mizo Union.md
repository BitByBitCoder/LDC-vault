# Mizo Union

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the formation and role of the Mizo Union, the first major political party in the Lushai Hills.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Mizo Union founded — 9 April 1946
- First political party formed in the Lushai Hills; pushed for abolition of chieftainship and democratic reforms
- Played a key role in post-independence integration debates (whether to join Assam/India)
- Rivalled later by the United Mizo Freedom Organisation (UMFO), which favored different political alignment
- Chieftainship formally abolished via Assam-Lushai District (Acquisition of Chief's Rights) Act — 1954, a Mizo Union-driven reform
