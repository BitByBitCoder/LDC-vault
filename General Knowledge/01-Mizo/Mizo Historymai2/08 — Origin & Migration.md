# Origin & Migration

Links: [[00 — Mizo History Index]]

## Overview
Covers the legendary origin story and the historical migration route of the Mizo people.

## Key Facts / Timeline
- Chhinlung legend — origin myth of Mizo people emerging from a large cave/rock (Chhinlung / Sinlung)
- Believed ancestral migration route: China → Shan State (Myanmar) → Kabaw Valley → Chin Hills → present-day Mizoram
- Migration into the Lushai Hills occurred in successive waves over centuries, generally grouped into old and new Kuki/Mizo migrations
- Different clans (Lusei, Hmar, Paite, Ralte, etc.) associated with different migration waves and settlement areas
