# **5. How to study each chapter for LDC**

For every chapter, use this four-step method:

1. **Understand:** Read the chapter to understand the events.
    
2. **Extract:** Write down names, dates, places, founders and important achievements.
    
3. **Practise:** Solve MCQs from that chapter.
    
4. **Revise:** Revise the short notes after 1, 3 and 7 days.
    

One important thing: your textbook covers Indian history, but your old papers also contain Mizoram-specific history questions, such as the Mizoram Buai, Lushai Hills and Mizo District. Study those separately; don't assume this textbook covers them.

**My strongest recommendation:** Finish chapters 23–35 first, especially 23, 24, 25, 27, 29, 30, 31, 33 and 35. These give you the best match with the history questions already found in your LDC papers.
