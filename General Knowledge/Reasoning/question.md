Based on the question patterns in these papers, I recommend this order:

|Priority|Topic|What to master|
|---|---|---|
|1|Number Series|Missing numbers, patterns|
|2|Analogy|Word and letter relationships|
|3|Classification|Odd one out|
|4|Coding-Decoding|Letter shifting, number coding|
|5|Blood Relations|Family trees|
|6|Direction & Distance|Turns, shortest distance|
|7|Ranking|Position from left, right, top and bottom|
|8|Seating Arrangement|Linear and circular|
|9|Syllogism|Statements and conclusions|
|10|Alphabet Series|Missing letters|
|11|Clock & Calendar|Angles and days|
|12|Non-Verbal Reasoning|Mirror images, figure series, matrices|
|13|Mathematical Operations|Symbol substitution|
|14|Logical Reasoning|Assumptions and courses of action|

**Important observation:** Your papers repeatedly test series, classification, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction, ranking, seating arrangements and figure-based reasoning. These deserve particular attention.
