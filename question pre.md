Here are the **exact types** you should master:

### 1. The 80% that keeps coming — Fixed Verb/Adjective + Preposition
This is where most students lose marks. LDC loves these:

**Study these 15 by heart:**
- **famous FOR** - Mumbai is famous *for* textiles
- **teeming WITH** - teeming *with* wealth
- **partial TO** - partial *to* friends
- **acquainted WITH** - acquainted *with* each other
- **agreed TO** (a proposal) / **agreed WITH** (a person)
- **superior TO** - NOT than. This is always asked. superior to, junior to, inferior to, senior to
- **detrimental TO** - detrimental *to* progress
- **inimical TO** - inimical *to* progress
- **cautioned AGAINST** - cautioned *against* pickpockets
- **ward OFF** - ward *off* evils
- **informed OF/ABOUT** - informed me *of/about* the affair
- **pour INTO** - pour the milk *into* the cup[movement]
- **accommodate? No - but for preposition:** origined **WITH** - originated *with* the PM

> **Trick for Mizoram exam:** If adjective ends with -ior (superior, inferior, senior, junior, prior) -> always **TO**, never than.

### 2. Time Prepositions — Guaranteed 2 questions
From your paper:
- **ON** = day/date: *on* Sunday, *on* 15th August
- **IN** = month/year/season: *in* February, *in* 2023, *in* haste, *in* Champhai [big place]
- **AT** = exact time/point: *at* sunset? No, exam says *by* sunset. But *at* Champhai vs *in* Champhai — LDC asks this. Rule: *at* = small place / point, *in* = big city/state.[deadline]

**Study list:** by sunset [before deadline], on Sunday, in February, at Peter's Street [specific address].

### 3. Place & Position — ON, OVER, ABOVE, ACROSS
They asked:
- kite *on/upon* the tree-top -> **on** [touching surface]
- sword hanging *over* my head -> **over** [directly above, danger]
- bridge *across* the river -> **across** [from one side to other]
- walked *to* the end -> **to**[destination]

Remember:
- **above** = higher than but not directly over
- **over** = directly above + covering/threat
- **on** = touching
- **across** = crossing

### 4. Phrasal Prepositions — 1 mark every year
- ward **off**, settle **among** yourselves, work done **in** haste, live **at/in** — these are not grammar, they are vocabulary.
